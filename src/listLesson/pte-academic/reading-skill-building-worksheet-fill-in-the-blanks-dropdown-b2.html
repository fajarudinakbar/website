Step 1 Contextual Warm-up: The "Meaning vs. Grammar" Trap

Often, two words in the dropdown have the exact same meaning (e.g., "affect" and "effect").

Predict: If two options mean the same thing, what must you look at in the sentence to choose the correct one?

The Rule: You must look at the Grammar and Parts of Speech. For example, "affect" is usually a verb, while "effect" is usually a noun. Also, look at the prepositions immediately before or after the blank (e.g., depends on vs. correlates with).

Step 3 Strategy Spotlight: The Preposition Check

Look at this sentence fragment: "The new regulations will ________ to a decrease in pollution."
Action: Which word fits grammatically: cause, lead, or result? Why?

Strategy: The correct word is lead. Why? Because of the preposition "to" immediately after the blank. We say "lead to". We say "cause" (no preposition) and "result in". Checking the surrounding words filters out wrong options instantly.

Step 8 Re-order Paragraph Drill

PTE Reading also tests cohesion. Put these sentences in logical order based on pronoun references and transition words.

A. This revolutionary invention drastically reduced the time needed to travel across the country.

B. The steam engine was arguably one of the most important inventions of the Industrial Revolution.

C. Consequently, national trade expanded rapidly during the late 19th century.

D. Before its invention, goods were transported primarily by slow horse-drawn wagons.

Correct Sequence: B - D - A - C
1. (B) Independent Topic Sentence introduces "steam engine".
2. (D) "its invention" refers back to the steam engine; gives historical context.
3. (A) "This revolutionary invention" refers to the steam engine replacing wagons; shows the impact (reduced time).
4. (C) "Consequently" shows the final result of the reduced travel time.
