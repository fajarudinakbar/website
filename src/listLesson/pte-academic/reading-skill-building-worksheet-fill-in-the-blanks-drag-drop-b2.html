Step 3 Strategy-in-Motion: The "Grammar Gap"

Determine the grammatical class needed for this blank: "The government decided to ____ the new policy immediately."

Strategy: You need a Base Verb (e.g., implement, enforce). The preposition "to" before the blank indicates an infinitive verb phrase. You can instantly eliminate any nouns or adjectives in the Drag & Drop options box.

Step 8 Re-order Logic Drill

Sequence these paragraphs to form a cohesive text about agricultural policy.

A. These subsidies, however, were criticized for favoring large corporations.

B. In 2010, the government launched a national farming initiative.

C. As a result, smallholder farmers struggled to compete in the market.

D. The primary goal was to provide financial subsidies to rural producers.

Correct Order: B - D - A - C
Logic: 'B' is the independent topic sentence. 'D' expands on the "initiative" mentioned in B. 'A' contrasts the "subsidies" mentioned in D. 'C' is the outcome ("As a result") of the criticism in A.

Step 9 Scenario Application

Draft a 1-sentence email to a university admissions office asking to defer your enrollment. You must use the words: circumstances, request, and subsequent.

Sample Output: "Due to unforeseen circumstances, I would like to formally request a deferral of my enrollment to the subsequent academic semester."
