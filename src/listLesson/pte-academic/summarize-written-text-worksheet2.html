C) Reading Passage

To succeed in the "Summarize Written Text" task, you must write a single sentence. This means you cannot simply write several simple sentences. Instead, you need to connect your ideas grammatically. One way is to create a compound sentence, where you join two main ideas with a conjunction like 'and' or 'but'. For example: "Just-in-time production synchronizes goods with demand, and many companies use this method to control costs". Another, more sophisticated, way is to create a complex sentence. This involves using a relative clause, often introduced by 'which', to add supporting information to the main point. For instance: "Legal safeguards lead to investments, which have a high impact on living standards". Mastering these structures is essential for creating a high-scoring, one-sentence summary that includes both a main idea and its key details.

G) Scenario-Based Task: Synthesize Ideas

You have identified the following key points from a text about electric vehicles (EVs). Combine them into a single, grammatically correct complex or compound sentence that is under 75 words.

  • EV adoption is increasing.
  • The increase is driven by environmental concerns.
  • The increase is also driven by government incentives.
  • High purchase prices remain a major barrier.
  • Lack of charging infrastructure is also a barrier.

Sample Answer: Although the adoption of electric vehicles is increasing, driven by environmental concerns and government incentives, major barriers such as high purchase prices and a lack of charging infrastructure remain.
