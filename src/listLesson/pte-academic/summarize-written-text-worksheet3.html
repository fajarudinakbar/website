C) Reading Passage

A high-quality summary demonstrates your understanding of a text, and this requires paraphrasing, not copying. You should not simply copy the sentences from the text and simply add 'and' between the ideas. Instead, you must synthesize the main points and express them using your own vocabulary and sentence structure. After writing, you must check that you have followed all the rules. The most important rules are about form. Your response must be only one sentence, beginning with a capital letter and ending with a single full stop. Additionally, you must check the word count. Your response needs to be between 5 and 75 words. If it is too short or too long, it will not be scored. Always leave a moment to check your grammar, spelling, and punctuation once you have finished writing the sentences.

G) Final Task: Paraphrase a Summary

Read the short text below. Then, improve the "bad summary" by paraphrasing it into a good, one-sentence summary that meets all task requirements (one sentence, 5-75 words, correct punctuation).

Text: Digital literacy is no longer a luxury but a fundamental skill for navigating the modern world. It encompasses the ability to find, evaluate, and create information using digital platforms. In education, schools are integrating digital literacy into curricula to prepare students for future careers. However, a significant digital divide persists, meaning that people in lower-income areas often lack the access and training needed to develop these essential skills, putting them at a disadvantage.

Bad Summary (Copies words): Digital literacy is a fundamental skill and it is the ability to find, evaluate and create information and schools are integrating it but a digital divide persists and people lack access and training.
