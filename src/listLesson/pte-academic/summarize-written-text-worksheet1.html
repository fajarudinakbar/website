C) Reading Passage

The phenomenon of urban heat islands is a growing concern for cities worldwide. An urban heat island occurs when a metropolitan area is significantly warmer than its surrounding rural areas. The primary cause of this is the modification of land surfaces. Materials like asphalt and concrete, which are common in cities, absorb and retain more of the sun's heat than natural landscapes like forests and bodies of water. A secondary cause is waste heat generated by energy usage from buildings, industry, and vehicles. This effect is not just about comfort; it has significant consequences, including increased energy consumption for cooling, elevated levels of air pollutants, and heat-related health issues for residents.

D) Comprehension: True or False?

1. Urban heat islands are cooler than the areas around them.

Correct! The text states they are "significantly warmer."

2. Natural landscapes are a primary cause of urban heat islands.

Correct! The text states materials like asphalt and concrete, which replace natural landscapes, are the cause.

3. Waste heat from cars and buildings contributes to the problem.

Correct! The text mentions "waste heat generated by energy usage from... vehicles."

4. The only consequence of urban heat islands is increased energy use.

Correct! The text lists three consequences: increased energy consumption, elevated air pollutants, and health issues.

G) Scenario-Based Task

Read the text below. Your task is NOT to write a summary. Instead, identify and list the single main idea and the three most important supporting points. This is the first step before writing.

Employee wellness programs are becoming a staple in modern corporations, moving beyond simple gym memberships to encompass a holistic approach to staff well-being. These programs now often include mental health support, financial planning workshops, and stress management resources. The primary motivation for companies is the understanding that a healthy workforce is a productive one. Research indicates that companies with comprehensive wellness programs see a reduction in absenteeism and lower staff turnover. Furthermore, these initiatives can lead to improved employee morale and a stronger company culture, making the organization a more attractive place to work.

Main Idea: Modern corporate wellness programs are becoming holistic and are motivated by the fact that a healthy workforce is more productive and beneficial for the company.

Supporting Point 1: The scope of these programs has expanded to include mental, financial, and stress management support.

Supporting Point 2: A key benefit for companies is a reduction in employee absenteeism and turnover.

Supporting Point 3: These programs also improve company morale and culture.
