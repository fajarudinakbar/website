Starting a Conversation

Topic: Community Events, Small Talk, and Social Skills

Instructions: Complete the Preparation tasks first. Then read the script (or listen if audio is available) and complete the comprehension tasks.

Task 1: Warm-Up Discussion

How do you feel before meeting new people? Where do people meet socially? What makes it comfortable?

Sample Ideas: I often feel nervous or shy. People meet at parks, cafes, or community events. A conversation feels comfortable when the other person smiles and asks questions.

Transcript

Note: This text represents the conversation. Read it aloud with a partner or read silently.

Nina: Hi, is this seat free?

Arif: Yes, it is. Please, go ahead.

Nina: Thanks. I’m Nina. This is my first time coming to this event.

Arif: Nice to meet you, Nina. I’m Arif. I’ve been coming here for a few months now.

Nina: Oh really? I was a bit nervous before coming. I didn’t know anyone.

Arif: I understand. I felt the same way at first. Why did you decide to join?

Nina: I work from home, so I don’t meet many people during the week. I wanted to be more social.

Arif: That makes sense. I work full-time in an office, but I still felt a bit isolated. This event helps me relax after work.

Nina: That sounds nice. Everyone seems friendly so far.

Arif: Yes, I think so too. I’ve noticed that people here listen and ask questions. It makes conversations easier.

Nina: I agree. When someone listens, I feel more comfortable talking.

Arif: Exactly. Simple conversations can really help people feel connected.

Nina: I’m glad I came today. Starting a conversation is hard, but this feels good.

Arif: I’m glad to hear that. It gets easier with practice.

Task 9: Case Study - Social Problem Solving

Scenario: New member stops coming because no one talked to them.

Discuss:
  • What went wrong in the conversation?
  • What could group members do differently?
  • What advice would you give the new member?
Wrong: Lack of welcoming behavior or questions.
Differently: Assign a "greeter" or actively ask open questions to new people.
Advice: Try one more time, but perhaps initiate a conversation yourself using "Is this seat free?" like Nina did.

Task 10: Final Reflection

Summarize the conversation. Main message? Importance of listening?

Sample Summary: Nina and Arif meet at a community event. Nina is new and nervous, but Arif welcomes her. They discuss why they joined—Nina works from home and wants to be social, while Arif wants to relax after office work. They agree that friendly people and good listening make conversations easier. By the end, Nina feels comfortable and glad she came.
