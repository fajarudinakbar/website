Listen: Optimized for B1 learners

Digital payment systems are growing quickly around the world. They make it easier for people to pay without using cash. One important step comes from Indonesia. Starting in April 2026, Indonesians can use QRIS in South Korea. This is a big moment for financial technology and will help both countries.

What is QRIS?

QRIS means Quick Response Code Indonesian Standard. It is a payment system created by Bank Indonesia in 2019. With QRIS, people can pay by scanning one QR code. This code works for many payment methods, such as mobile banking and digital wallets. Instead of using different apps or cards, QRIS makes payments simple and universal.

At first, QRIS was only used inside Indonesia. But later, it started to connect with other countries. This helps reduce the need for cash or international cards. It also supports the growth of digital finance.

QRIS in South Korea

From April 2026, QRIS will officially work in South Korea. This is the result of cooperation between Bank Indonesia and the Bank of Korea. The two banks signed an agreement in 2024 and tested the system in early 2026. Now, the system is ready for full use.

Indonesian tourists in South Korea can pay at many shops by scanning QR codes with their usual Indonesian apps. This makes travel easier and faster. It also shows Indonesia’s plan to connect its payment system with the global market. QRIS is already linked with some countries in Southeast Asia and East Asia. Adding South Korea makes Indonesia stronger in the regional payment network.

Benefits for Tourists and Businesses

For Indonesian tourists, QRIS brings many advantages. They do not need to carry large amounts of cash or exchange money before traveling. Payments can be made directly through their apps. This saves time and reduces costs. QRIS also lowers fees for currency exchange and international transactions.

For businesses in South Korea, QRIS means more customers from Indonesia. Shops and restaurants can serve Indonesian tourists more easily. For Indonesian businesses, this is also a chance to expand into global markets and improve competitiveness.

Impact on the Digital Economy

The use of QRIS in South Korea will help not only tourism but also the digital economy. Easier payments across countries will increase trade and consumption. The cooperation also supports the use of local currencies in international transactions. This reduces dependence on certain global currencies and helps economic stability in the region.

In Indonesia, QRIS use is already growing fast. More people are comfortable with digital payments. This shows that society is ready for cross border payment systems.

Challenges and Future Plans

Of course, there are challenges. Both countries need strong infrastructure and systems that work together. Education for users and businesses is also important. People must understand how to use QRIS safely and effectively.

Even so, the future looks bright. More countries are joining QR payment systems. QRIS could become part of a wider global payment network. With good cooperation and strong support, QRIS may play an important role in the future of world finance.
