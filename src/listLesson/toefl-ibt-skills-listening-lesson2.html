Part II: Predicting Main Ideas

In English listening, to predict means to "guess" what you are going to hear before you listen. You can use your own background knowledge, titles, pictures, or the beginning of the audio to form an idea of what the content will be. Being able to predict correctly can help you better understand long and complex conversations.

▶ Play Audio: Library Services

Transcript: The campus library provides several useful services to our students. First, the library is an excellent resource for study materials. At the library, you can find books and magazines on almost any topic. And, if you need to do some research online, you can check out, or borrow, a laptop on the 3rd floor of the library. Also, we have many skilled librarians who specialize in diverse areas, such as the natural sciences, engineering, social sciences, and art. These librarians can help you do a literature search or locate materials that you need. They can even teach you how to do research effectively. We highly encourage you to utilize these kind professionals. Finally, the library has over thirty study rooms for you to use. The study rooms have different capacities, or sizes. Some rooms can hold four people, while others can hold up to eight. All of these rooms have projectors, whiteboards and other equipment that can aid in your study. The library is open twenty-four hours a day, all week long. Please drop by and take advantage of this valuable educational resource.

▶ Play Audio: Office Hours

Transcript: Every professor at the university has office hours. Going to your professor's office hours is a great idea for several reasons. To start with, you can ask questions about difficult course material during a professor's office hours. If you ever get confused about a topic, don't panic, and just visit your professor. Your professor will be glad to clarify things for you! Second, professors are excellent resources for study materials and assignment help. During a professor's office hours, you can chat about the best study tips, top academic resources in a field, and research topics. Finally, seeing professors during their office hours is one way to get academic or even career advice. Professors know all about the courses offered by their departments. They can help you choose the perfect classes for the career path you want to pursue. Professors are generally approachable and happy to help you. If you cannot make a particular professor's office hours, you can also set up an appointment with him or her.

Part IV: TOEFL Tip

Predicting the main idea before listening allows you to use background information and vocabulary you already know to better understand a listening passage. Predictions can help you listen more effectively in academic settings and during exams like the TOEFL iBT® test.
