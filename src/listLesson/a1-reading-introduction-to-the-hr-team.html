From: Hartono (HR Manager)

To: Dewi (HR Admin)

Subject: Welcome to the Team / Office Guide

Selamat Pagi Dewi,

Welcome to PT. Nusantara. We are very happy you are joining the Human Resources (HR) team today. I am in a meeting right now, so here is a guide to your new colleagues in the room.

The Recruitment Team

Look at the front of the room. The woman wearing the blue batik is Siti. She is our Recruitment Officer. She interviews new candidates every day. If you need to find a file about a new employee, please ask her.

Payroll and Finance

In the corner, you can see a man with glasses and a white shirt. That is Budi. He manages the salaries (payroll). He is very busy at the end of the month!

Training and Development

Next to Budi, there is a woman with short hair sitting near the window. Her name is Lestari. She organizes training for the staff. You will work with her next week to prepare the meeting room.

My desk is the big one at the back. See you at 10:00 AM for coffee.

Best regards,
Pak Hartono
