Listening Center

Video Script

Speaker 1: Hello. Can I help you?

Speaker 2: Yes, please. I need some apples. How much are they?

Speaker 1: The red apples are two dollars for one kilo.

Speaker 2: Great. I want two kilos of red apples, please.

Speaker 1: Okay, that is four dollars. Do you need anything else?

Speaker 2: Yes. How much is a bottle of orange juice?

Speaker 1: One bottle of orange juice costs three dollars.

Speaker 2: I want three bottles of orange juice, please.

Speaker 1: Perfect. That is nine dollars for the juice. The total price is thirteen dollars.

Speaker 2: Here is fifteen dollars.

Speaker 1: Thank you. Here is your change: two dollars.

Speaker 2: Thank you very much. Goodbye.
