Listening Center

Video Script

Tom: Hello Anna. Please help me with this task.

Anna: Yes, Tom. What do I need to do?

Tom: Please listen carefully. First, open the paper tray on the copy machine.

Anna: Okay, Tom. I see the tray. It is open.

Tom: Good. Now, put paper inside. Then, press the big green button.

Anna: I am pressing the green button now. How many copies do we need?

Tom: We need five copies of this document.

Anna: The machine is finished. Where do I put the copies, Tom?

Tom: Put them inside the blue folder on my desk. They are for Mr. Davis.

Anna: I will put them in the folder and give them to Mr. Davis now.

Tom: Thank you, Anna.
