Listening Center

Video Script

Hello everyone, my name is Marco. Today, I want to tell you about my life. I live in a small, modern house in Rome, Italy. My house has two quiet bedrooms, a big kitchen, and a small garden. I live with my happy family.

I am married and we have one young daughter. Her name is Giulia and she is four years old. I work as a professional chef in a busy restaurant downtown. I love my job because I like to cook delicious food for people. I work there from Tuesday to Saturday every week. That is my description of my home, family, and job. Thank you very much.
