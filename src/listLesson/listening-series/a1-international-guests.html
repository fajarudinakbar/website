Phase 1: Vocabulary Practice

Key Words and Phrases

Study the words in the table below before you listen to the conversation.

Word / Phrase Meaning Example
receptionist a person who welcomes visitors at the front desk The receptionist helps you find the correct room.
engineer a person whose job is to design or build things She is an engineer and works with computers.
manager the person in charge of a team or department Talk to the manager if you have a work problem.
meeting room a special room where people talk about business We have a large table inside the meeting room.
office a building or room where people work at desks Our main office is located in the city center.

Listening Center

Video Script

Speaker 1: Good morning. Welcome to our London office. How can I help you?

Speaker 2: Hello. My name is Maria. I am a new engineer here.

Speaker 1: Nice to meet you, Maria. My name is Tom. Are you from our office in Spain?

Speaker 2: Yes, I work at the Madrid office. Today I have a meeting here.

Speaker 1: Great. Who is your meeting with?

Speaker 2: I am here to see the manager. His name is Mr. Davis.

Speaker 1: Wonderful. Mr. Davis is in Meeting Room 3.

Speaker 2: Thank you, Tom. Where is Meeting Room 3?

Speaker 1: It is on the first floor. Enjoy your day in London!

Speaker 2: Thank you very much.
