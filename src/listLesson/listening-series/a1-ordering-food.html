Listening Center

Video Script

Lisa (Speaker 1): Good morning, John. Welcome back to the campus cafe. What can I get for you today?

John (Speaker 2): Hi, Lisa. I am so hungry after class. Can I have a chicken sandwich and a cold bottle of water, please?

Lisa (Speaker 1): Sure. Do you want a hot coffee too? The coffee is fresh.

John (Speaker 2): No, thank you. I do not drink coffee. Just the sandwich and water is fine.

Lisa (Speaker 1): Okay. The total price is six dollars. Are you paying with a card?

John (Speaker 2): No, I only have cash today. Here is a ten-dollar bill.

Lisa (Speaker 1): Perfect. Here is your change and your food. Enjoy your lunch, John!

John (Speaker 2): Thanks, Lisa. Have a great day.
