Listening Center

Video Script

Speaker 1 (Paul): Hello. My name is Paul. I am the new office assistant.

Speaker 2 (Susan): Hi Paul. Nice to meet you. I am Susan.

Speaker 1 (Paul): Nice to meet you, Susan. What is your job here?

Speaker 2 (Susan): I am a manager in the sales team. Today is your first day, right?

Speaker 1 (Paul): Yes, it is. I work from Monday to Friday.

Speaker 2 (Susan): Welcome to our company. Do you live near the office building?

Speaker 1 (Paul): Yes, I live in a small apartment near the local train station.

Speaker 2 (Susan): That is very good. Do you want to drink a cup of coffee now?

Speaker 1 (Paul): Yes, please. Where is the cafeteria?

Speaker 2 (Susan): It is on the first floor. Let us walk there together.

Speaker 1 (Paul): Thank you, Susan. You are very kind.
