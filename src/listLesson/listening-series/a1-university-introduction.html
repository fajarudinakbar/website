Hello everyone. My name is Anna. I am a new student at this university. This is my first year here on campus. My major is history because I love to read books about the past. I live in a small apartment near the university. Every morning, I walk to my classroom.

My favorite place on campus is the big library. I study there every afternoon from 2:00 PM to 5:00 PM. My main history classroom is located on the second floor of the science building. I really want to meet new friends in this class. Please say hello to me when you see me. Thank you very much and nice to meet you.
