Video Script

Hello everyone. My name is John Lee. I am the new IT assistant. Today is my first day at this large company. I work in the technology department on the third floor. My manager is Mr. Smith. His office is next to the main elevator.

I work here from Monday to Friday, from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. I am very happy to join this great team. In my free time, I like to drink hot coffee and read books. The company cafeteria is on the first floor, so I hope to see you there during lunch time. Nice to meet you all. Thank you very much.
