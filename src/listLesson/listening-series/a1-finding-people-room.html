Listening Center

Video Script

Speaker 1: Good morning. Welcome to our building. My name is Alex.

Speaker 2: Hello, Alex. I am new here. My name is Robert.

Speaker 1: Nice to meet you, Robert. Are you from our Jakarta office?

Speaker 2: No, I work at the Singapore office. I need to find the design team.

Speaker 1: I see. You have a meeting with the manager, Ms. Clara.

Speaker 2: Yes, that is correct. Where is her office today?

Speaker 1: Ms. Clara is in the Paris Room. It is on the second floor.

Speaker 2: Thank you, Alex. Is the Paris Room near the stairs?

Speaker 1: Yes, it is next to the stairs.

Speaker 2: Perfect. Thank you for your help.

Speaker 1: You are welcome. Have a good day.
