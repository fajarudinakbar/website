AI Response:

"Softening means making your words less direct to sound more polite and respectful. We use 'I was wondering if' because it gives the other person a gentle way to say no without feeling pressured. Examples in a hospital include: 'Would you mind helping me with this chart?', 'Could we possibly schedule a meeting?', and 'I was hoping you might have time to review this.'"

Requesting an Extension

Ready to play native browser audio.

Listening Transcript

Junior Resident: "Excuse me, Dr. Hartono. I am sorry to bother you, but I was wondering if I could speak with you for a moment regarding the clinical trial report?"

Senior Consultant: "Of course. Is everything alright with the data?"

Junior Resident: "The data is fine, but I have encountered some unforeseen CIRCUMSTANCES with the lab results. I was hoping you might be able to grant me a short EXTENSION on the submission? I do not want to HESITATE to ask, but I think the report would be much stronger with the final blood panels included."

Senior Consultant: "I see. When do you think you could have the final version ready?"

Junior Resident: "Would it be possible to submit it by Friday morning instead of Wednesday? I would really APPRECIATE your FLEXIBILITY on this. I understand how busy you are, and I am grateful for any ASSISTANCE you can provide with the lab coordination."

Senior Consultant: "Since you want to include the blood panels for accuracy, I can agree to that. Please ensure it is on my desk by Friday at 9:00 AM."

Junior Resident: "Thank you very much, Dr. Hartono. That is very helpful."

Task 9 Case Study Decision Making

Scenario:

A colleague is consistently late with their part of a shared research project. It is affecting your own work.

Option A: Use very soft language to ask if they need help, avoiding any mention of their lateness to keep the "Kizuna" (bond).
Option B: Use a "Sandwich" approach: Start with a compliment, clearly state the need for the data, and end with a polite request for a specific new deadline.

Discuss which option is more effective for a B1 professional.

Analysis:

Option B is more effective. The "Sandwich" approach balances professional accountability with politeness. Option A may protect the bond in the short term, but it fails to solve the work delay, which can lead to larger conflicts later. Option B uses structure to soften the direct request.
