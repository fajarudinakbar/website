Conversation Transcript

Peter: Hello there, I don’t think we’ve met yet. I’m Peter, one of the senior designers.

Carla: Nice to meet you, Peter. I’m Carla. I actually just joined the firm recently as a marketing associate.

Peter: Well, welcome aboard, Carla! How has your first week been so far?

Carla: It has been quite intense but very rewarding. Everyone here seems extremely collaborative, which makes the transition much easier.

Peter: That’s great to hear. I’m based in the design department—our workstations are located just across the hall if you ever need anything.

Carla: Oh, interesting! I’ve spent most of my time this week on onboarding and getting familiar with our current digital campaigns.

Peter: Marketing is a vital role here. Are you finding the workflow easy to manage?

Carla: Mostly, though I have to admit there is a steep learning curve with the project management software we use.

Peter: I completely understand. It took me a few months to master it myself. If you ever run into technical issues, feel free to drop by my desk.

Carla: I really appreciate that, Peter. What exactly is your focus within the design team?

Peter: I primarily handle product development and user experience. We try to ensure everything we build is functional and attractive.

Carla: That sounds perfect. My role involves coordinating promotional events for our upcoming launches, so our teams will definitely need to sync up soon.

Peter: I agree. It’s always better when Marketing and Design are on the same page. By the way, I noticed your accent—where are you originally from?

Carla: I’m from Brazil, specifically São Paulo. And yourself?

Peter: I’m from the UK. I’ve been based here for about five years now; I originally relocated to finish my Master’s degree and decided to stay once I landed this position three years ago.

  • How does Carla describe the culture of the company so far?
