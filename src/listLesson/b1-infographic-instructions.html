GRAMMAR TOOLKIT

Passive Causatives

Use this structure when someone else does a service for you.

Structure: have/get + object + past participle

Example: "I need to have my car fixed before the trip."
PHRASE TOOLKIT

Polite Commands & Sequence

Use these phrases to give clear and polite instructions.

  • First of all, make sure to...
  • After that, try to avoid...
  • Finally, it's important that...
  • Would you mind making the...?
