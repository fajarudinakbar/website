Professional Email Model

Subject: Progress Update Week 18 (May 4, 2026)
To: Dr. Hiroshi Tanaka, Senior Supervisor
From: Cardiology Department Research Intern

Dear Dr. Tanaka,

Please find below the summary of my activities and research achievements for the current week. Following our last meeting, I have focused on the following objectives:

  • FINALIZED the data entry for the Phase 2 heart valve study. All 50 patient records are now updated in the central database.

  • COLLABORATED with the radiology team to review three remaining echocardiogram reports. We identified a small DISCREPANCY in one report, which is currently being corrected.

  • INITIATED the literature review for our upcoming paper on pediatric arrhythmias. I have already cataloged fifteen relevant peer-reviewed articles.

  • ATTENDED the multidisciplinary team (MDT) meeting on Wednesday to observe the surgical planning for the aortic aneurysm case.

  • SCHEDULED a follow-up interview with the pharmaceutical representative to clarify the side effects of the new beta-blocker trial.

Upcoming Objectives (Week 19):

  • Incorporate the supervisor's feedback into the draft of the arrhythmia paper.
  • Perform a preliminary ANALYSIS of the Phase 2 data trends.

Thank you for your guidance.

Best regards,

Junior Researcher
Cardiology Department

Task 6 Sentence Combining

Writing skill: Combining ideas clearly. Combine the cause and effect into one professional sentence using words like "resulting in", "therefore", or "which is".

1. Cause: We found a discrepancy in the report.
Effect: We are currently correcting the report.

2. Cause: I attended the MDT meeting on Wednesday.
Effect: I gained insight into the surgical planning process.

Suggested Combinations:
  • We found a discrepancy in the report, which is currently being corrected.
  • I attended the MDT meeting on Wednesday, resulting in valuable insight into the surgical planning process.
