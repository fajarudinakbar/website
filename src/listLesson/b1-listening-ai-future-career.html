AI and Your Future Career

Many people today fear that Artificial Intelligence will destroy white-collar office jobs. However, data suggests that AI is more likely to RESHAPE jobs rather than erase them entirely. Experts describe the future office as a "cyborg" environment, where humans and AI work together as a team.

Looking at data from the last three years, white-collar employment has actually increased by 3 million jobs. Even in professions like software development, radiology, and legal work—tasks that AI can perform well—employment has grown significantly. For example, paralegal jobs increased by 20%. Historically, new technologies like the internet have been a BOON for the workforce. While some routine jobs, like typists, were replaced, they were overcompensated by the creation of new roles in e-commerce and logistics.

Currently, AI can fully AUTOMATE very few roles. Most jobs consist of many different tasks, and AI usually only automates the routine ones. This allows humans to focus on higher-value activities like coordination and judgment. However, there are risks. Entry-level workers, such as interns and junior programmers, are more VULNERABLE to DISPLACEMENT. Additionally, routine back-office roles, like administrative assistants, have seen a 20% decrease in employment. Society must find ways to help these workers transition into new roles as the white-collar landscape continues to adjust and expand.
