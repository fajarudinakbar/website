When you feel stuck in an unfulfilling job, quitting suddenly might seem tempting. But how you leave a job can affect your career and relationships. Career coach Gala Jackson explains that quitting well can be an opportunity for growth if you do it with courage, confidence, and clarity.

First, reflect on why you want to leave and what you want in your next role. Set small goals that help you move forward. Do a financial checkup so you are prepared for changes in your income. Give your manager the proper notice and close your tasks in a positive way. Strengthen your relationships with coworkers before you leave.

Networking is important. Talk with people in your field and share your plans. Sometimes taking a short break can help you focus on your goals and let your network know what you are looking for. Leaving a job can be a chance to learn more about your strengths and build better opportunities for the future.

5.1 Rushed Resignation

Scenario: A worker angrily quits without notice and posts about it online.

Discuss:
  • What problems might this cause for their career?
  • What could they have done differently?
  • How might their reputation be affected?
Problems: Bad reference from boss, looks unprofessional.
Action: Wait to calm down, talk privately, give standard notice.
Reputation: Future employers might see them as unreliable or difficult.

5.2 Prepared Exit

Scenario: A professional plans to quit but worries about money.

Discuss:
  • What financial preparations should they make?
  • How can they use networking to find better opportunities?
  • What benefits could careful planning bring?
Prep: Save 3-6 months of expenses, cut unnecessary costs.
Networking: Tell contacts "I'm looking for new challenges" before leaving.
Benefit: Less stress, more confidence to wait for the right job.

5.3 Career Break

Scenario: Someone plans a short career break after quitting.

Discuss:
  • Why might a break help with clarity?
  • What risks come with taking a break?
  • How can they stay active in their field during the break?
Clarity: Distance from daily stress helps see big picture.
Risks: Gap in resume, money running out.
Active: Online courses, volunteering, or attending industry events.
