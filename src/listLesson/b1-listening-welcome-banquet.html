Task 9: Case Study Decision Making

Scenario:

The House Specialty arrives. It looks delicious, but you realize it contains a small amount of an ingredient you are mildly allergic to. You do not want to cause a scene in front of the Chief of Medicine.

  • Option A: Quietly move the food around your plate and do not eat it, focusing on the conversation instead.
  • Option B: Politely apologize to the host and the waiter immediately to ask for a replacement.

Discuss which option is more professional in a medical context.

Analysis: Both options carry meaning. Option A prioritizes social harmony but carries an unmanaged health risk, which conflicts with a medical professional's core values. Option B prioritizes safety and clear communication, which are essential medical traits. The best approach might be Option B executed with extreme politeness.
