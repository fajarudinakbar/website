Debt Challenges in Developing Nations

Many developing countries, including Indonesia, are facing significant challenges this year as their debt burdens increase. Based on recent data, this rise in debt is INFLUENCED by uncertain global economic conditions. One of the main factors is high international interest rates, which make interest payments more expensive for governments.

In Indonesia, the government is working to keep the debt ratio safe and controlled. However, this increasing burden still requires a strong MITIGATION strategy to avoid a financial crisis. The government must ensure that the national budget is used EFFICIENTLY, especially for projects that support long-term economic growth.

Additionally, dependence on foreign debt must be managed carefully. If the value of the rupiah weakens against the dollar, the debt burden will become even more HEAVY. Therefore, experts suggest that the government focus more on domestic income sources, such as taxes, and strengthen macroeconomic STABILITY. International cooperation is also considered important to provide technical assistance or relief for countries most affected by these global economic fluctuations.
