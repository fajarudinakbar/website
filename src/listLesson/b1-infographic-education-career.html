PAST SIMPLE

Finished Actions

Use this for actions that are completely finished at a specific time in the past.

Time Signals: yesterday, in 2020, last year, ago.

Example: "I graduated from university in 2022."
PRESENT PERFECT

Life Experience

Use this for actions that connect the past to the present, or for life experiences.

Time Signals: since, for, already, yet, ever, never.

Example: "I have worked in sales since 2020."
