Task 2: Vocabulary in Context

Read the sentences and choose the correct meaning for the capitalized word.

Fuel prices in the Philippines are currently skyrocketing due to international conflicts between the United States, Israel, and Iran. Diesel prices, in particular, have doubled recently. This is a major problem because "jeeps" (the local mini-buses) and tricycles rely on diesel to operate. Drivers are struggling to survive because they must absorb these extra costs, which means they take home much less money for their families.

To help, the government has started a program to provide a fuel subsidy to public transport workers. Officials have confirmed that $500 million has been earmarked for this assistance. There is also talk of reducing taxes on fuel, but experts worry this won't help much because the local oil industry is deregulated. This means the government does not control the prices, and the country depends almost entirely on imported oil. As costs stay high, transportation officials may be forced to increase passenger fares, which will impact all commuters.
