TOEFL iBT Speaking Skill-Building Worksheet

Task Type: Campus Announcement

Skill Focus: Summarizing announcement + Explaining opinion

Step 1: Understand the Task Type

In the Campus Announcement task, you will:

  1. Read a short announcement about a change or decision on campus.
  2. Listen to two students reacting to the announcement.
  3. Explain:
    • what the announcement says
    • what one student thinks
    • why the student feels that way (reasons)

Step 2: Read the Example Announcement

University Announcement

The university plans to introduce a new bike-sharing program next semester. Students will be able to borrow bicycles from stations around campus. The goal is to reduce traffic and make it easier for students to travel between buildings.

Check Understanding:

1. What new service will the university offer?

2. Why is the university introducing this program?

3. Who will use the bicycles?

  • Service: A bike-sharing program (borrow bicycles).
  • Reason: To reduce traffic and make travel easier between buildings.
  • Who: Students.

Step 3: Understand Student Opinions

Student A (Male): “I think the bike-sharing program is a great idea because it will save students a lot of time. Many of us have classes in different buildings, and walking across campus can take too long.”

Student B (Female): “I’m not so sure. Bikes are helpful, but I’m worried there won’t be enough of them during busy times. Also, some students don’t know how to ride safely, so there could be accidents.”

Identify the opinion + reasons:

Student A
Student B

Step 4: Build the Response Structure

A strong response follows this pattern:

  • Summary: "The announcement states that the university will..."
  • Opinion: "The student supports/opposes this idea because..."
  • R1 + Explanation: "One reason is that..."
  • R2 + Explanation: "Another reason is..."

Practice: Complete the following frame using Student A's opinion.

“The announcement says that the university will start a bike-sharing program. The student thinks this is a good idea because . Another reason is that .”

Sample Completion:

“The announcement says that the university will start a bike-sharing program. The student thinks this is a good idea because it will save students a lot of time. Another reason is that walking across campus takes too long.”

Step 5: Independent Speaking Practice (TOEFL-Style)

New Announcement

The university cafeteria will extend its opening hours until midnight. The goal is to give students more flexibility, especially those who study late or attend evening classes.

Task

Explain the announcement and describe the student’s opinion about this change. Include clear reasons supporting the opinion.

01:00

Note: In a real test, you would hear a student conversation here. For this practice, invent an opinion (e.g., "The student loves it because she studies late").

Model: "The announcement states that the university cafeteria will stay open until midnight to help students who study late. The student thinks this is a fantastic idea for two reasons.

First, she mentions that she often studies in the library until 11 PM and gets very hungry, but everything is usually closed. This change will allow her to get food without leaving campus.

Second, she points out that evening classes finish late, and students don't have time to cook when they get home. So, this extended schedule gives them a convenient option for dinner."
