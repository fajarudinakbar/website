DET Read and Complete

Target Score: 80–100 | Skill: Lexical Recognition & Syntactic Knowledge

Instructions: Master the DET Read and Complete task by identifying letter counts, context anchors, and syntactic clues. Complete the drills in order to build speed and accuracy.

Step 1: What to Focus On

  • Sentence Boundaries: Always read the first and last sentences fully; they are your context anchors.
  • Articles & Prepositions: Identify small "functional" words (e.g., th-, o-, i-) first.
  • Count the Dashes: Each dash (-) represents exactly one missing letter.
  • Discourse Markers: Identify transitions like ho---- (however) to understand sentence relationships.
  • Subject-Verb Agreement: Ensure the word ending matches the subject (e.g., play- vs plays).

Step 2: The Audit

The Scenario: You see the sentence: "He needed to buy a gift, so he went to the st---."

The Trap: The student types "stage." While "stage" has 5 letters and starts with "st," it makes no sense to buy a gift at a stage. Correct Pattern: The student identifies "store" (st + 3 letters) fitting the letter count AND context.

Task Audit Analysis

Look at the word pl--- in this sentence: "The kids like to pl--- in the park." If the student writes "place," why is it a "trap" even though the letter count is correct?

Answer: "Place" is a trap because the syntax requires a verb after the infinitive marker "to" (to play). Contextually, "play" makes sense for kids in a park, while "place" disrupts the grammatical structure and meaning.
