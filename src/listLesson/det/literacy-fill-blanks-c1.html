Fill in the Blanks

Score Range: 120–135 | Focus: Vocabulary & Spelling

Instructions: Read the sentence and complete the missing word. The first few letters are provided. You have 2 attempts per exercise before the answer is revealed.

Step 1 The Concept Breakdown

The Task: You will see a sentence where a specific academic word is partially hidden. You must type the remaining letters.

The Goal: Demonstrate precise vocabulary recall. For example, seeing "partic..." and typing "ularly" to make "particularly".

Step 2 Recognition Drill

Identify the missing parts in these C1 examples:

"The evidence provided was purely anecdotal, lacking scientific rigor."

Target: Anecdotal (based on personal accounts rather than facts).

"The manager tried to alleviate the fears of the staff regarding layoffs."

Target: Alleviate (to make something less severe).

Step 3 Strategic Roadmap

1. Visual Length

Estimate the number of letters needed. If the box is short, it might be a suffix like "-ed" or "-ly".

2. Suffix Logic

If the word starts with "develop" and is a noun, try "-ment". If adjective, try "-ing" or "-ed".

3. Context Clues

Look at the surrounding words. "Due to s_ _ _ _ _" likely refers to "supply" if followed by "demand".

Step 4 Foundation Warm-up

Complete these common academic words:

"The price of gasoline fluctuates due to su and demand."

"The scientists conducted a thoro investigation."

"While many expected the merger to be a disaster, the two companies showed a remar ability to integrate smoothly."

Thinking Prompts:

  • Contrast: "While" + "disaster" indicates the blank is a positive adjective.
  • Stem: "remar..." suggests something worthy of attention.
