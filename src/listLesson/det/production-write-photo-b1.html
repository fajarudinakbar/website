Write About the Photo

Score Range: 80–110 | Focus: Description, Action & Syntax

Instructions: Master the 60-second photo description task. Move through the steps to build your observation and sentence structure skills.

Step 1 The Concept Breakdown

You have exactly 60 seconds. To achieve a B1 score (80–110), aim for 2–3 clear sentences describing the subject, action, and setting.

Measures: Vocabulary range, prepositions, and basic complex sentences.

Step 2 Recognition Drill: Categories

Match the photo description to the correct category.

Step 3 Strategic Roadmap

1. Main to Detail

Start with a "big picture" sentence (Subject + Action), then add details (colors, emotions).

2. Maximize the Clock

Don't stop at one sentence. Aim for 25-30 words to show volume.

3. Preposition Power

Use "next to", "in the background", or "on the left" to show spatial awareness.

Step 4 Foundation Warm-up

Unscramble the words to create a correct B1 sentence.

Thinking Prompts

  • Who? A chef/cook.
  • Action? Cooking/tossing food.
  • Where? Professional kitchen.

Subject: A photo showing a chef in a kitchen tossing vegetables in a pan.

Draft Your Response:

Aim for 2-3 sentences.

B1 Model: "In this image, a professional chef is cooking vegetables in a large pan. He is standing in a modern kitchen, and there is steam rising from the food. He looks very focused on his work."

Why: Uses present continuous ("is cooking"), compound sentences, and descriptive adjectives.

Step 6: Technical Protocol

  • Disable Auto-Correct: No spell-check allowed.
  • Full-Screen: Avoid distractions.
  • Timer: You have exactly 60 seconds.

Step 7 Independent Challenge

Model Response:

"This photo shows three students sitting together on the grass. They are studying a book and look very happy because they are smiling. In the background, there is a large building and some trees."
