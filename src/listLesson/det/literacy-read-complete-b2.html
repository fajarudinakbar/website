Step 1 The Concept Breakdown

The Task: Fill in missing letters in the middle sentences of a paragraph. First/last sentences are complete.

The Goal: Contextual Precision. Use grammar and theme to solve the puzzle.

Step 2 Recognition Drill

Identify the missing parts:

Step 3 Strategic Roadmap

1. "Bookends"

Read the first & last sentence FIRST. They define the topic and tense.

2. Letter Math

Count the visual space. 'cont____' (4 letters given) usually needs ~4-5 more letters (continue).

3. Predict Grammar

After "The"? Noun. After "could"? Verb. Guess the grammar before the word.
