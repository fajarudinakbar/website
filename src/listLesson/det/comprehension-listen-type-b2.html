Listen and Type

Score Range: 105–125 | Focus: Precision, Spelling & Punctuation

Instructions: Master the art of verbatim transcription. You must type exactly what you hear with 100% accuracy in spelling and punctuation.

Step 1 The Concept Breakdown

The Task: You hear a recording. You have one minute to type exactly what you hear. Replay allowed twice (3 listens total).

The Goal: Precision. 100% verbatim match required. Spelling, capitalization, and punctuation must be perfect.

Step 2 Recognition Drill

You will encounter these sentence types:

Simple (A2/B1): "The library is closed on Sundays."
Compound (B2): "I wanted to go to the concert, but I couldn't find a ticket."
Complex/Academic (C1): "Although the results were inconclusive, the scientists decided to publish their preliminary findings."

Step 3 Strategic Roadmap

1-2-3 Listening

  1. Type main keywords.
  2. Fill small words/fix spelling.
  3. Final polish (caps/punct).

Punctuation Mandatory

Must start with Capital and end with Period/Question mark.

Homophone Check

Use grammar context for there/their/they're.

Step 4 Foundation Warm-up: Spelling

Choose the correct spelling/word for these common errors.

Target Audio Script

"The unexpected delay caused significant frustration among the passengers."

Student Draft (Flawed):

the unexpected delay caused significant frustrasion among the passengers

Errors:
  • Lower case 't'
  • Spelling: 'frustrasion' (should be -tion)
  • Missing period '.'

Model Response (C1):

The unexpected delay caused significant frustration among the passengers.

Why it works:
  • Correct mechanics (Capital T, period).
  • Complex vocabulary spelled correctly.
