"Do you think it is better to study alone or in a group?"

Model Answer A (Fragmented - B1)

"I like studying in groups. You can ask friends questions. It is fun and we can share notes. Sometimes it is loud, but I think it is better than being alone at home."

Model Answer B (PEEL Structured - B2+)

"(P) I maintain that group study is more effective due to the collaborative environment it fosters. (E) For instance, explaining a concept to a peer solidifies one's own understanding. (Ex) This active engagement prevents passive reading and highlights knowledge gaps. (L) Consequently, group work often leads to superior academic outcomes compared to solitary study."

Step 3: The 'Strategy Fix-It' Drill

Mediocre Response:

"Electric cars are good. They don't use gas so the air is clean. People should buy them to help the earth. I want one in the future."

Task: Rewrite immediately using PEEL and these Pro-Tips:
  • Pro-Tip 1: Start with a Point about "environmental sustainability."
  • Pro-Tip 2: Use the word 'Efficacy' or 'Implementation'.
  • Pro-Tip 3: Replace 'good' with 'imperative' or 'advantageous'.
"The transition to electric vehicles is imperative for long-term environmental sustainability. By eliminating reliance on fossil fuels, these vehicles significantly reduce urban air pollution. The efficacy of this technology in combating climate change is well-documented. Consequently, widespread implementation of electric transit systems is advantageous for securing a greener future."

Read, Then Speak Prompt:

"Talk about a book you read recently. What was it about and would you recommend it?"

Task 1: PEEL Skeleton
  • P (Point): "Recently, I completed [Title], which explores the theme of..."
  • E (Evidence): "A specific example of this is when the protagonist..."
  • E (Explanation): "This illustrates the author's message regarding..."
  • L (Link): "For these reasons, I would highly recommend it to anyone interested in..."
Task 2: The Upgrade

Integrate these 3 'Booster Phrases':

  • "...the narrative delves into..."
  • "...presents a thought-provoking perspective on..."
  • "...is widely regarded as..."
"Recently, I completed '1984', which explores the theme of totalitarianism. The narrative delves into the dangers of absolute government control. A specific example of this is when the protagonist struggles with constant surveillance, which presents a thought-provoking perspective on modern privacy. This illustrates the author's message regarding freedom. Because it is widely regarded as a classic warning, I would highly recommend it to anyone interested in political history."
