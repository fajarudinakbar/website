DET Read, Then Speak

Target Score: 120–135 | Skill: Complex Thought Organization & Oral Fluency

Instructions: Master the C1 DET "Read, Then Speak" task by utilizing inversions, advanced lexis, and complex signposting. Practice organizing your thoughts instantly to boost Production and Oral Fluency subscores.

Step 1: What to Focus On

Score Impact: Heavily influences Production and Oral Fluency subscores. It measures your ability to organize complex thoughts instantly.

  • Inversion for Emphasis: (e.g., "Not only... but also", "Had I known...").
  • Nuanced Adverbs: (e.g., profoundly, arguably, essentially).
  • Hypothetical Conditionals: (Third/Mixed Conditionals).
  • Complex Signposting: (e.g., "Turning to the latter point").
  • Precise Lexis: (avoiding good/bad/thing).

Step 2: The Audit

Prompt: Talk about a time you had to learn a new skill. What was it, and why was it challenging?

Basic Answer (B1/B2)

"I learned to code last year. It was very hard because the language was new. I practiced every day and finally I got better. It is a good skill for my job."

High-Score Answer (C1)

"Last year, I embarked on the journey of learning Python. Had I realized how steep the learning curve was, I might have hesitated. It was exceptionally daunting because it required a shift in my logical framework. However, through persistent iteration, I mastered it, which has been paramount to my career progression."

Task Analysis Checklist

Use this checklist to identify why the "High-Score" answer wins:

  • Conditional Inversion: "Had I realized..." (instead of "If I had realized...")
  • Low-Frequency Adjectives: "exceptionally daunting", "paramount"
  • Transition for Contrast: "However"

Step 3 The Level-Up Drill

Weak Response: "I think social media is bad for kids. They use it too much and don't talk to people. We should stop this."

Task: Rewrite this response using these 3 Improvement Rules:
(1) Start with a concessive clause (e.g., While...)
(2) Replace "bad" with a C1 synonym (e.g., detrimental)
(3) Include the phrase "the rami-------- (10) are vast."

Sample Answer: While many view social media as a tool for connection, its effects are arguably detrimental; the ramifications are vast.
