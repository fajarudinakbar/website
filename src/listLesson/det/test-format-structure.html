DET Format & Structure

Score Range: 10–160 Target Level: CEFR A1–C2 Focus: Test Format, Subscores & Rules

Goal: Understand the adaptive nature of the DET and how to navigate the technical environment.

The Concept Breakdown

The Duolingo English Test (DET) is a computer-adaptive proficiency exam. Unlike traditional tests, it is integrated, impacting four main subscores: Literacy, Comprehension, Conversation, and Production.

1. Introduction (~5 min) Onboarding, camera/mic check, ID submission.
2. Adaptive Test (~45 min) Difficulty changes based on performance (Correct = Harder, Incorrect = Easier).
3. Samples (~10 min) Writing and Speaking samples sent to institutions (non-adaptive).
