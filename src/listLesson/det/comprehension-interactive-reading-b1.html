Interactive Reading Strategy

Score Range: 60–90 | Focus: Logical Flow & Text Completion

Instructions: Follow the strategic steps below. Use the interactive tools to practice connecting ideas logically within a text.

Step 1 Concept Breakdown

Interactive Reading evaluates how well you understand the "big picture" of a text. It's not just "what happened," but "how does this fit together?" You will fill in missing words, complete paragraphs, and identify main titles.

Step 2 Foundation Warm-up: Contextual Matching

Match the transition words to their function. This is key for the "Complete the Passage" section.

Step 3 Strategic Roadmap

1. The "Surrounding Clue" Rule

Read the sentence before and after the blank. The answer connects both sides.

2. Grammar Consistency

Check tenses (past/present). Look for "s" on verbs for third-person subjects.

3. Time Management

You have ~7-8 minutes for the whole passage. Don't get stuck on one blank.

Step 4 Recognition Drill

You will encounter these three variations:

Read and Complete

"The house w__ built in 1990."

Task: Fill missing letters.

Complete the Passage

Choose the best sentence to fill a gap between paragraphs.

Task: Ensure logical flow.

Identify Main Idea

Select the best title for the entire passage.

Task: Summarize the whole text.

Step 5 Indonesian Context Bridge

In Indonesia’s tech and tourism sectors, this skill is like reading a project brief. Imagine working for a Jakarta startup and getting a memo about a partnership. You need to "fill in the blanks" of what is expected based on context. Spotting logical flow prevents misunderstandings in contracts or client requests.

Step 6 Complete the Transition

"Many tourists visit Bali for its beautiful beaches. , the island also offers rich cultural experiences in towns like Ubud."

Thinking Prompts:

  • Is the second sentence adding similar info or contrasting?
  • Does "also offers" suggest the first point was only one part?

C1 Model Responses:

  • "However" (Acceptable B1)
  • "Nevertheless" (Sophisticated)
  • "Beyond the coastline" (High-level cohesion)

Why? These acknowledge the first sentence while introducing a new topic.

Step 7 DIY Simulation Protocol

  1. Open a blank document (Word/Docs).
  2. Set a timer for 3 minutes.
  3. Find a medium-length English news article. copy one paragraph.
  4. Delete 5 random adjectives or verbs.
  5. Task: Fill them back in from logic/memory before the timer hits zero.

Pro-Tip: Use a "Distraction Blocker" extension to mimic the locked-down browser.

Step 8 Exam Task: Select the Best Sentence

"Digital payment systems have become very popular in Southeast Asia over the last five years. Most people now use mobile apps to pay for groceries and transport. [ ___________ ] This shift has allowed small businesses to reach more customers than ever before."
