Interactive Reading Strategy

Score Range: 105–130 | Focus: Inference, Context & Logic

Instructions: Master the multi-stage reading task. Learn to complete sentences, highlight answers, and identify main ideas under timed conditions.

Step 1 The Concept Breakdown

The Task: A reading "detective" mission. You engage with ONE text through 5-6 different sub-tasks (Complete Sentences, Highlight Answer, Identify Idea, etc.).

The Goal: Prove you understand the "big picture" (Literacy) and specific details (Comprehension) within a 7-8 minute limit.

Step 2 Recognition Drill

Identify the "clues" in these sub-tasks:

1. Complete the Sentences

"Modern architecture [---] focuses on [---] and functionality." (Select from dropdown options).

2. Highlight the Answer

Q: What influenced the materials? -> Click & drag to select "local limestone" in the text.

3. Identify the Idea

Choose the best summary statement (A, B, or C) for the whole text.

Step 3 Strategic Roadmap

1. One-Minute Rule

Don't get stuck. Guess and move on. You cannot go back once you click NEXT.

2. Pronoun Tracking

Look for "This", "They", "However" to link sentences logically in 'Complete the Passage'.

3. Keyword Markers

Find synonyms. Question asks "challenges"? Text says "difficulties". Highlight that.

Step 4 Foundation Warm-up: Context Predictor

Determine the part of speech needed for the blank.

Task Complete the Sentences

"The migration patterns of monarch butterflies are . Every year, millions of these insects thousands of miles to reach warmer climates."

Thinking Prompts:

  • Gap 1: Is it "random" or "extraordinary"? Hint: Look at "millions" and "thousands of miles"—this implies something impressive.
  • Gap 2: What do they do for "thousands of miles"?

Step 6: Technical Simulation

  • Timer: 7 Minutes Total.
  • Workflow: Summarize (1 sentence) -> Highlight Details -> Identify Main Idea -> Title.
  • Tip: Practice shifting between "scanning" and "skimming".

Step 7 Exam Challenge

"The Renaissance was a period of intense cultural and artistic 'rebirth' in Europe. [ Blank 1 ]. This era saw the rise of iconic figures like Leonardo da Vinci and Michelangelo, who blended science with art. Their work reflected a new in human potential, moving away from the purely religious focus of the Middle Ages."

Task B (Complete the Passage): Which sentence fits [ Blank 1 ]?
