Step 1: Rapid-Fire Task Specs (60-Second Brief)

The Impact: Directly dictates the Literacy (read/write) and Comprehension (read/listen) subscores.

The Targets:

  • Collocational Awareness: Recognizing words that naturally pair (e.g., 'highly likely').
  • Morphological Logic: Identifying correct suffixes for parts of speech (-tion, -ment, -ive).
  • Discourse Markers: Spotting 'however,' 'therefore,' etc. from initial letters.
  • Prepositional Precision: Filling in 'of,' 'to,' or 'with' based on the preceding verb.
  • Tense Continuity: Matching the verb ending to the overall passage timeline.
