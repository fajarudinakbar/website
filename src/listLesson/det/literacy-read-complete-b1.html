DET Skill Building: Read and Complete

Score Range: 80–105 | Focus: Syntax & Vocabulary in Context

Instructions: Master the "Read and Complete" task. Follow the steps from Preparation to Independent Challenge.
(Fill in the missing letters in the exercises below.)

Step 1 Concept Breakdown

The "Read and Complete" task tests your ability to fill in mutilated words where half the letters are missing. It measures your grasp of syntax (word order) and vocabulary in context.

Step 2 Foundation Warm-up: Suffixes

Match the common B1 suffixes to their word roots. This helps anticipate word endings.

Step 3 Strategic Roadmap

1. Context Sandwich

Read words before and after the blank.

2. Count the Clues

Check if your added letters match the length.

3. Function Words

Master small words like "th__" (the) or "wi____" (with).

Step 4 Recognition Drill

Identify the missing parts in these common B1 themes:

Narrative (Daily Life)

"Last week, I wen__ to the mar____ to buy some fre____ fruit."

Target: went, market, fresh

Descriptive (Travel)

"The hot____ was very comf________ and had a beautiful vi____ of the ocean."

Target: hotel, comfortable, view

Informative (Education)

"Teach____ often use techn______ to help stud______ learn more effect______."

Target: Teachers, technology, students, effectively

Step 5 Indonesian Context Bridge

In Indonesia, multinational companies in Jakarta or tech startups in Bandung require employees to read English "shorthand" or technical briefs quickly. Mastering this task builds the "pattern recognition" you need to skim a business email and understand the core meaning without getting stuck on every word. It ensures you don't lose the "big picture".

Step 8 Scaffolded Completion

"Computers are essential tools in modern life. Mo people u them for wo or education. Th allow u to find inform quickly. Without th, our lives wou be much mo difficult."

Thinking Prompts:

  • Mo____: Starts a sentence, describes "people". (Most vs More?)
  • inform____: Follows "find". Likely a long noun.
  • wou____: Often appears before "be" in conditional sentences.
