Step 3: The 'Strategy Fix-It' Drill

Mediocre Response:

"Global warming is getting worse because people use too much oil. If we don't change our habits, the planet will have big problems in the future. We need to use green energy now."

Task: Rewrite immediately using these Pro-Tips:
  • Pro-Tip 1: Start with a Perfect Participle Clause (e.g., "Having witnessed...").
  • Pro-Tip 2: Use 'precipitated by' instead of 'because.'
  • Pro-Tip 3: Replace 'big problems' with 'catastrophic ramifications.'
"Having witnessed the escalating severity of global warming, which is largely precipitated by excessive fossil fuel reliance, it is evident that inaction will lead to catastrophic ramifications. Consequently, an immediate transition to sustainable energy infrastructure is imperative."

Prompt:

"The meritocratic system in modern education is often criticized for reinforcing social inequality. Discuss."

Task 1: Follow the Skeleton
  • Lead with Fronting: "Of significant concern to contemporary sociologists is..."
  • Counter (Subjunctive): "Be that as it may, proponents argue..."
  • Syntactic Peak: "Under no circumstances..."
  • Conclusion: "Ultimately, the [Noun] of [Noun] necessitates..."
Task 2 (The Upgrade): Integrate these
  • "...perpetuates systemic disparities..."
  • "...the ostensible neutrality of..."
  • "...is inextricably linked to..."
"Of significant concern to contemporary sociologists is the extent to which the meritocratic system perpetuates systemic disparities. Be that as it may, proponents argue that the ostensible neutrality of standardized testing provides a fair baseline for all students. Under no circumstances, however, should we ignore that educational attainment is inextricably linked to socioeconomic privilege. Ultimately, the illusion of pure meritocracy necessitates a fundamental reevaluation of pedagogical assessment frameworks."
