Interactive Listening Strategy

Score Range: 80–110 | Focus: Social Cues & Logical Flow

Instructions: Master the "Listen and Respond" task. You are an active participant. Follow the steps to build your conversation skills.

Step 1 The Concept Breakdown

The Interactive Listening task is a simulation of a real-life conversation. You will read a situation description and then engage in a 5 to 6-turn dialogue. After each turn, you must select the most appropriate response.

Measures: Logical thread following, social cues, and formality levels.

Goal: Seek Guidance

Step 3 Strategic Roadmap

1. "Context is King"

Spend the first 10 seconds reading the prompt. Know who (formality) and why (objective).

2. "Paper Trail"

Re-read the last two turns if you get lost. Ensure your choice follows the logical flow.

3. Elimination Method

Ignore off-topic or rude options. Choose the one that directly answers the previous speaker.

Step 4 Warm-up: Match the Function

Match the "Conversation Function" with the correct "B1 Phrase".

Available Phrases:

  • A. "That sounds like a great plan, I'm in!"
  • B. "Could you explain what you meant by 'prerequisites'?"
  • C. "I see your point, but I'm worried about the deadline."
  • D. "What if we divided the presentation into three parts?"

Scenario: Group Project

"I've finished my part of the research, but I'm struggling to format the bibliography correctly. Do you know how to do it?" — Sarah (Classmate)

Thinking Prompts:

  • Is Sarah asking for help or giving information? (Asking for help)
  • What is the specific topic? (Bibliography formatting)
  • What would a helpful teammate say?

Select the Best Response:

Why it Works: Choice B directly addresses Sarah’s struggle and offers a relevant solution. A is just an opinion, and C is irrelevant to the formatting question.

Step 6: Technical Simulation Tips

  • 20-second timer: Give yourself only 20s to read options and click.
  • Voice Recording: Record yourself reading the completed dialogue aloud to hear the rhythm.
  • AI Prompting: Ask an AI to "Act as a university professor" and give you options.
