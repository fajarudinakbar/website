Interactive Speaking

Score Range: 120–135 | Focus: Dialogue & Summary

Instructions: Master the two-part speaking task: engage in a simulated academic dialogue, then summarize the interaction in 90 seconds.

Step 1 The Concept Breakdown

Part 1 (Listen & Respond): Select the best written response in a 5-6 turn dialogue.

Part 2 (Summarize): Speak for 75-90 seconds summarizing the problem, discussion, and resolution.

Step 2 Recognition Drill

Student-to-Student

Discussing a group project on marine biology. Partner suggests a broad topic.

Student-to-Professor

Visiting office hours to ask for a deadline extension due to an emergency.

Information Seeking

Asking a librarian how to access restricted digital archives for a thesis.

Step 3 Strategic Roadmap

1. Anchor to Scenario: The first correct response mirrors keywords in the opening prompt.
2. Active Listening: Focus on the last thing said to maintain flow. No replays allowed.
3. "Who, What, Outcome": Your summary must cover: Who you spoke to, the Problem, and the Resolution.

Step 4 Foundation Warm-up

Match the phrase to its conversational purpose.

Step 5 Guided Practice (Scaffolded)

Scenario:

You are talking to your professor about a low grade on your recent essay. Your goal is to be polite but firm about wanting to improve.

Professor:

"I've reviewed your paper. While your arguments were strong, the formatting didn't meet the rubric's requirements."

"I see. I apologize for the oversight. Could you point out the specific sections where the formatting was incorrect so I can fix it next time?"

Why: Acknowledges feedback (Politeness) + asks specific follow-up (Academic Goal).

Step 6: Technical Simulation

  • Timer: 4 minutes (Conversation) + 90 seconds (Summary).
  • Flow: Read scenario -> Speak summary immediately.
