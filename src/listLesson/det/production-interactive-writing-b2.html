Interactive Writing

Score Range: 100–125 | Focus: Narrative & Argumentation

Instructions: Master the two-part writing task. Write a coherent 5-minute response, then expand on it in a 3-minute follow-up.

Step 1 The Concept Breakdown

The Task: A 5-minute writing prompt followed immediately by a 3-minute related follow-up.

The Goal: "Conversation in Writing." Develop a theme and expand on ideas coherently.

Step 2 Recognition Drill

Narrative

"Talk about a time you had to learn a new skill quickly."

Argumentative

"To what extent do you agree that university education should be free?"

Descriptive

"Describe a major environmental issue in your country."

Step 3 Strategic Roadmap

1. Quantity & Quality

Aim for 100-130+ words in Part 1. Write fluently while maintaining control.

2. The "Anchor"

In Part 2, refer back to Part 1 ("As previously mentioned...") to show cohesion.

3. Syntactic Variety

Use compound/complex sentences. Avoid repetitive "I" starts. Use relative clauses.

Step 4 Foundation Warm-up: Transition Bridge

Rewrite the sentences into one complex sentence using the connector.

Step 5 Guided Practice (Scaffolded)

Prompt:

"Do you think it is better to work for a large company or a small business? Explain your preference."

Thinking Prompts:
  • Opening: State preference clearly.
  • Body: Give 2 reasons (stability vs freedom).
  • Prep: Anticipate the opposite view.

"In my view, beginning a career at a large corporation is significantly more advantageous for professional development. Firstly, large companies offer structured mentorship programs and a clear hierarchy, which provides a roadmap for advancement that small businesses often lack. Furthermore, the networking opportunities within a global firm are unparalleled, allowing employees to connect with experts across various departments. Although small businesses offer more flexibility, the stability and resources of a large organization create a more secure environment for long-term growth."

Why it Works: Advanced transitions (Furthermore, Although), Lexical range (unparalleled, hierarchy), Clear stance.

Step 6: Technical Simulation

  • Environment: No Spell-check. Plain text.
  • Timers: 5 mins (Part 1) + 3 mins (Part 2).
  • Tip: Keep typing! Fluency matters.
