Speak About the Photo

Score Range: 100–125 | Focus: Spatial Orientation & Descriptive Depth

Instructions: Master the 90-second photo description task. You must analyze the scene, predict contexts, and speak fluently. Auto-graded drills offer 2 attempts before revealing answers.

Step 1: Rapid-Fire Task Specs

Overview: Presented with an image, you have 20 seconds to prepare and must speak for 30–90 seconds describing the image to an academic audience. Impacts Speaking subscore.

Linguistic Targets (B2):

  • Present Continuous Tense: "The man is leaning..."
  • Prepositions of Place: "In the bottom-right corner," "Adjacent to..."
  • Relative Clauses: "The woman, who appears to be an architect, is..."
  • Speculative Modals: "She might be preparing for..."
  • Compound Adjectives: "Sun-drenched patio," "Densely-populated street"

Model B (Score: 115-125)

In the background, the stainless steel kitchen appears exceptionally organized, featuring various professional utensils hanging above the workspace. It seems as though he is finalizing a meal for a customer, as he is focusing intensely on the placement of the herbs."

Key Differences:
1. Lexical Depth: Replaced "putting greens" with "meticulously garnishing a gourmet dish".
2. Spatial Anchoring: Used "In the background" rather than just listing items.
3. Speculation: Used "It seems as though..." instead of the basic "I think...".

Step 3: Strategic Application Drill

Fix the Flawed Response:

"There are people. They walk on the street. It is a city. The buildings are tall. It is sunny."

Apply these Pro-Tips:

  • Action-Oriented Verbs: Replace "walk" with strolling, commuting, etc.
  • Spatial Anchoring: Start overview, move foreground to background.
  • Adjectival Depth: Use dual adjectives (e.g., "looming, glass-fronted").

Step 8: The Error-Correction Clinic

Identify and correct the 6 common pitfalls in this transcript.

"In this photo, there is a man. There is also a computer. He is work on the computer. I think he is happy because he smile. On the left side, there is a coffee. The room is very bright. It is a office. He is wearing a blue shirt. I can see a window too."

1. Repetitive simple sentences ("There is").
2. Grammar error ("is work" -> "is working").
3. Grammar error ("he smile" -> "he is smiling").
4. Missing quantifier ("a coffee" -> "a cup of coffee").
5. Article error ("a office" -> "an office").
6. Disjointed organization / lack of spatial flow (jumping from man to coffee to room to shirt).
