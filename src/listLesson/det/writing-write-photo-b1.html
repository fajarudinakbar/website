DET Write About the Photo

Target Score: 80-110 | Skill: Literacy & Production

Instructions: Master the B1 DET "Write About the Photo" task by moving beyond simple labeling. Focus on using present continuous tenses, relative clauses, and descriptive adjectives to connect ideas and boost your Production score.

Step 1: Scoring Mechanics & Triggers

The Scoring Needle: This task measures your ability to organize information logically, moving from a general overview of the subject to specific environmental details.

Grammatical Complexity: Production scores increase when you move beyond simple labeling to using present continuous tenses and relative clauses to connect ideas.

The Target 5:

  • Present Continuous Tense: (e.g., "The man is sitting").
  • Prepositional Phrases: (e.g., "in the background," "to the left").
  • Compound Sentences: (using "and," "but," or "so").
  • Descriptive Adjectives: (specifying color, size, or material).
  • Relative Clauses: (using "who" or "which" to add detail).

Step 2: The Comparison Lab

Prompt: A photo of a woman drinking coffee in a park.

Standard (Score 80-90)

"A woman is in the park. She is drinking coffee. She is wearing a blue shirt. The trees are green."

Advanced (Score 120+)

"This image depicts a woman who is sitting on a wooden bench in a sunny park while drinking a cup of coffee. She is wearing a bright blue shirt, and there are several tall green trees visible in the background."
