Score Impact: Boosts Literacy and Comprehension by testing how you link ideas between sentences.

  • Chronological Markers: (e.g., initially, subsequently).
  • Cause-Effect Logic: Identify the result of a stated action.

Step 2: The Audit (The "Bridge" Method)

The goal of this task is to find the "Bridge" that connects two sentences.

Sentence Before Gap: "The company decided to switch to renewable energy to reduce its carbon footprint."

The "Keyword Trap" Choice

"Renewable energy is becoming more popular for many companies today."

Why it fails: It repeats the keywords, but it is just a general statement. It doesn't "bridge" to a specific result or next step.

The "Logical Bridge" Choice

"This transition resulted in a 20% decrease in operational costs within the first year."

Why it works: "This transition" is the bridge—it refers specifically to the "switch" mentioned in the first sentence.
