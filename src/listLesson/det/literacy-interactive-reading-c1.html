Step 2: The Diagnostic 'Warm-Up'

Example 1 (Complete the Sentence): "The researcher's findings were initially met with skepticism; __________, subsequent peer-reviewed studies corroborated the data."

Options: (A) resultantly (B) however (C) furthermore.

Correct Answer: (B) however

Linguistic Analysis: The sentence requires a contrastive discourse marker. The first clause expresses a negative reception ("skepticism"), while the second clause expresses a positive validation ("corroborated"). "However" establishes this necessary contrast, whereas "resultantly" indicates causation and "furthermore" indicates addition.

Step 8: The Error-Correction Clinic

Identify and correct the "Cohesion Pitfalls" in this C1-level paragraph.

"The Industrial Revolution marked a turning point. Moreover, it led to urbanization. People moved to cities for work. This resulted in overcrowding. However, the government did nothing. Consequently, the standard of living for the poor remained low. In contrast, the middle class grew wealthy."

Correction Task:

The transitions above are repetitive or basic. Replace them with: Subsequently, Such mass migration, Notwithstanding this demographic shift, As a direct result, conversely.
