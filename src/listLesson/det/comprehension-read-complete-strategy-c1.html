Read and Complete

Score Range: 120–135 | Focus: Advanced Syntax & Vocabulary

Instructions: Use context and grammar to reconstruct the mutilated words. This is a C1-level "cloze" task requiring high precision. You have 2 attempts per exercise before the answer is revealed.

Step 1: The Concept Breakdown

The Task: You are presented with a short passage (70–140 words). The first and last sentences are intact. Middle sentences have unfinished words (first half provided).

The Goal: Contextual Precision. Use the "glue" of the language (syntax) and the theme to rebuild words.

Step 2: Recognition Drill

Identify these C1 word patterns:

Academic:

"The researcher conducted a comprehensive analysis of the genetic data."

Narrative:

"Despite the obstacles, she remained optimistic about the outcome of the project."

Business:

"The company intends to implement a more rigorous threshold for quality control."

Step 3: Strategic Roadmap

Context Sandwich

Read the first & last sentences FIRST. They provide the "field of play" (e.g., Physics vs. History).

PoS Forensics

Ask: "What kind of word is this?" After "the" -> Noun. Look for complex suffixes (-tion, -ity).

The "Repeat"

Stuck on "envi..."? Look elsewhere in the text; "environment" might be in the first sentence.

Step 4: Foundation Warm-up

Complete these common C1-level academic words:

Further... (Adverb)
Signific... (Adjective)
Neverthe... (Transition)
Consid... (Verb)

Scaffolded Passage

"The impact of urbanization on local ecosystems is a growing concern for biologists. Many spec of bi are find it increas diffi to thri in ur environments. This phenome is primar caused b the destruct of natu habitats."

Thinking Prompts:

  • Spec... of bi...: Plural noun for animals + plural noun for category (birds).
  • increas... diffi...: Adverb + Adjective pairing.
  • phenome...: Singular noun describing a situation.
