INTERNAL MEMO

To: HR Department From: Siti, Recruitment Officer Date: January 8, 2026 Subject: Planning for next week’s Recruitment Meeting

Hi Team,

I am currently planning our meeting to discuss the five new positions we need to fill. First, I need to confirm the venue. I tried to book "Meeting Room B," but it is busy. So, I have moved the meeting to the Lobby Conference Room.

Next, please check the attendee list. I have invited Pak Hartono, Budi from Payroll, and the training team. If you cannot attend, please tell me by Friday so I can reschedule if necessary.

Finally, I am attaching the agenda. We will talk about:

  • Reviewing CVs for the new IT Assistant.
  • Dates for the next interviews.
  • New employee orientation dates.

Please bring your laptops. Lestari will take the minutes for this meeting. Thank you!
