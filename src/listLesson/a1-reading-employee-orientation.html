From: Sari (HR Manager)

To: All New Staff

Subject: Invitation: New Employee Orientation Day

Welcome to PT. Global Solusi. We are happy you are joining our team!

Next week is your Orientation Day. This training is mandatory (you must attend). Here is the important information:

1. Time and Location

  • Date: Monday, August 5th.
  • Time: We start at 09:00 AM sharp. Please arrive 10 minutes early.
  • Location: Training Room A (2nd Floor). Please report to the Receptionist when you arrive. She will give you a visitor pass.

2. The Schedule

In the morning, we will talk about company rules and office safety. At 12:00 PM, we provide a free lunch box (Chicken or Fish).

In the afternoon, you will meet your new manager. Finally, at 3:00 PM, you will sign your employment contract.

3. What to Bring

Please bring these documents with you:

  • Your original ID Card (KTP).
  • Your Bank Account book (so we can pay your salary).
  • A red background photo (4x6) for your ID Badge.

See you on Monday!

Sari
