  • International students often struggle with confusing portal interfaces, strict formatting rules for document uploads, and understanding complex eligibility language.
  • A symptom checker prevents highly contagious students from coming to a busy clinic unnecessarily, protecting the campus community.

Task 9 Case Study Decision Making

Scenario:

The university portal crashes two hours before the scholarship deadline. You have all your documents ready, but you cannot submit them.

Option A: Wait for the portal to work again, even if it is after the deadline, and hope the university understands.
Option B: Email the admissions office immediately with all your documents attached and a screenshot of the portal error.

Discuss which option demonstrates better Strategic Thinking for a professional.

Analysis:

Option B demonstrates clear professional accountability and problem solving. By taking a screenshot and sending the files before the deadline, you prove you completed the work on time despite the technical failure. Waiting silently (Option A) risks missing the opportunity completely, as administration cannot verify when you finished the work.
