Task 1 Warm-Up Speaking

Do you usually work or rest during holidays? What do people do on holidays in your country?

Sample Answer: "I usually rest during holidays. In my country, people visit family, go to the beach, or cook special food together. It is very common for shops to stay open, but offices usually close."

Task 2 Vocabulary Preparation

Working During the Holiday

For many people, holidays are a time to rest, travel, or spend time with family. However, some people still need to work during holidays. This is common in jobs such as healthcare, tourism, retail, and customer service.

Some people work during holidays because their company needs staff every day. Hospitals, hotels, and airports cannot close, even on public holidays. Other people choose to work because they can earn extra money. In some countries, workers get higher pay if they work on holidays.

Working during a holiday can be difficult. People may feel tired or sad because they cannot join family celebrations. It can also be stressful when many customers travel or shop at the same time. However, there are also positive sides. Some workers enjoy quieter workdays, and others can take time off on another day instead.

To manage holiday work better, good planning is important. Employers should share schedules early and allow workers to choose shifts when possible. Workers should also take care of their health and rest well before and after work. With the right support, working during holidays can be less stressful.

Task 3 General Understanding

  • Why: Companies need staff (hospitals, airports) or workers want extra money.
  • Jobs: Healthcare, tourism, retail, customer service.
  • Positive: Quieter workdays, or taking time off on another day.

Task 8 Case 1: The Busy Shop

Shop is busy, only 2 workers. What problem? What solution?

Solution: The manager should hire part-time staff or come to the shop to help. Workers can share tasks (one person at the register, one helping customers).

Task 9 Case 2: Family or Work?

Choose between work or family event. Good/Bad sides? What would you do?

Choice Work: Good (money), Bad (miss family).
Choice Family: Good (happy memories), Bad (lose money/job risk).
Idea: Ask to work a half-shift so you can do both.

Task 10 Case 3: Fair Scheduling

Some always work holidays, others never do. Is this fair? What rule to make?

Fairness: Not fair. Everyone should share the hard shifts.
Rule: "If you work Christmas this year, you get New Year off," or "Rotate holiday shifts every year."

Task 11 Final Speaking/Writing

Say 2 reasons people work, 1 problem + solution, and your opinion.

Sample Summary: People work on holidays because companies like hotels cannot close, or because they want extra money. One problem is feeling sad about missing family. A solution is to plan schedules early. I think working on holidays is okay if the pay is good.
