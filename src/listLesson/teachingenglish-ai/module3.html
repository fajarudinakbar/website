Building Your Custom AI Teaching Assistant

Instructions: In Module 1 & 2, you controlled AI manually. Now, you will scale your expertise. Complete the Preparation, learn about System Instructions, and build your own Gemini Gem.

Task 1 Reflection

How often do you find yourself writing the same instructions to AI? (e.g., "Don't use complex words," "Include an answer key"). Why is this inefficient?

Insight: Repeating instructions is time-consuming and inconsistent. A Custom Agent (Gem) stores these rules once, allowing you to focus on the content rather than the formatting every single time.

Task 2 Key Terminology

Section 1. Why Build a Custom Agent?

Most teachers repeatedly rewrite the same instructions: "Use communicative tasks," "Keep vocabulary level appropriate," "Include interaction."

A custom AI agent (Gem) stores these principles in its system instructions. You are externalizing your pedagogical knowledge into a reusable design system. This turns AI from a chatbot into a specialized instructional designer.

Section 2. What Is a Gem?

In Gemini, a Gem is a custom version of the AI that follows permanent instructions you define.

Instead of prompting from scratch, you design a "Worksheet Architect" that:

  • Understands GSE alignment
  • Follows your task structure automatically
  • Uses communicative methodology
  • Maintains level control

Section 3. The Pedagogical System Instructions

Your Gem must include these five core rules:

1. Pedagogical Role

"You are an ELT instructional designer specializing in communicative, task-based learning."

2. Standards Alignment Rule

"All materials must be aligned to a provided GSE Can-Do statement."

3. Output Structure Rule

"Follow this format: Warm-up → Input → Guided task → Communicative task → Reflection."

4. Language Control Rule

"Grammar and vocabulary must match the performance described in the Can-Do statement."

5. Interaction Rule

"Avoid isolated drills. Tasks must involve meaning-focused communication."

Section 4. How to Create Your Gem

  1. Go to Gemini Gems.
  2. Click "Create a Gem".
  3. Name it (e.g., "Worksheet Architect").
  4. Paste your System Instructions.
  5. Save.

You now have a reusable AI teaching assistant.

Section 5. Using Your Gem

Once built, you only need to provide the variable information:

User Prompt:

Learning Objective: Learners can explain the purpose of a meeting.

Context: Business English, team meeting.

Output: 1-page speaking lesson.

The Gem automatically applies all your structural and pedagogical rules to this simple request.
