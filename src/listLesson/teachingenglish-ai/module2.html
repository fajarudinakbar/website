Multimodal Scenario Design with AI

Topic: Creating Performance-Based Listening & Visuals

Instructions: In Module 1, you learned to control text. In this module, you extend that control to listening and scenario design. Complete the Preparation, study the Scenario Pack Structure, and build your own audio/visual lesson assets.

Task 1 Reflection

When you need a listening track for a specific topic (e.g., "Job Interviews"), do you usually find an existing one or create it? What is the challenge with existing materials?

Insight: Existing materials often don't match the exact proficiency level or specific performance goal you are teaching. By generating your own scripts and audio, you ensure 100% alignment with the Can-Do descriptor.

Task 2 Key Terminology

Section 1. From GSE Descriptor to Listening Input

Listening materials should begin with a performance goal, not a topic.

Example Can-Do: "Can describe past work experience in a job interview." This implies:

  • Professional interview setting
  • Past tense narration
  • Work-related vocabulary
  • Question–answer interaction

Section 2. Creating the Script with a GSE-Embedded Prompt

Use a text-based AI tool (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude) to generate the script. The key is to embed the GSE objective directly into the prompt.

Model Prompt:

System Role: You are an ELT materials designer aligned with the Global Scale of English.

Learning Objective: Learners can describe past work experience in a job interview.

Language Scope: Past simple, sequencing expressions, work project vocabulary.

Context: Formal job interview in a company office.

Output: 8-line dialogue between interviewer and candidate.

Section 3. Turning the Script into Audio

Once the script is ready, move to Google AI Studio. Use its text-to-speech function to convert the script into natural spoken audio.

Section 4. Generating Visual Context

In the same tool, generate an image representing the communication environment to anchor the situation.

"Professional job interview in a modern office. Candidate and interviewer seated across a desk, laptop and notepad visible, formal but calm atmosphere."

Section 5. The Scenario Pack Structure

Every listening lesson you design should include:

  1. GSE Performance Anchor
  2. Listening Script (generated with GSE-embedded prompt)
  3. Audio file (from Google AI Studio)
  4. Visual scene (from Google AI Studio)
  5. Learner task (requiring the Can-Do performance)
