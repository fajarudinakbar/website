Task 2 Key Concepts

Section 1. Why Performance Targets Matter in AI Prompting

When teachers ask AI to "create B1 materials," AI interprets broad level labels and fills in with guesswork. This can result in materials that are too easy, too hard, or not aligned with learners’ real communicative needs.

The GSE consists of detailed performance targets called Can-Do descriptors that describe what learners should be able to do with language. Using these descriptors as constraints makes your AI prompts specific, measurable, and pedagogically sound.

Core Principle: This is the heart of constraint-based prompt engineering — a method that replaces vague prompts with precise, performance-driven instructions for the AI.

Section 2. What is the GSE Teacher Toolkit?

The GSE Teacher Toolkit is a free interactive online database that helps educators find level-appropriate learning objectives, grammar targets, vocabulary lists, and more. It integrates the GSE and CEFR frameworks so users can search by skill type, learner type, topic, or proficiency level.

  • Locate specific Can-Do descriptors that match your instructional goal
  • Find vocabulary and grammar aligned to those descriptors
  • Audit or plan curriculum units based on detailed goals
  • Identify potential gaps in current lesson plans

Task 7 Build Your Constraint-Based Prompt

Combine your Toolkit findings into a full constraint prompt.

System Role: You are an ELT instructional designer using the Global Scale of English. Target Performance: Learners can [exact Can-Do descriptor]. Grammar Target: [List grammar structures from Toolkit] Vocabulary Target: [List word sets] Context: [Describe real-world context] Output: [Task type] Support Level: [Level of scaffolding]

Task 9 Output Evaluation

Run your prompt in your chosen AI tool. Check the results against these questions:

Does the task require the target performance?
Are grammar and vocabulary aligned with descriptors?
Is the communication context authentic and specific?
If the answer to any of these is "No", refine your prompt constraints. This iteration is professional practice.
