Task 2: Vocabulary in Context

Mastering the Art of Customer Service

In the modern business environment, handling customer complaints is an ESSENTIAL skill. Professional representatives use a specific strategy to turn a negative situation into a positive one. The first step is to listen without interrupting. This shows that you value the customer's perspective.

Effective communication requires EMPATHY. This means understanding how the customer feels. After listening, the representative must apologize sincerely, focusing on the INCONVENIENCE caused to the customer. The next stage is to find a RESOLUTION. Instead of saying "I can't do that," professional speakers offer alternatives. If a problem is too complex, the staff might need to ESCALATE the issue to a manager.

Finally, following up is vital. A quick email or phone call after the problem is solved shows that the company cares about long-term satisfaction.
