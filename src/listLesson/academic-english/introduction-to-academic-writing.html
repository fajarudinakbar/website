Part 2: What Is Academic Writing?

Academic writing is formal writing used in schools, universities, journals, and reports. It is clear, organised, and based on ideas and information. The writer gives reasons and examples, not opinions without support.

Task 2. True or False.

1. Academic writing is usually informal.
2. Academic writing needs clear organisation.
3. Academic writing can include personal stories only.

Part 3: The Purpose

According to UEfAP, the purpose is to answer a question, explain an idea, or present an argument.

Part 6: Analysis

A "I think school uniforms are boring and I do not like them."
B "School uniforms can improve discipline and reduce social pressure among students."

Task 6. Which sentence is more academic? Why?

Model Answer: Sentence B is more academic because it gives reasons (discipline, social pressure) and uses objective, formal language instead of personal feelings ("I think", "boring").

Part 7: Features

Common Features: Clear purpose, Formal language, Organized structure, Examples and reasons, No unnecessary digressions.

Task 7. Identify the feature being used.

"Students need enough sleep because it improves concentration."

Suggested: Gives a reason / Examples and reasons

"In this essay, I will explain why exercise is important."

Suggested: Clear purpose / States purpose

"Many young people use phones for study and communication."

Suggested: Formal language / Gives information
