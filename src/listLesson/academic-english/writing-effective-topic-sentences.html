Part 1: Reviewing Paragraph Purpose

Task 1. Choose the correct statement.

Part 2: Understanding Topic Sentences

A topic sentence introduces the main idea of a paragraph and signals what the paragraph will discuss.

Paragraph A

[1]Employee training programmes are essential for organisational development. [2]Training helps employees improve their skills and adapt to changing job requirements. [3]Many companies invest in professional development to remain competitive. [4]For example, regular training can increase efficiency and reduce errors. [5]As a result, training supports both employee growth and organisational success.

Task 2. Identify the topic sentence.

Production Tasks

Task 7. Controlled Writing Practice

Write a topic sentence for: The impact of leadership on employee motivation.

Sample Answer: "Leadership practices have a strong influence on employee motivation in organisations."

Task 8. Developing Support

Add two supporting sentences and one example to the topic sentence you wrote in Task 7.

Sample Answer: "Effective leaders communicate clear goals and provide regular feedback. This support helps employees feel valued and engaged. For example, managers who recognise employee achievements often increase motivation."

Task 9. Writing a Full Body Paragraph

Combine your work from Tasks 7 and 8 to write a complete body paragraph (4–5 sentences) on leadership and employee motivation.

Sample Paragraph: "Leadership practices have a strong influence on employee motivation in organisations. Effective leaders communicate clear goals and provide regular feedback to their teams. This support helps employees feel valued and engaged in their work. For example, managers who recognise employee achievements often improve motivation and performance."

Task 10. Reflection

How does a clear topic sentence help the reader understand a paragraph?

Sample Answer: "A clear topic sentence tells the reader what the paragraph is about and prepares them for the supporting details."
