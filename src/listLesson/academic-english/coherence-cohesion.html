Coherence and Cohesion

Level B2 | Lesson 5: Coherence and Cohesion in Academic Writing

Part 1: Understanding Coherence

Coherence refers to how ideas are logically organised and easy to follow in a paragraph or text.

Task 1. Choose the best definition of coherence.

Part 2: Understanding Cohesion

Cohesion refers to the language tools used to connect sentences and ideas (linking words, reference words, repetition).

Task 2. Decide if each example shows Coherence (CO) or Cohesion (CE).

1. Ideas move from cause to effect in a clear order.
2. The word “this” refers to the previous sentence.
3. The paragraph focuses on one main idea.

Part 3: Analysing Cohesion

Paragraph A

"Employee training improves workplace performance. Well-trained employees can complete tasks more efficiently and accurately. As a result, organisations benefit from higher productivity. This improvement also increases employee confidence. Therefore, training programmes play an important role in organisational success."

Task 3. Identify three cohesive devices used in the paragraph.

Sample Answer: as a result, this, therefore

Part 4: Identifying Logical Flow

Paragraph B

"Customer satisfaction affects business success. Satisfied customers are more likely to return and recommend a company. For example, positive service experiences increase customer loyalty. However, many organisations fail to invest in customer service training. This lack of investment can damage long-term relationships with customers."

Task 4. Identify the function of the words in the text.

1. Which word shows contrast?

2. Which word shows example?

Part 5: Reordering for Coherence

Put the sentences in the correct order to form a logical paragraph.

A. As a result, managers must develop new communication strategies.
B. Remote work has become common in many organisations.
C. Digital tools now allow employees to work from different locations.
D. These changes have affected how teams collaborate.

Task 5. Assign the order (1st, 2nd, 3rd, 4th).

Part 6: Improving Weak Cohesion

Weak Flow:

"Leadership affects employee performance. Leadership styles are different. Leadership styles affect motivation. Motivation affects productivity."

Task 6. Rewrite the paragraph using cohesive devices to improve flow.

Sample Answer: "Leadership affects employee performance because different leadership styles influence motivation. Higher motivation often leads to increased productivity."

Task 8. Linking Paragraphs

Paragraph 1 topic: Causes of employee stress

Paragraph 2 topic: Effects of employee stress

Write one sentence that links Paragraph 1 to Paragraph 2.

Sample Answer: "These causes lead to several negative effects on employees and organisations."

Task 9. Writing a Coherent Paragraph

Write a paragraph (4–5 sentences) on: The benefits of effective communication in management.

Requirement: Use at least three cohesive devices.

Sample Paragraph: "Effective communication is essential in management. Clear instructions help employees understand their roles and responsibilities. As a result, tasks are completed more efficiently. For example, regular meetings can reduce misunderstandings. Therefore, strong communication practices improve overall organisational performance."

Task 10. Reflection

Why are coherence and cohesion important for the reader in academic writing?

Sample Answer: "Coherence and cohesion help the reader follow ideas easily and understand how sentences and paragraphs are connected."
