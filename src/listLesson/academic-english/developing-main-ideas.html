Developing Main Ideas

Level B2 | Lesson 3: Developing Clear Main Ideas and Purpose Statements

Part 1: Reviewing the Role of the Main Idea

In academic writing, the main idea tells the reader what the discussion focuses on. It limits the topic and guides the development of body paragraphs.

Task 1. Choose the best description of a main idea.

Part 2: Main Idea vs Topic

A topic is broad. A main idea is more focused.

Task 2. Decide whether each item is a topic (T) or a main idea (M).

1. Leadership in organisations
2. Leadership styles influence employee motivation in different ways
3. Digital marketing
4. Digital marketing offers cost-effective opportunities for small businesses

Part 3: Analysing Main Ideas in Context

Paragraph A

[1]Businesses operate in competitive global markets. [2]Many organisations seek ways to improve productivity and employee performance. [3]As a result, leadership practices have become a key area of interest in management research. [4]Different leadership styles can influence how employees respond to organisational goals. [5]Leadership approaches play a significant role in shaping employee motivation and performance in modern organisations.

Task 3. Identify the sentence that functions as the main idea.
