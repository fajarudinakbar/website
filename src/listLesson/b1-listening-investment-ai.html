Video Summary

Recently, investors have become nervous about the high cost of Artificial Intelligence (AI). Big companies are spending jaw-dropping amounts of money, and many people worry they won’t see a profit soon. This makes the stock market wobble as investors get jittery.

History shows this often happens with new technology. During the internet boom, people were bidding up share prices too high. When the market eventually crashed, many investors lost money. However, those who held their stocks for a long time eventually made a profit.

Traditional safety options like bonds and gold have been unstable recently. Gold prices have swung wildly, making it hard to trust as a "safe haven." Experts suggest that the best strategy is to buy reliable stocks and hold them even through the depths of a crash. Investing slowly and steadily for a long time is usually the safest path.
