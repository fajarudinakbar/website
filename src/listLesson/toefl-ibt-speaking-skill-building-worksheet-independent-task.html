TOEFL iBT Speaking Skill-Building Worksheet

Task Type: Independent Speaking Task

Skill Focus: Giving Clear Opinions + Supporting with Reasons & Examples

Step 1: Understand the Structure of a Strong Response

A strong response usually includes:

  • A clear opinion (I agree… / I disagree… / I prefer…)
  • Reason 1 + explanation
  • Example 1 (specific details)
  • Reason 2 + explanation
  • Example 2 (specific details)
“I agree with the idea of giving children homework on a daily basis. I feel this way for two reasons. First of all, I think that it helps children retain what they learn for a longer period of time. Kids absorb a lesson better when they review it at home. Secondly, homework gives children an opportunity to bond with their parents. For example, when I was in school, I used to do my homework with my parents, and this helped us become very close.”

Step 2: Identify the Components

Read the short model response. Label each part correctly.

1. "I prefer studying in the morning. I feel this way for two reasons."

2. "First, I can concentrate better because my house is quiet at that time."

3. "For example, I usually wake up early and read my notes before breakfast, and I get a lot done."

4. "Second, studying in the morning gives me more free time later in the day."

5. "For instance, after school I can meet my friends or play sports because I finished all my studying earlier."

Step 3: Build Your Reason-Example Skills

Match each reason with the appropriate example.

Reasons:

2. I prefer group projects because they help me learn new ideas.

Examples:

A. My classmates often explain concepts in a different way, and that helps me understand them.

B. I don’t have to travel to campus, so I can use that time to finish my assignments.

Step 4: Controlled Practice

Write a short example for each reason. Use phrases like: “For example, last year I…,” “For instance, when I read…”

Step 5: Independent Speaking Practice

Speaking Question

"Some students prefer taking classes with a strict teacher who gives a lot of assignments. Others prefer a relaxed teacher who gives fewer tasks. Which do you prefer? Explain why."

Goal: Include Clear Opinion + Reason 1/Example 1 + Reason 2/Example 2.
