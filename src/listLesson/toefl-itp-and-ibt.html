TOEFL ITP vs Updated iBT

A comprehensive comparison between the Institutional Testing Program and the Internet-Based Test.

Executive Summary

Hover over the cards to reveal the comparison

🎯

Test Purpose

Placement vs. Mobility

TOEFL ITP

Institutional use. Placement, progress, and internal scholarships.

Updated iBT

Global admissions, professional licensing, and migration.

💻

Format

Linear vs. Adaptive

TOEFL ITP

Paper or Computer. Linear structure (Fixed sections).

Updated iBT

Computer (Center/Home). Multistage Adaptive Reading & Listening.

🧠

Skill Focus

Passive vs. Active

TOEFL ITP

Receptive skills + Grammar. No Speaking or Writing output.

Updated iBT

Communicative competence. Integrated 4 skills tasks.

⏱️

Duration

Total Test Time

TOEFL ITP

Approx. 115 Minutes.
Fixed time.

Updated iBT

Under 2 Hours.
Streamlined & Adaptive.
