The Hook: Identifying Strong Posts

A good academic discussion post states an opinion clearly and supports it with a reason. Read the two posts below.

Noticing Activity

Post A

I think online classes are bad. I do not like them at all. Traditional classes are much better.

Post B

I agree that online classes can be difficult, but I prefer them. In my opinion, they are better because they save travel time and offer more flexibility.

Question: Why is Post B better for a university discussion?

Answer & Insight

Correct: A
Post B uses an opinion phrase ("In my opinion") and supports the belief with a specific reason ("because they save travel time"). It also politely acknowledges the other side ("I agree that...").

Strategy: Opinion Phrase Construction

The "Half-Word" Rule: Complete the missing sections to practice spelling standard discussion phrases. If a word has an even number of letters, the first half is given. If odd, the first part given is longer.

"In my opin, group work is bet beca you can sha ideas."

Think-Aloud Logic:

  • opin___: A belief or view about something. Likely opinion.
  • bet___: A comparative adjective for "good". Likely better.
  • beca___: A conjunction used to introduce a reason. Likely because.
  • sha__: A verb meaning to give a portion of something to others. Likely share.
