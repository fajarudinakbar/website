Task 1 Warm-Up Thinking

  • Why is a first impression important in a medical research environment?
  • How do you usually balance talking about your past experience versus your future goals?
  • In your culture, is it more common to be humble or assertive when introducing yourself to a new team?
Sample Ideas: A first impression establishes credibility and trust. Balancing past experience shows capability, while future goals show ambition. Cultural norms vary greatly; for example, Japanese culture often values humility, while Western contexts might expect more assertiveness.
