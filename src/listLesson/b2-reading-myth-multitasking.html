Strategy: Solving the C-Test

The "Half-Word" Rule: If a word has an even number of letters (e.g., TIME), the first half is given (TI) and you provide the second half (ME). If odd (e.g., PHONE), the first part given is longer (PHO) and you provide the rest (NE).

"Your bra cann foc on t tas at on."

Think-Aloud Logic:

  • bra__: Related to thinking and cognitive load. Likely brain.
  • cann__: A negative modal verb. Likely cannot.
  • foc__: An action the brain does when concentrating. Likely focus.
  • t_: A number indicating more than one task. Likely two.
  • tas__: Things you have to do or complete. Likely tasks.
  • on__: Part of the phrase "at ____" meaning simultaneously. Likely once.
