Conditionals in Economics

Use the 1st conditional for real future possibilities, and the 2nd conditional to brainstorm hypothetical risk plans.

First Conditional (Likely):
If the exchange rate reaches Rp 18,500, import costs will rise significantly.

Second Conditional (Hypothetical):
If we hedged our currency contracts, our profit margins would remain fully stable.

VOCABULARY TOOLKIT

Cause & Effect Phrasals

Replace generic verbs with these advanced structures to explain market crashes fluently:

  • stem from / arise from: The crisis stems from Middle East tension.
  • put pressure on: Expensive global oil puts immense pressure on rupiah.
  • act as a safe-haven: Investors choose US Dollars to act as a safe-haven.
  • stabilize around: The rate managed to stabilize around Rp 17,900.
