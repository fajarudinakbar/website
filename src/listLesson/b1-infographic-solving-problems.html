GRAMMAR TOOLKIT

The Past Perfect

Use this tense to show that one action happened before another action in the past.

Structure: had + past participle (V3)

Example: "I arrived at the interview, but I realized I had forgotten my suit at home."
PHRASE TOOLKIT

Handling Daily Issues

Use these polite phrases when you are in a difficult situation and need assistance.

  • I am really stuck right now.
  • I am in a bind.
  • Would you do me a huge favor?
  • Could you possibly help me with...?
  • Excuse me, this is not what I ordered.
