How We See Each Other

Perceptions and Bias in the Workplace

Course: Business English Issues Level: Intermediate (B1-B2)

Today's Objectives

By the end of this lesson, you will be able to:

  • Reflect on relationships with managers.
  • Define and use key vocabulary for feedback and perception.
  • Read and analyze an article on gender bias in management.
  • Listen to and understand different perspectives on managers.
  • Formulate and express your opinions on workplace expectations.

Speaking Practice (Part 1)

Prepare Your Answers

Prepare your own answers to the following questions. Be ready to share with the class.

  • What is your opinion on the article? Do you think it's true for you? Why/why not?
  • Have you had experience with male and female managers? What were your experiences like?
  • Which of the speakers (1, 2, or 3) did you agree or disagree with most? Why?

Speaking Practice (Part 2)

Continue Preparing

Continue preparing your answers to these questions.

  • How do you react if you get criticism about your work?
  • What do you think your manager's perception is of you and your work?
  • What are your expectations of a good manager?

Extended Activity / Homework

Individual Task

Imagine you are your manager. Write feedback about your own work from your manager's point of view (at least 150 words).

Consider these questions:

  • What areas of your work are you doing well with?
  • What criticism would they give about your work?
  • Is there any training you need? What would your manager suggest?

Lesson Review

Final Reflection

Think about these final questions. Be prepared to share one final thought.

  • What was the most surprising fact from today's article?
  • What is the difference between 'criticism' and 'feedback' in your own words?
  • What is one expectation you will have for your next manager?
