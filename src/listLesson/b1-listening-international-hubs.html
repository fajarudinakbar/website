Track 01: The Airport Express Announcement

"Attention, passengers. This is an urgent travel update for the Haruka Limited Express service to Kyoto Medical Center and Kyoto Station. Due to unexpected track maintenance between Osaka and Shin-Osaka, there has been a platform ALTERATION. The 10:15 service, originally DEPARTING from Platform 4, will now PROCEED from Platform 7. Please use the stairs or the elevator ADJACENT to the Information Desk to reach Platform 7. We apologize for the inconvenience and the IMMEDIATE change. Please check your tickets to ensure you are boarding the DESIGNATED car for reserved seating. For passengers heading to the International Medical Conference, a shuttle bus remains available at the West Exit as a secondary option."

Task 3: General Understanding (Gist)

  • What is the primary purpose of this announcement?
  • Where is the train service heading?
  • What is the reason for the platform change?
  • Purpose: To inform passengers about an urgent platform change for the 10:15 service.
  • Destination: Kyoto Medical Center and Kyoto Station.
  • Reason: Unexpected track maintenance between Osaka and Shin-Osaka.

Task 4: Detail and Meaning

  • What was the original platform for the 10:15 service?
  • Where exactly is the elevator located?
  • What alternative transport is available at the West Exit?
  • Original Platform: Platform 4.
  • Elevator Location: Adjacent (next to) the Information Desk.
  • Alternative Transport: A shuttle bus for passengers heading to the International Medical Conference.

Task 9: Case Study – Decision Making

Scenario:

You arrive at Platform 7, but the train is delayed by another 20 minutes. Your presentation at the Kyoto Medical Center starts in 50 minutes.

  • Option A: Wait for the train, which goes directly to the Medical Center station.
  • Option B: Go to the West Exit and take the conference shuttle bus, which is leaving now but faces city traffic.

Discuss the pros and cons of each with your partner, then document your choice.

Analysis: Train delays are generally predictable once a new time is set, and rail bypasses traffic (Option A). However, a shuttle bus guarantees immediate movement, but is subject to unpredictable road conditions (Option B). The professional choice depends on knowing the city's traffic reputation.
