Instructions for Candidates:

  • This simulation is adapted from a paper test. You must listen to the audio carefully and progress through the questions according to the audio pace.
  • Ensure your headset is plugged in and the volume is comfortable.
  • Once you click "Start Simulation", the exam timer will begin immediately.
  • The official test audio will commence automatically on the General Directions page.
  • Do not use the browser's back or forward buttons during the simulation.

Listening Test

In the Listening test, you will be asked to demonstrate how well you understand spoken English. The entire Listening test will last approximately 45 minutes. There are four parts, and directions are given for each part. You must mark your answers on the digital interface.

Test Structure Overview

  • Part 1: Photographs (Questions 1 - 6)
  • Part 2: Question-Response (Questions 7 - 31)
  • Part 3: Conversations (Questions 32 - 70)
  • Part 4: Talks (Questions 71 - 100)
