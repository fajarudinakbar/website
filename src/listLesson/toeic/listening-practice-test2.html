TOEIC® Listening Practice Test 2 (TTS Edition)

Instructions for Candidates:

This practice test utilizes advanced Text-to-Speech technology. You must use Microsoft Edge browser to support the Web Speech API.

  • Ensure your headset is plugged in and the volume is comfortable.
  • The audio will play automatically. You can use the Play/Pause button if you need to stop.
  • You can adjust the playback speed in the top header.
  • Once you click "Start Simulation", the exam timer will begin immediately.

Listening Test

In the Listening test, you will be asked to demonstrate how well you understand spoken English. The entire Listening test will last approximately 45 minutes. There are four parts, and directions are given for each part. You must mark your answers on the screen.

Audio Controls Notice

Audio will play automatically when you enter a new section or navigate to a new question.
