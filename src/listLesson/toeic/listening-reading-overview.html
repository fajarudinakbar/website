Abilities Measured

The TOEIC Listening and Reading test evaluates critical English skills required for effective professional communication.

Listening Skills

  • Inferring gist, purpose, and basic context
  • Understanding specific details in spoken texts
  • Grasping a speaker's implied meaning (pragmatic understanding)

Reading Skills

  • Locating specific information in tables and passages
  • Connecting information across multiple texts
  • Making inferences based on written text
  • Understanding workplace vocabulary and grammar

Questions are drawn from a diverse range of real world settings, making the test a highly practical measure of proficiency.
