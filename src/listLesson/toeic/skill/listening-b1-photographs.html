Step 1 Skill Diagnostic

When looking at a Part 1 photograph before the audio plays, what are the top 3 elements you should immediately identify in your mind?

Diagnostic Baseline:
  • The Subject(s): Who or what is the main focus? (Man, woman, vehicles, nature).
  • The Action: What are the main subjects actively doing? (Examine the verbs: carrying, writing, leaning).
  • The Location/State: Where are things placed? (Examine the prepositions: next to, under, stacked on).

Step 2 Lexical Match (10 Items)

Step 3 Pattern Identification

A frequent trap in Part 1 is the "Wrong Action" distractor. If a photo shows a man standing next to a bicycle reading a map, why is the option "The man is riding a bicycle" incorrect, even though the noun "bicycle" is perfectly accurate?

Pattern Analysis:

Test makers often use the correct noun (bicycle) but pair it with a verb that is not happening (riding). Your eyes naturally gravitate toward the prominent object, but you must ensure the verb matches the present continuous action of the subject.

Answer Key & Explanations:
  • Photo 1 Correct Answer: (A) She is typing on a keyboard.

    Explanation: The woman's hands are visibly positioned on the laptop keyboard, actively typing. Option (B) "examining a document" is now a trap because no paper document is the primary focus.

  • Photo 2 Correct Answer: (C) One of the men is pointing at a plan.

    Explanation: The action matches the visual of the meeting where a man is drawing attention to the document/plan. Option (B) "taking off their hats" is a trap as they are indoors and not wearing hats.

Step 9 The "Wrong-Option" Analysis (5 Items)

Look at the distractors from Step 6. Why are these options incorrect traps? Label them as "Wrong Action", "Wrong State", or "Out of Context".

1. (Q1) Distractor: "She is typing on a keyboard."
2. (Q2) Distractor: "They are taking off their hats."
3. (Q3) Distractor: "He is paying for groceries."
4. (Q4) Distractor: "A waiter is serving food."
5. (Q5) Distractor: "The boat is sailing on the ocean."
Analysis Key:
  • Wrong Action: The laptop is present, but she is holding a pen, not actively typing.
  • Wrong Action/Opposite: They are currently wearing the hats, not actively removing them.
  • Out of Context/Future: He is pushing the cart, not at a register paying yet.
  • Out of Context/Ghost Noun: No people or waiters are visible in a description of stacked chairs.
  • Wrong State: The boat is docked (stationary), not actively sailing.

Step 10 Cumulative Speed Drill (5 Items)

Match the photograph to the correct grammatical observation or trap identification.

Q1. Look at the photo. Which statement contains a trap?
(A) "People are attending a meeting." (B) "Chairs are tucked under the desk."
Q2. Look at the photo. Which statement is correct?
(A) "She is cleaning a window." (B) "She is adjusting her eyewear."
Q3. Look at the photo. Which statement is correct?
(A) "He is opening a box." (B) "He is piling up some cartons."
Q4. Look at the photo. Which statement is correct?
(A) "Vehicles are parked side-by-side." (B) "Traffic is heavily congested."
Q5. Look at the photo. Which statement is correct?
(A) "A plant is resting on the ledge." (B) "Someone is watering a plant."
Answer Key:
  • Q1: (B) The room is empty; "people" is a ghost noun trap.
  • Q2: (B) "Wearing" describes a state. "Putting on" is the wrong action trap because the glasses are already on her face.
  • Q3: (B) The man is holding/carrying the box. "Opening" is the wrong action trap.
  • Q4: (A) The street is quiet, so traffic is not congested.
  • Q5: (A) No one is in the photo, so "someone is watering" is a ghost action trap.
