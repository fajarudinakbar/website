Instructions for Candidates:

This is a full-length Reading test simulation. You will read a variety of texts and answer several different types of reading comprehension questions.

  • The entire Reading test will last exactly 75 minutes.
  • There are three parts (Part 5, Part 6, and Part 7).

  • Do not use the browser's back or forward buttons during the simulation.
  • You can navigate freely between questions 101 to 200 using the grid.

Reading Test

In the Reading test, you will read a variety of texts and answer several different types of reading comprehension questions. The entire Reading test will last 75 minutes. There are three parts, and directions are given for each part. You are encouraged to answer as many questions as possible within the time allowed.

Test Structure Overview

  • Part 5: Incomplete Sentences (Questions 101 - 130)
  • Part 6: Text Completion (Questions 131 - 146)
  • Part 7: Reading Comprehension (Questions 147 - 200)
