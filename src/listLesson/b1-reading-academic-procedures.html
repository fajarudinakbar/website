Applying for a Master’s degree can be stressful, but following this procedure will ensure your application is successful. The process for the September intake begins in January.

Step 1: Preparation and Eligibility

First, prospective students must check the website to see if they are eligible. You must hold a Bachelor's degree with a GPA of at least 3.0. If your GPA is lower than 3.0, you cannot apply.

Step 2: Documentation and Submission

Next, gather your documents. You need a valid passport, an IELTS certificate, and your academic transcripts. Once ready, create an account on the portal, upload the files, and pay the $50 application fee. You must submit everything before the March 31st deadline.

Step 3: Assessment (The Decision Phase)

After submission, the Admissions Office performs an "Admin Check." If your documents are incomplete, they will email you to request the missing files. You have 7 days to reply. If the documents are complete, your application is sent to the Faculty for review. This takes 4–6 weeks. If the Faculty rejects you, you will receive a notification email. The process ends here. If the Faculty accepts you, you will receive a conditional Letter of Offer.

Step 4: Acceptance and Enrollment

To accept the offer, you must pay the first semester’s tuition deposit. Finally, once the payment is confirmed, you will receive a "Confirmation of Enrollment" (CoE), which allows you to apply for a student visa.
