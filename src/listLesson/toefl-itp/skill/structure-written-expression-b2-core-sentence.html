Step 1 Skill Diagnostic

Identify the grammatical error in each sentence using the dropdown menus.

1. The construction of traditional Pinisi ships in South Sulawesi (A)requiring an intricate understanding of navigation.

2. (A)Despite the shipbuilders use no written plans, the vessels are structurally sound.

3. The timber is carefully selected, (A)because of the durability of the wood determines the lifespan of the ship.

Diagnostic Key:
  • 1. Fake Verb: "Requiring" is a participle, not a main verb. It should be "requires".
  • 2. Preposition vs Connector: "Despite" is a preposition and cannot be followed by a full clause. It should be the connector "Although".
  • 3. Preposition vs Connector: "Because of" is a preposition. Since it is followed by a full clause (the durability... determines), it must be the connector "because".

Step 3 Pattern Identification

Identify the specific grammatical trap in this sentence: "The Pinisi ship, which it was built by the Bugis people, navigates the deep oceans."

Trap Identified: Double Subject / Extra Pronoun.

In the adjective clause "which it was built", the relative pronoun "which" already acts as the subject. The pronoun "it" is an incorrect double subject. The correct phrase is: "which was built".

The Pinisi: Masters of the Maritime Tradition

The Pinisi, a traditional Indonesian sailing vessel, has been constructed by the Bugis and Makassar people of South Sulawesi for centuries. Because the builders rely entirely on oral traditions, no written blueprints are used during the construction process. Instead, the intricate knowledge of maritime engineering is passed down from generation to generation. Recognized by UNESCO as an Intangible Cultural Heritage, the Pinisi remains an integral part of Indonesia's maritime identity.

Although modern navigation systems have become widespread, many Pinisi captains still navigate by the stars. The hull of the ship is built first, which is a reversal of standard Western shipbuilding techniques. During the assembly, the wooden planks are fitted together with wooden pegs rather than iron nails. This unique method ensures that the vessel is highly resilient against heavy ocean waves.

In recent years, the demand for Pinisi ships has shifted from cargo transport to luxury tourism. Despite this modern transition, the core techniques of the South Sulawesi shipbuilders have remained unchanged. Anyone who visits the shipyards in Bulukumba will witness a remarkable blend of history, art, and engineering.

Answer:

Step 8 Structural Transformation

Choose the sentence that correctly transforms the clause (using Although) into a phrase (using Despite) without changing the meaning.

"Although the weather was bad, the ferry departed for Lombok on time."

Explanation:

The correct sentence is: "Despite the bad weather, the ferry departed for Lombok on time."

Rule: "Despite" is a preposition. It must be followed by a noun phrase ("the bad weather"). "Despite" never takes the word "of" (unlike "in spite of").

Step 9 The "Wrong-Option" Analysis

In TOEFL ITP, eliminating traps is key. Look at this sentence missing its first word:

Question: _______ the library was closed for renovations, the students went to a cafe to study.

(A) Due to

(B) Because

(C) Despite

(D) Therefore

The correct answer is (B) Because, since it connects two clauses. Your task is to identify why A, C, and D are traps.

(A)
(C)
(D)
Expert Breakdown:
  • (A) Due to: Wrong because it is a preposition. It must be followed by a noun phrase, not the clause "the library was closed".
  • (C) Despite: Wrong structurally (it is a preposition) AND logically (the meaning requires a cause-and-effect relationship, not contrast).
  • (D) Therefore: Wrong because transition words like "Therefore" or "Thus" cannot link two dependent clauses in this comma-separated structure without a semicolon or period.
