Step 3 Pattern Identification (The Traps)

When asked for the Main Idea, test makers use specific distractors. Match the trap type to its description.

Analysis & Answer Key:

1. Too Specific: The correct option is "An option that is factually true according to the text, but only covers one small detail or one paragraph." This is the most common trap! You read the option, remember seeing it in the text, and choose it. However, a main idea must cover the entire passage.

2. Too Broad: The correct option is "An option that covers a much larger topic than the passage actually discusses." This option sounds like a good title for a 300-page book, not a brief 3-paragraph passage.

Step 4 Instructions: Read the following B2-level academic passage. As you read, focus on the first sentence of each paragraph (the topic sentence) to quickly grasp the overarching theme.

Harnessing the Heat: Indonesia's Geothermal Potential

1 As the world seeks viable alternatives to fossil fuels to mitigate climate change, Indonesia finds itself in a highly advantageous geographic position. Situated squarely on the Pacific "Ring of Fire," the archipelago is characterized by intense volcanic and tectonic activity. This volatile geology, while responsible for frequent earthquakes and volcanic eruptions, also blesses the nation with the world’s largest reserves of geothermal energy. Geothermal energy involves extracting natural heat from the Earth's core, usually via heated water or steam, to drive turbines and generate electricity.

2 Despite this abundant natural wealth, Indonesia has historically been slow to fully harness its geothermal capacity. For decades, the nation relied heavily on its domestic coal and oil reserves, which were cheaper to extract and process in the short term. The initial infrastructure required to build a geothermal power plant is incredibly capital intensive. Deep drilling operations, specialized heat-resistant equipment, and extensive geological surveys present significant financial barriers, deterring prominent private investors.

3 Furthermore, the transition to geothermal power is complicated by geographical and environmental conflicts. Many of Indonesia’s most promising geothermal reservoirs are located deep within protected conservation forests. Developing these sites requires clearing land and building access roads, which inevitably sparks conflict with environmental groups concerned about biodiversity loss. The government faces the delicate task of balancing the urgent need for clean energy development with the equally critical mandate to protect its dwindling tropical rainforests.

4 Recently, however, the Indonesian government has implemented new policy frameworks designed to accelerate geothermal development. By offering tax incentives, streamlining the complex permitting process, and guaranteeing electricity purchase prices, the state hopes to attract international expertise and funding. If these initiatives prove successful, Indonesia could transition from a coal-dependent nation into a global leader in sustainable geothermal energy production.

Step 5 Rapid Gist Check

Without looking at the detailed questions yet, select the statement that best captures the primary purpose of the entire passage.

Answer: (B)

This is the only option that spans the whole text: Potential (Para 1), Obstacles/Costs (Para 2 & 3), and Government Actions (Para 4).

Step 9 The "Wrong-Option" Analysis

Below is the Main Idea question for Paragraph 3. The correct answer is (A). Your task is to identify why B, C, and D are incorrect by matching them to the trap type.

Question: What is the main point the author makes in the third paragraph?

(A) Developing geothermal energy often creates inevitable conflicts with vital forest conservation efforts.

(B) The government has decided to destroy tropical rainforests to build necessary access roads.

(C) Promising geothermal reservoirs are located deep within legally protected conservation forests.

(D) Balancing economic development with environmental protection is an increasingly complex global challenge.

(B)
(C)
(D)
Expert Breakdown:
  • (B) is False: The text states the government has a "critical mandate to protect" the forests, not destroy them.
  • (C) is Too Specific: This is a true detail that explains the context, but it does not represent the overarching point of the paragraph, which is the resulting conflict.
  • (D) is Too Broad: The text is specifically about geothermal energy in Indonesia, not a generalized global challenge.
