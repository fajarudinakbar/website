Instructions: TOEFL Reading is not just about Main Idea. You must also master Details, Vocabulary, References ("it", "they"), and Inference. This worksheet covers the strategies for all of them.

Task 1 Question Identification

Before you read the answers, you must identify what the question is asking. Match the phrase to the question type.

Why is this important?
If you know the type, you know where to look.
  • Reference? Look at the sentence before.
  • Detail? Scan for keywords.
  • Main Idea? Read the first lines.

Task 3 Context Clues

Sentence: "The plant is ephemeral; it blooms for only one day a year and then dies."
Even if you do not know "ephemeral", what does the context tell you it means?

  • Meaning: Short-lived or Temporary.
  • Clue: "Blooms for only one day... then dies." The definition was right next to the word!

1 Main Idea

Ask: "What is the topic?"

Strategy: Read the first sentence of each paragraph. Avoid "Too Narrow" (details) or "Too Broad" answers.

2 Detail / Fact

Ask: "According to the passage..."

Strategy: Pick a KEYWORD from the question. Scan the text to find that word. Read the sentence containing it.

3 Vocabulary

Ask: "The word 'x' is closest in meaning to..."

Strategy: Do not just use your dictionary memory. Read the sentence. Try replacing the word with the answer choices.

4 Reference

Ask: "The word 'they' refers to..."

Strategy: Look at the sentence before the pronoun. Look for a noun that matches in number (singular/plural).

5 Inference

Ask: "It can be inferred..." or "The author implies..."

Strategy: The answer is NOT stated directly. You must use logic. Example: "The window is wet." Inference: "It is raining."

Anchor Text: Plate Tectonics

[1] The theory of plate tectonics describes the large-scale motion of the Earth's lithosphere. This theory builds on the concept of continental drift, which was developed during the first few decades of the 20th century. The Earth's outer shell is divided into several plates that glide over the mantle, the rocky inner layer above the core. The plates act like a hard and rigid shell compared to the Earth's mantle.

[2] Tectonic plates are composed of oceanic lithosphere and thicker continental lithosphere, each topped by its own kind of crust. Along convergent boundaries, subduction carries plates into the mantle; the material lost is roughly balanced by the formation of new (oceanic) crust along divergent margins by seafloor spreading. In this way, it remains constant in size.

[3] The movement of these plates is responsible for many geological phenomena. Earthquakes, volcanic activity, mountain-building, and oceanic trench formation occur along plate boundaries. The lateral movement of the plates is typically at speeds of 50 to 100 mm annually.

Task 7 The "EXCEPT" Trap

Questions like "All of the following are mentioned EXCEPT..." or "Which is NOT true?" are time-consuming.

Passage: "Apples are rich in fiber, Vitamin C, and antioxidants. However, they do not contain much protein."
Question: Which is NOT found in apples?

A Fiber
B Vitamin C
C Protein
Answer: C. Protein.

Strategy: In "Negative" questions, you are looking for the False statement. A and B are True (mentioned in text), so they are wrong answers. C is False (mentioned as NOT containing), so it is the correct answer. It is reverse logic!
