Step 3 Pattern Identification

Identify the specific grammatical trap in this sentence: "The government distributed the funds equal among the provinces."

Trap Identified: Word Form Trap.

The word "equal" is an adjective. However, we need an adverb to describe how the funds were distributed. The correct form is "equally".

Step 5 Rapid Gist Check

Look at the bolded words in the Anchor Text (e.g., substantial, equitable, heavily, efficiently). What grammatical pattern is being demonstrated?

Answer:

The distinction between Adjectives and Adverbs.

  • Substantial and Equitable are adjectives modifying nouns ("funds", "growth").
  • Heavily and Efficiently are adverbs modifying verbs ("depends", "operate").

Step 8 Error Correction

The following sentence is INCORRECT. Choose the option that perfectly repairs the grammar.

"The number of smart cities in Indonesia are increasing rapidly."

Explanation:

The correct sentence is:
"The number of smart cities in Indonesia is increasing rapidly."

Rule: The phrase "The number of" takes a singular verb ("is"). The phrase "A number of" takes a plural verb ("are").
