Task 1 The Train Analogy

Think of a sentence like a train. It needs an Engine (Subject) to lead it and Wheels (Verb) to move it. Without both, it goes nowhere.

Question: What happens if you have an engine but no wheels? (e.g., "The large, expensive car...")

Answer: It is a Fragment (incomplete).

"The large, expensive car..." is just a Noun Phrase. It needs a verb (e.g., "...is fast") to become a complete sentence.

Task 3 The Preposition Trap

Look at this sentence: "In the morning, the birds sing."
What is the subject? "Morning" or "Birds"? Why?

Answer: The subject is Birds.

Rule: A subject is never inside a prepositional phrase. "In the morning" is just extra information telling us 'when'. Cross out prepositional phrases to find the real subject!

The Golden Formula: S + V

Every correct sentence in English must have at least one Subject and one Main Verb.

A Part A: Completion

The Goal: Find what is missing.

Strategy:

  1. Find the Verb. Is it missing?
  2. Find the Subject. Is it missing?
  3. If both are there, is a connector missing?

Example: "_______ was ringing." (Needs Subject)

B Part B: Written Expression

The Goal: Find what is extra or wrong.

Strategy:

  1. Double Subject: "My brother he is..." (Wrong)
  2. Double Verb: "The sun rose set." (Wrong)
  3. Missing Verb: "The boy playing." (Needs 'is')

Example: "The girl she is happy." ("She" is error)

Task 4 Identify S & V

Identify the Subject (S) and Verb (V) in this sentence:
"In the late 19th century, many immigrants arrived in New York."

  • Subject: Immigrants (Not "Century" or "New York" - these are objects of prepositions).
  • Verb: Arrived.

Task 7 Strategy Logic

Task 8 The "Double Subject" Trap

Why is this sentence wrong? "My brother he lives in Jakarta."

Answer: It has a Double Subject.

"My brother" is the subject. "He" is a pronoun referring to the same subject. You cannot have both right next to each other acting as the subject of the same verb.

Task 9 Fragment Hunting

The following text is a fragment (error): "The large yellow bus waiting at the corner."
Rewrite it to make it a correct sentence.

Sample Answer:

"The large yellow bus was waiting at the corner."
(Added 'was' to turn 'waiting' into a main verb.)

OR

"The large yellow bus waiting at the corner left early."
(Added 'left' as the main verb for the subject 'bus'.)

Task 10 Sentence Building

Write 3 simple sentences about your hometown. Ensure every sentence has a clear Subject and Verb.

Sample Sentences:

1. Jakarta is a very crowded city.
2. The local street food tastes absolutely amazing.
3. Many tourists visit the national monument every single year.

Task 11 Reflection

Which is harder: finding the missing word (Part A) or finding the wrong word (Part B)?

Sample Reflection:

"I personally find Part B harder because a sentence can look perfectly normal to me until I actively analyze the specific grammar rules, like number agreement or double subjects. Part A is slightly easier for me because the 'blank' space clearly shows exactly where the missing piece needs to go."
