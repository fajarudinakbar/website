Diagnostic Key:
  • 1. (A) at Preposition of Time error. Days of the week require the preposition "on" (on Monday).
  • 2. (B) in Dependent Preposition error. The verb "rely" is always followed by "on" or "upon", never "in".
  • 3. (C) about Redundant Preposition. The verb "discuss" means "to talk about," so adding "about" is redundant. It should simply be "discuss the new policies."

Step 3 Pattern Identification

Identify the specific grammatical trap in this sentence: "The university's graduation ceremony will take place in October 15."

Trap Identified: Preposition of Time Confusion (in vs. on).

While we use "in" for months (in October), we must use "on" when a specific date or day is provided. The correct phrase is "on October 15".

Step 8 Error Correction

The following sentence is INCORRECT. Choose the option that perfectly repairs the grammar.

"The university administration consists with five separate departments."

Explanation:

The correct sentence is:
"The university administration consists of five separate departments."

Rule: The verb "consist" must always be followed by the dependent preposition "of".

Expert Breakdown:
  • (A) new: Correct. Adjectives go immediately before the nouns they describe.
  • (B) was: Correct. It combines with the past participle "built" to form a singular passive verb matching "library."
  • (D) serve: Correct. "To" + base verb creates an infinitive of purpose. It answers "why" the library was built.
