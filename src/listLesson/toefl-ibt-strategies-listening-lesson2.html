Listening Lesson 2 Note-taking

Why Note-Taking Matters

In the TOEFL Listening section, you will hear conversations and lectures that are 3–5 minutes long. You only hear them once. Developing a shorthand system is critical to capturing key details without losing focus on the speaker.

Use Symbols

Don't write full sentences. Use arrows (→) for causes, math symbols (+, -, =) for relationships, and short abbreviations.

Listen for Structure

Pay attention to transition words like "however," "for example," and "consequently." These signal important shifts in the topic.
