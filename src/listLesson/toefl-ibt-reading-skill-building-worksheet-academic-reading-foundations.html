Short Academic Text (B2 Level)

Urban planners are increasingly interested in new city designs that improve daily life while reducing environmental impact. One popular idea focuses on bringing essential services closer to where people live. Supporters believe this approach can reduce traffic, improve health, and strengthen local communities. However, critics argue that such changes are difficult to implement fairly and may create new social problems if not carefully planned.
