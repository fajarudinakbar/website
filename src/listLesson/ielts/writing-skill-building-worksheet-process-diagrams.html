Skill Focus: Process Diagrams (Passive Voice, Sequencing, Cohesion)

Level: B2 (Upper Intermediate)

Step 1: Awareness - The Power of Passive Voice

In Process Diagrams, we often don't know who is doing the action. The focus is on the object. We use the Passive Voice.

A (Active - Less Academic)

"First, the machine digs up the clay. Then, a worker puts the clay on a metal grid."

B (Passive - IELTS Appropriate)

"First, the clay is dug up. Then, it is placed on a metal grid."

Why B is better:

  • It focuses on the material (the clay), not the worker.
  • It sounds more objective and formal.

📝 Essay Structure Checklist:

  • Introduction: Paraphrase the question ("The diagram depicts...").
  • Overview: Mention the 2 distinct phases (Nature & Processing).
  • Body 1: Describe Phase 1 using sequencing words.
  • Body 2: Describe Phase 2 using Passive Voice.

Model Answer

The diagram illustrates the life cycle of honey, from its initial collection by bees to the final processing stage for consumer sale. Overall, the process consists of two distinct phases: the natural production within the hive and the human manufacturing and packaging stages.

In the first stage, bees collect nectar from flowers. This nectar is then transported back to the hive, where it is deposited into individual honeycomb cells. Over time, the water content evaporates from the nectar, resulting in the creation of pure honey.

The second phase begins with the collection of the honeycombs. Once collected, they are placed into a centrifuge machine where they are spun rapidly to extract the liquid honey. Following extraction, the honey is passed through a filter into large storage tanks to remove impurities. Finally, the clean honey is poured into glass jars, labeled, and delivered to supermarkets for sale to the public.
