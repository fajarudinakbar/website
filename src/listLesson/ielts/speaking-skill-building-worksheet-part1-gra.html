IELTS Speaking Part 1 B2 Level

Speaking Skill-Building

Focus: Grammatical Range & Accuracy

Topic: Leisure Time Target: Band 7+

Step 1: Contextual Warm-up

Think about your favorite way to spend a weekend. Answer the following questions briefly in your head:

  • Do you prefer spending your free time alone or with others?
  • Is there a hobby you enjoyed as a child that you still do now?
  • How has the way you spend your free time changed over the last five years?

Step 2: Strategy Breakdown

To achieve a Band 7+ in Grammatical Range, you must move beyond simple subject-verb-object sentences.

Variety is King

Don't just use "I like..." Use a mix of present perfect, conditionals, and relative clauses.

Avoid 'The Robot'

Don't just answer "Yes" or "No." Extend your answer using conjunctions (e.g., because, although).

Show, Don't Tell

Use the PPF Method (Past, Present, Future) to naturally force different tenses into your response.

Step 3: Vocabulary & Lexical Input

To unwind (verb) To relax after tension.

"Swimming is the best way to unwind after work."

A bookworm (noun) Someone who reads a lot.

"I'm a bit of a bookworm, so I love libraries."

Sedentary (adj) Little physical activity.

"My job is sedentary, so I hike on weekends."

To take up (verb) Start a new hobby.

"I plan to take up photography soon."

Time-consuming (adj) Taking a lot of time.

"Model building is fun but time-consuming."

Catch up with (verb) Talk to friends / reach a standard.

"I catch up with old friends over coffee."

Step 4: Awareness & Analysis

"Actually, I don't play much now because I'm quite busy with my studies. However, I used to play competitive basketball when I was in high school. If I had more free time these days, I would probably join a local league, but for now, I just go jogging occasionally."

Identify the Grammar Structures:

"I used to play competitive basketball..."
"If I had more free time... I would probably join..."
"Actually... However..."

Step 5: Sentence Transformation (Micro)

Transform these simple sentences into complex ones using the prompt in brackets. Write your version, then check the suggested answer.

1. I like movies. (Use: Not only... but also)

Suggested: "Not only do I like movies, but I also enjoy analyzing the cinematography."

2. I played guitar three years ago. I stopped. (Use: Used to)

Suggested: "I used to play guitar, but I gave it up three years ago."

3. I will go to the park if it is sunny. (Use: Provided that)

Suggested: "I will go to the park provided that the weather stays clear."

4. I enjoy cooking. It is relaxing. (Use: Which)

Suggested: "I really enjoy cooking, which I find incredibly relaxing after work."

5. I want to learn French. I don't have time. (Use: Although)

Suggested: "Although I want to learn French, I simply don't have the time right now."
