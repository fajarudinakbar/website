IELTS Reading – Skill-Building Worksheet

Level: B2 (Upper-Intermediate)

Skill Focus: Reading Completion Question Types

Part Overview

Completion questions test your ability to find and understand specific information in a passage. You must read carefully, identify key ideas, and complete sentences, notes, tables, or summaries using information from the text. You will often need to recognize synonyms and paraphrased ideas.

Step 1 – Understand the Completion Question Types

  • Sentence Completion – Fill in the missing word(s) in a sentence.
  • Note/Table Completion – Complete notes or tables using details from the passage.
  • Summary Completion – Complete a summary using words from the text or from a box.
  • Short Answer Completion – Write short factual answers based on the reading.

Each type tests reading for detail, scanning, and understanding of key points.

Step 2 – Learn the General Strategy

  1. Read the instructions carefully (look for word limit).
  2. Read the incomplete items or sentences first.
  3. Underline keywords and think about what type of word is missing (noun, number, place, reason, etc.).
  4. Predict synonyms or paraphrases that might appear in the text.
  5. Scan the passage for these ideas, not just for exact words.
  6. Read carefully around that section to confirm the correct answer.
  7. Check spelling and grammar before finalizing your answers.

Step 3 – Sentence Completion Practice

Many people now prefer to shop online because it is more convenient and time-saving. Online stores also provide detailed product descriptions and reviews from other customers. However, some people still enjoy shopping in person because they can see and touch the products before buying.

Complete the sentences below with NO MORE THAN THREE WORDS from the passage.

before buying.
the products.

Answer Key & Explanations

  • 1. more convenient (The text says "...because it is more convenient and time-saving.")
  • 2. reviews / product descriptions / detailed product descriptions (The text says "...detailed product descriptions and reviews from other customers.")
  • 3. see and touch (The text says "...they can see and touch the products...")

Step 4 – Note / Table Completion Practice

A recent study found that regular exercise improves concentration and reduces stress. The study also showed that walking for 30 minutes a day can lower blood pressure. However, too much intense training without rest may cause fatigue or injury.

Complete the table below with NO MORE THAN FOUR WORDS AND/OR A NUMBER.

Benefit / Problem Cause / Activity
Better concentration
Lower blood pressure
Fatigue or injury

Answer Key & Explanations

  • 1. regular exercise (The text says "...regular exercise improves concentration...")
  • 2. walking for 30 minutes (The text says "...walking for 30 minutes a day can lower blood pressure.")
  • 3. intense training / too much intense training (The text says "...too much intense training... may cause fatigue or injury.")

Step 5 – Summary Completion Practice

Electric vehicles (EVs) are becoming more popular due to advances in battery technology. They produce no exhaust emissions and are cheaper to run than petrol cars. However, some drivers worry about limited charging stations, especially on long journeys.

Complete the summary below with NO MORE THAN THREE WORDS from the passage.

Electric vehicles are growing in popularity because of improvements in . They are more economical to use and do not create . One problem is the lack of for long-distance trips.

Answer Key & Explanations

  • 1. battery technology (The text says "...due to advances in battery technology.")
  • 2. exhaust emissions (The text says "They produce no exhaust emissions..." The summary's "do not create" + "no..." would be a double negative.)
  • 3. charging stations (The text says "...worry about limited charging stations..." The summary's "lack of" + "limited..." would be redundant.)

Step 6 – Short Answer Completion Practice

The Great Wall of China is one of the world’s most famous landmarks. It was built to protect China from invasions and stretches for over 20,000 kilometers. Today, millions of tourists visit the wall each year, making it one of China’s top attractions.

Answer the questions below with NO MORE THAN THREE WORDS AND/OR A NUMBER from the passage.

Answer Key & Explanations

  • 1. to protect China / from invasions (The text says "...built to protect China from invasions...")
  • 2. over 20,000 kilometers (The text says "...stretches for over 20,000 kilometers.")
  • 3. tourists (The text says "...millions of tourists visit...")

Step 7 – Reflect and Build Mastery

Checklist for Reading Completion Success

  • Did I identify the correct word limit for each question?
  • Did I underline key ideas before scanning?
  • Did I recognize synonyms and paraphrases instead of copying words?
  • Did I check spelling and grammar for each answer?
  • Did I manage my reading time effectively?
