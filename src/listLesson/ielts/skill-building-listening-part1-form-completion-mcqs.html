3. Understanding Instructions

Instruction: "Write NO MORE THAN THREE WORDS AND/OR A NUMBER"

Decide if the following are True or False based on the instruction:

a) You can write one word.
b) You can write one number only.
c) You can write words AND numbers.
d) You can write four words.

Activity 1: Answers & Explanations

1. Understanding the Situation
  • Topic: Sending a package. The form title "Shipping Agency" and fields like "Country of destination" give this away.
  • Speakers: Customer & Agent. One person is providing personal details to a business service.
2. Predicting Information
  • 1 (Name): A Surname is required after the first name "Jacob".
  • 2 (College): A specific place name is needed before "College".
  • 3 (Postcode): A mixture of letters and numbers (UK postcode format).
  • 4/5 (Dimensions): Measurements/Numbers are needed for Width/Height.
  • 8 (Value): A currency/cost amount is required (indicated by the £ sign).
3. Instructions

"NO MORE THAN THREE WORDS AND/OR A NUMBER"

  • a, b, c are True because they all fit within the limit (1 word, 1 number, or a mix < 3 words).
  • d is False because writing four words exceeds the limit of three.

Answer Key & Explanations

  • 1. Mkere: The customer spells his surname clearly: "M-K-E-R-E".
  • 2. Westall: The college name is spelled out: "W-E-S-T-A-L-L".
  • 3. BS8 9PU: The customer states the postcode directly: "BS8 9PU".
  • 4. 0.75m: The customer says "0.75m wide". (Note: 'width' matches 'wide').
  • 5. 0.5m: The customer says "0.5m high or deep".
  • 6. books: The customer lists the contents: "mostly clothes... and there's some books".
  • 7. toys: When asked "Anything else?", the customer adds "Yes, there's also some toys".
  • 8. 1700: The customer calculates the total: £1500 + £200 = "so I'd put down £1700".
  • 9. C (Premium): The customer rejects 'economy' because he was "stung before" and says "I'll go for the highest". The agent previously identified the "highest comprehensive cover" as "Premium rate".
