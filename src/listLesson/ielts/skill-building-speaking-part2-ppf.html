IELTS Speaking Task 2

The PPF Strategy

Skill Focus: Part 2 (The Long Turn)

Level: B1 Leisure & Hobbies

Rule Book

In Speaking Part 2, talk for 1-2 minutes. The Past, Present, Future (PPF) strategy stops you from running out of ideas. Expand across three time zones.

Think about a hobby. Answer briefly:

Split your answer into three distinct zones to naturally force wider grammar usage.

Past

Origins and how it began.

"I started this when..."

Present

Current habits and feelings.

"Nowadays, I usually..."

Future

Intentions and predictions.

"In the future, I hope..."
Transcript

"I’d like to talk about playing the piano. I first started learning when I was only seven years old because my mother wanted me to try music. However, these days, I find it very relaxing. I usually practice for thirty minutes every evening. Looking ahead, I’m planning to take a formal exam next year."

Identify the Time

1. "I first started learning..."
2. "However, these days..."
3. "Looking ahead, I’m planning..."

Transform sentences into the required tense.

1. "I play tennis."

Change to: Past Simple

2. "I don't have time."

Change to: Future (going to)

3. "I take lessons on Sundays."

Change to: Past Habit (Used to)

Describe a sport you enjoy

  • What the sport is
  • How long you have been interested in it
  • Who you play it with
Explain why you enjoy it & future plans.
