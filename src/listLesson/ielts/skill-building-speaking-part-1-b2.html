IELTS Speaking Skill-Building

Focus: Producing Natural, Extended Answers

Target: Band 7

Part 0: What Part 1 Tests

In IELTS Speaking Part 1, the examiner asks short questions about familiar topics (work, study, hobbies, routines). At Band 7, you are expected to:

  • Answer fluently in 2-3 sentences.
  • Extend ideas naturally (avoid short Yes/No replies).
  • Include reasons or examples.
  • Use a range of vocabulary and grammar accurately.

Part 1: Understanding Strong Answers

The Band 7 Pattern:

Answer + Reason + Example (Optional)

Exercise 1: Identifying Strong Answers

Question: Do you enjoy cooking?

Answer A

"Yes, I do."

Too short. Band 5.
Answer B

"Yes, I enjoy cooking, especially on weekends, because it helps me relax. I usually try simple recipes at home."

Correct! Band 7.

Part 3: Language for Band 7

Frequency: usually, quite often, from time to time
Reasons: because, since, as, mainly because
Preferences: I prefer..., I'd rather...
Time: these days..., compared to the past...
