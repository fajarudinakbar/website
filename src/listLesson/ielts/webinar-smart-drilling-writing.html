Opening and Framing

Focus: Writing as a performance skill

Speaker Actions

Present the common IELTS writing problem: learners recognise high-band answers but cannot reproduce them under exam pressure. Introduce the concept of “Smart Drilling for Writing to Build Muscle Memory”.

Professor's Note

Connect this to the "communicative approach." Ask audience: "How many of you have students who know the grammar rules but freeze when the timer starts?"

Visual / Slide Concept

Image of a musician or athlete training vs. a student reading a book. Text: "Knowledge vs. Performance."
