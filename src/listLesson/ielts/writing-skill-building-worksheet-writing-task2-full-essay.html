Step 2: Strategy Breakdown

Writing an essay is like building a house. For B1 students, we use the PEEL Method for every body paragraph to ensure you never get lost.

P: Point

State your main idea (Topic Sentence).

E: Explanation

Explain why or how your point is true.

E: Example

Give a specific real-world example.

L: Link

Connect this idea back to the main question.

Part 2: Core Content

Step 3: Vocabulary & Lexical Input

Academic Qualifications Degrees or certificates from schools/universities.
Hands-on experience Knowledge gained by actually doing a job.
Theoretical Based on ideas and books rather than practice.
Entry-level position A job for someone new to a career field.
Competitive A situation where many people want the same thing.
To specialize To focus on a specific area of study or work.

Step 5: Guided Analysis (Model Essay)

Prompt: Some people think that a university degree is the best way to get a good job. Others believe that work experience is more important. To what extent do you agree?

Introduction:

Currently, there is a debate about whether higher education or practical experience is better for a career. While some argue for the importance of working early, I strongly agree that a university degree provides a more solid foundation for long-term success.

Body Paragraph 1 (Focus: Knowledge):

To begin with, universities provide deep theoretical knowledge that work experience cannot offer. In a classroom, students learn the "why" behind their tasks, which helps them solve complex problems later. For example, an architect needs to understand physics and math before they start building. Consequently, this academic background makes a candidate more reliable.

Body Paragraph 2 (Focus: Salary & Growth):

Furthermore, people with degrees usually earn higher salaries over time. Statistics show that university graduates often start at a higher level than those with only a high school diploma. In many large companies, you cannot be promoted to a manager without a degree. Thus, a degree acts as a "key" to open doors to better positions.

Conclusion:

In conclusion, although work experience is helpful for learning daily tasks, I believe a degree is more important. It provides the necessary knowledge and better career growth in the future.
