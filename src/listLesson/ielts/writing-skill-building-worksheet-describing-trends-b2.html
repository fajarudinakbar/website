Describing Trends Over Time

Topic: Vocabulary for movement, line graphs, and structuring the overview.

Instructions: This worksheet focuses on B1 level skills (Band 4.0 - 5.0). You will analyze a report on internet usage in Indonesia, learn verbs of movement, and practice writing a clear overview.

Task 1 Cultural Connection

Think about technology in Indonesia over the last 10 years. How has the price of mobile data packages (pulsa/kuota) changed? Has it gone up or down? Why?

Teacher's Note: In Indonesia, while the amount of data we use has rocketed (gone up quickly), the price per gigabyte has generally declined (gone down) or remained competitive due to providers like Telkomsel, Indosat, and XL fighting for customers.

Task 2 Movement Verbs (B1-B2 Focus)

Match the verbs and adverbs to their correct definitions/synonyms.

Percentage of Population Using the Internet in Indonesia (2010–2020)

Model Response:

The line graph illustrates the proportion of people in Indonesia who used the internet between the years 2010 and 2020.

Overall, it is clear that internet usage in Indonesia increased significantly over the ten-year period. While the growth was slow at the beginning of the decade, it became much faster after 2014.

In 2010, only a small minority of the population, approximately 10%, had access to the internet. Over the next four years, this figure rose gradually to reach just under 20% in 2014.

However, after 2014, the trend changed. The percentage of internet users climbed steeply. By 2018, half of the population (50%) was online. This upward trend continued until the end of the period, with the figure peaking at roughly 75% in 2020.

Strategy Tip: The 'Overview'

For Band 5.0+, you must include an overview (summary). Look for the "Overall" paragraph above. It does not look at specific numbers (like 10% or 75%), but looks at the general trend (it increased significantly). Always write this after your introduction!

Task 6 Paraphrasing the Introduction

The question prompt was: "The graph below shows the percentage of internet users in Indonesia from 2010 to 2020."

Look at the model answer's first sentence. How did the writer paraphrase "shows"? How did they paraphrase "from 2010 to 2020"?

Analysis:
1. "Shows" changed to "Illustrates".
2. "Percentage" changed to "Proportion".
3. "From 2010 to 2020" changed to "between the years 2010 and 2020".
Paraphrasing is essential for a high score!

Task 7 Writing an Overview

Scenario: Imagine a graph showing "Foreign Tourists in Bali (2019-2022)".
Data: 2019 (High), 2020 (Dropped sharply), 2021 (Stayed low), 2022 (Recovered slightly).

Write only the Overview paragraph (starts with "Overall..."). Don't give specific numbers. Describe the main trend (the drop and the slight recovery).

Writing Area Word Count: 0
Model Overview:
Overall, it is clear that the number of foreign tourists visiting Bali decreased dramatically over the period shown. While visitor numbers were high at the beginning of the period, they plummeted in 2020 and remained stable at a low level, before showing a slight recovery in 2022.
