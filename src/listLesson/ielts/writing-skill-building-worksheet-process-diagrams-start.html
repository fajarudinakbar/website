IELTS Writing – Skill-Building Worksheet

Skill Focus: The "Start Strategy" (Intro & Overview)

Level: B2 (Upper Intermediate)

Step 1: Awareness – The "Map" of Your Essay

A high-scoring Task 1 essay always begins with two specific elements:

  • The Introduction (Paraphrase): Rewrite the question prompt using different words.
  • The Overview (The Big Picture): Summarize the process without specific data. Ask: Is it Linear or Cyclical? How many stages? Start/End points?

Weak Start:

"The diagram shows how to make olive oil. It has 6 steps."

Strong Start:

"The provided illustration outlines the technical process by which olive oil is produced. Overall, there are seven distinct stages, commencing with the harvesting of olives and culminating in the sale of bottles in shops."

Step 2: Guided Practice

💡

Key Concepts:

  • Linear (L): Starts at A and ends at B (e.g., Making coffee).
  • Cyclical (C): Starts at A and goes back to A (e.g., The water cycle).

Writing Formula:

  • Introduction: The provided diagrams [synonym for show] the [synonym for life cycle] of the silkworm and the [synonym for production] of silk cloth.
  • Overview: Overall, there are two processes: a [natural/cyclical] biological cycle and a [linear] industrial process. The first begins with... and the second ends with...

Model Introduction + Overview

The provided diagrams illustrate the natural life cycle of the silkworm and the subsequent stages involved in the manufacture of silk cloth.

Overall, it is immediately apparent that the process involves two distinct phases: a biological cycle consisting of four main stages, and a linear industrial process that transforms the raw cocoon into finished fabric. The process commences naturally with the moth laying eggs and concludes with the dyeing of the woven silk cloth.
