Topic: Identifying Main Ideas vs. Specific Details

Instructions: The "Matching Headings" task asks you to choose the correct summary (heading) for each paragraph. This tests your ability to find the Main Idea (usually in the first or second sentence) and ignore distracting details.

Task 1 Cultural Connection: Reading the News

When you scroll through news apps like Detik or Kompas, you see dozens of headlines. What makes a good headline? Does a headline tell you every single detail, or does it just give you the "big picture"?

Teacher's Note: A good headline captures the Main Idea or the "gist" of the article. It summarizes the whole story in a few words. IELTS headings do exactly the same thing for a single paragraph.

The Rise of E-Sports in Education

Strategy Tip: Read the first sentence of a paragraph carefully. It is usually the Topic Sentence and contains the main idea. Look at the rest of the paragraph quickly to make sure it supports that first sentence.

A. Competitive video gaming, widely known as e-sports, has exploded in popularity over the last decade. What was once a casual hobby played in bedrooms has transformed into a massive global industry. Today, millions of fans fill stadiums to watch professional gamers compete, and major tournaments offer millions of dollars in prize money. The sheer number of viewers now rivals traditional sports like basketball and baseball.

B. While some parents worry that gaming is a waste of time, researchers are discovering that e-sports can develop valuable cognitive skills. Players must make split-second decisions, solve complex problems under pressure, and communicate effectively with their teammates. These strategy and teamwork skills are highly transferable and are exactly the kinds of abilities that modern employers look for in the workplace.

C. However, this digital phenomenon is not without its serious drawbacks. Professional gamers often practice for up to 14 hours a day, leading to severe physical problems such as wrist injuries, eye strain, and back pain. Furthermore, the intense pressure to win can cause high levels of stress and burnout. Health professionals are increasingly concerned about gaming addiction among young people who try to imitate their e-sports heroes.

D. Recognizing both the popularity and the skill required, some educational institutions are now embracing e-sports. High schools are creating official e-sports teams, treating them with the same respect as the football or debate teams. More surprisingly, several top universities have started offering full degrees in e-sports management, as well as scholarships for talented players, proving that gaming is now seen as a legitimate academic and career path.

Task 3 Main Idea vs. Detail

Look at Paragraph A. It mentions "millions of dollars in prize money". Why is a heading like "The Financial Cost of Gaming" a bad choice for this paragraph?

Answer: "Prize money" is just a specific detail used to support the main point. The whole paragraph is about the overall growth and popularity of e-sports, not just the money. Also, prize money is not a "cost".

Task 6 Beware of Exact Word Matches

Look at Paragraph D. It mentions the words "educational institutions" and "universities".

If there is a heading that says: "Why universities are losing students to gaming", why should you be suspicious of it?

Analysis:
In IELTS, if a heading uses the exact same words as the text (like "universities"), it is very often a trap! The heading says universities are "losing students", but the text actually says universities are offering degrees in gaming. Always check the meaning, not just the words!
