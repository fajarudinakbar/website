Identify the Weakness

Prompt: The charts show the main reasons for migration to and from the UK in 2007.

Student Introduction: "The charts show the main reasons why people migrated to and from the UK in 2007."
Teacher Fajar's Analysis: The student basically copied the prompt word-for-word. This is called "lifting." You must demonstrate vocabulary range.

Better: "The pie charts illustrate the primary factors influencing individuals' decisions to relocate to and emigrate from the United Kingdom during the year 2007."

Find the Fatal Flaw

Read this student's Overview. What critical error will prevent them from achieving a C1/Band 7.0 score?

"Overall, it is clear that renewable energy production increased significantly. Specifically, it rose from 20% in 2010 to 65% in 2020. In contrast, reliance on fossil fuels saw a steady decline."
Teacher's Analysis: The student included specific data (20% and 65%) in the Overview. The overview must remain a "helicopter view" of the trends. Once you include specific data points, it becomes a Body Paragraph.

Coherence and Cohesion relies on how you structure Body Paragraph 1 and 2. Evaluate the best ways to group data.
