How to Extend Your Answers

In Part 3, a short answer is not enough. Aim for 3-5 sentences using the P.E.E.L. structure:

P - Point

Answer the question directly.

E - Explain

Give a reason (Why?). "This is because..."

E - Example

Give a specific detail. "For instance..."

L - Link

Summarize back to the question. "Ultimately..."

Model Q&A

Examiner: "Do you think online shopping will replace physical stores in the future?"

POINT To be honest, I don't think shops will disappear completely, but their role will definitely change.

EXPLAIN The main reason is that people still enjoy the physical experience of shopping. We like to touch fabrics or try on clothes to make sure they fit, which you simply can't do on a screen.

EXAMPLE For instance, even though I buy gadgets online, I always go to a mall to buy shoes because I need to walk around in them first.

LINK So, while online shopping is convenient, physical stores still have a future as places for 'experience' rather than just sales.

Task 3 Analyzing Structure

Read the sentence below. Which part of P.E.E.L. is it? (Point, Explain, Example, or Link?)

"This is primarily due to the fact that shipping costs have become much cheaper recently."
Answer: This is the Explanation. The phrase "due to the fact that" gives a reason.

Task 6 Your Turn

"Is it better for the economy if people save money or spend it?"

Model Structure:
Point: Generally speaking, I believe spending is better for the economy.
Explain: The reason is that when people spend, businesses make profit, which allows them to hire more staff and expand.
Example: For instance, during holidays like Lebaran, high consumer spending boosts small businesses significantly.
Link: Ultimately, while saving is good for the individual, high spending is essential for keeping the economic cycle moving.
