IELTS Writing Task 2 Body Paragraphs

IELTS Skill-Building

Target Skill: How to Write Effective Body Paragraphs

Level: B1 to B2 Focus: T.E.E. Structure

Instructions: Master the building blocks of a high-scoring IELTS essay. Learn how to construct logical, well-developed body paragraphs using the T.E.E. method.

In IELTS Writing Task 2, you must present your ideas clearly. A strong body paragraph focuses on one main idea and develops it fully. A weak paragraph is often just a scattered list of ideas. A simple, effective structure to follow is T.E.E.:

  • T Topic Sentence: The first sentence, which introduces your ONE main idea.
  • E Explanation: One or two sentences that explain your idea. Ask yourself: Why? or How?
  • E Example: One or two sentences that give a specific, concrete example to support your idea.

The Hook: Evaluation

Task: Read the two paragraphs below, which answer the question: "What are the advantages of working from home?" Which paragraph is stronger?

Paragraph A

"Working from home has many advantages. People do not have to commute, which saves time. They can also save money on transport. Furthermore, they have more flexibility and can manage their personal life better. Many companies also save money on office space. It is also good for the environment."

Paragraph B

"(T) One significant advantage of working from home is the improved work-life balance for employees. (E) This is because people save a large amount of time by not commuting, which they can then use for family, hobbies, or rest. (Ex) For example, a parent can finish their work at 5 p.m. and immediately join their family, rather than spending an hour in traffic. This flexibility greatly reduces stress."

Paragraph B is much stronger.
Why? Paragraph A is just a scattered list of ideas with no depth. Paragraph B takes one specific idea ("improved work-life balance") and explains it fully with a concrete example. This depth and structure is what you must demonstrate in your IELTS essay.

Topic: The benefits of public libraries.

[Sentence A] For instance, a person who is unemployed can use the library's computers to apply for jobs or learn new skills through free workshops.

[Sentence B] Public libraries play a vital role in providing free access to educational resources for all members of the community.

[Sentence C] This means that people who cannot afford to buy books or pay for internet access can still get information and learning opportunities.

Task 2: Label the Paragraph

Read the paragraph below. Identify and label the Topic Sentence (T), the Explanation (E), and the Example (Ex).

"Banning advertising for fast food products could be an effective way to reduce public health problems."

"This is because constant advertising, especially during popular TV shows, normalizes the consumption of unhealthy, high-calorie food."

"If people, particularly children, see these products less often, they are less likely to desire them."

"For example, several countries that banned tobacco advertising saw a significant long-term decrease in smoking rates, suggesting this approach can work for food as well."

Task 1 Correct Order:

1st: Sentence B (T)
2nd: Sentence C (E)
3rd: Sentence A (Ex)

Task 2 Correct Labels:

  • Sentence 1 = T (Topic)
  • Sentence 2 = E (Explanation)
  • Sentence 3 = E (Explanation)
  • Sentence 4 = Ex (Example)

Now, let's practice creating the individual T.E.E. components yourself.

Tasks 1 to 3: Write a Topic Sentence (T)

1. Main Idea Studying abroad is expensive.

Sample: "One significant disadvantage of studying abroad is the high financial cost involved."

2. Main Idea The internet has made it easy to find information.

Sample: "The internet has revolutionized modern life, primarily by providing instant access to global information."

3. Main Idea Cities have a problem with traffic.

Sample: "A major challenge facing most large metropolitan areas today is severe traffic congestion."

Tasks 4 to 6: Write an Explanation (E)

4. T Governments should invest more money in public transport.

Sample: "This is because high-quality, reliable buses and trains encourage people to use their personal vehicles less, which significantly reduces pollution and traffic."

5. T Learning a second language can improve a person's career opportunities.

Sample: "This is due to the fact that many multinational companies require employees who can seamlessly communicate with diverse clients and overseas partners."

6. T Online shopping has become extremely popular in recent years.

Sample: "The main reason for this trend is the unparalleled convenience of being able to compare prices and purchase products from home at any hour of the day."

Tasks 7 to 9: Provide an Example (Ex)

7. T Using too much social media can negatively affect young people's mental health.

E They often see unrealistic images of success or happiness, which can make them feel insecure.

Sample: "For instance, a teenager might constantly see photos of celebrities on perfect holidays and feel that their own life is inadequate by comparison."

8. T Many traditional skills are being lost in the modern age.

E This is because automated machines can now perform these jobs, and younger generations are uninterested in learning them.

Sample: "For example, skills like traditional weaving or handmade furniture crafting are disappearing rapidly as consumers increasingly prefer cheaper, mass-produced items."

9. T Team sports are highly beneficial for children's development.

E They teach children essential social skills and teamwork that cannot be easily replicated in a standard classroom setting.

Sample: "For example, by playing on a football team, children must learn how to communicate effectively under pressure and support their teammates towards a common goal."

Task 10: Order the Paragraph

Put these sentences in the correct T.E.E. order.
Topic: The disadvantage of low-cost air travel.

[Sentence A] For example, a single return flight from London to New York can generate more CO2 per person than an individual in some countries produces in an entire year.

[Sentence B] This is because flying is a highly carbon-intensive activity, and the increased number of flights directly contributes to greenhouse gas emissions.

[Sentence C] The primary disadvantage of the boom in low-cost air travel is its severe impact on the environment.

Correct Order:
1st: Sentence C (Topic) 2nd: Sentence B (Explanation) 3rd: Sentence A (Example)

Use the skills you have practiced to write one full body paragraph for an Academic Writing Task 2 prompt.

Academic Task 2 Prompt

"Some people believe that university students should pay for their own education, while others argue that governments should pay for it."

"Discuss both these views and give your own opinion."

Your Task:

Write one body paragraph supporting the view that the government should pay for university education. Make sure to use the T.E.E. structure.

Model Paragraph:

Here is a high-scoring model. Your paragraph may be different, but it should contain similar distinct structural parts.

T One strong argument for government-funded higher education is that it ensures equality of opportunity for all citizens. E If students are forced to pay high tuition fees, many intelligent individuals from poorer backgrounds will be unable to attend university. This creates an unfair system where only the wealthy can access the best education and subsequent jobs. Ex For example, in countries like Finland or Germany, where tuition is free or heavily subsidized, young people are able to choose their careers based on their talent and passion, rather than their family's financial situation.

Review the paragraph you wrote in Step 4 and analyze it using the checklist below.

1. Topic Sentence: Can you clearly identify your Topic Sentence? Does it focus on exactly ONE main idea?

2. Explanation: Does your Explanation sentence clearly and logically explain *why* or *how* your topic sentence is true?

3. Example: Is your Example specific, concrete, and directly relevant to the main point?

4. Weakest Link: Which part of the T.E.E. structure do you find the most difficult to write (T, E, or E)? Why?
