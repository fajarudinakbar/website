Working on the Holiday

Topic: Work-Life Balance & Holiday Shifts in Indonesia

Instructions: Complete the Preparation first. Then read about Putu, Dian, and Arief.
(Correct answers appear after 2 incorrect attempts.)

Match the work words (1–6) with their meanings (a–f).

Most people love public holidays. It is a time to relax, visit family, or go on vacation. However, for some workers, holidays are the busiest days of the year.

Putu

Bali

Hotel Receptionist

"For me, the holiday season is very crazy. I work at a hotel in Nusa Dua. In December and January, the hotel is 100% full. I work the night shift from 10 PM to 6 AM. It is tiring, but I like it because the guests are happy. Also, the hotel gives us a nice bonus at the end of the month."

Dian

Jakarta

IT Support

"My office is usually very loud, but during the holidays, it is very quiet. Most of my colleagues take annual leave to go back to their hometowns. I choose to stay in Jakarta. Why? Because the company pays double pay on public holidays. I can save this money to buy a new laptop. I will visit my parents next month when the tickets are cheaper."

Arief

Surabaya

Train Conductor

"I work for the railway company. Holidays are stressful because thousands of people want to travel at the same time. I have to arrange the passengers and check tickets quickly. I miss eating special food with my family, but I feel proud. I help people get home safely to see their mothers and fathers."
