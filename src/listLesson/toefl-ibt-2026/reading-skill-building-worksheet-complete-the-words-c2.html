Step 1: Schema Activation (The Hook)

Consider the concept of "Linguistic Redundancy." In high-level texts, language patterns allow a proficient reader to predict the end of a word the moment they see the prefix. At the C2 level, you aren't reading letter-by-letter; you are predicting structure.

Step 2: Skill Definition & Strategy

This task evaluates your intuitive grasp of morphology (word building) and syntax (sentence function). The focus shifts from simple nouns to nuanced adverbs and abstract academic collocations.

Pro Tip for 2026:

Pay attention to prosody and register. Formal texts often require "heavy" Latinate/Greek suffixes (-ization, -ological, -centric) rather than simple Germanic endings.
