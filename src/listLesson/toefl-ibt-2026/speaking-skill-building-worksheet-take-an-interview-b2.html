Structuring a Spontaneous Answer

Because you have zero preparation time, you must rely on a mental framework to organize your thoughts instantly. Use the P.R.E.P. method to speak fluently for 45 seconds:

P = Point

Answer the question directly in the first sentence. "I definitely prefer living in a big city."

R = Reason

Give one clear reason why you feel that way. Use a transition word. "The main reason is that cities offer more opportunities."

E = Example

Support your reason with a personal story or a specific example. This takes up most of your time. "For example, when I moved to Tokyo last year..."

P = Point (Conclude)

If you have a few seconds left, restate your main idea to finish strongly. "So, that is why I prefer the city environment."

💡 Lexical Fillers (Buying Time)

If you need 2 seconds to think, don't say "Umm." Say: "That's a very interesting question..." or "Well, in my opinion..."
