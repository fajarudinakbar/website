Lesson Concept & Direct Alignment

The TOEFL iBT Write an Email task grades you on how effectively you integrate context, register control, syntactic complexity, and pragmatic functions. Rather than generalized feedback, we compare student drafts across identical scenarios using indicators linked to the official criteria.

SCORE 5

Fully Successful

The response is effective, is clearly expressed, and shows consistent facility in the use of language.

Elaboration

Elaboration that effectively supports the communicative purpose.

Social Conventions

Consistent use of appropriate social conventions (politeness, register, info organization, and request formulation).

Language Control

Effective syntactic variety and precise, idiomatic word choice throughout the drafting.

Error Allowance

Almost no lexical/grammatical errors, other than expected natural typos from competent writers under timed limits.

SCORE 4

Generally Successful

The response is mostly effective and easily understood. Language facility is adequate to the task.

Elaboration

Adequate elaboration to support the communicative purpose with minimal gaps.

Social Conventions

Mostly appropriate social conventions; general control over politeness structures.

Language Control

Syntactic variety and appropriate, functional vocabulary choice.

Error Tolerance

Few lexical or grammatical errors that do not impact or impede comprehension.

SCORE 3

Partially Successful

The response generally accomplishes the task. Limitations in language facility prevent parts of message from being fully clear.

Elaboration

Elaboration that only partially supports the communicative intent.

Social Conventions

Some noticeable errors in structures or social conventions (often too abrupt or command driven).

Language Range

Moderate range of syntax and vocabulary with basic configurations.

Error Accumulation

Noticeable errors in structure, word forms, or use of idiomatic expressions.

SCORE 2

Mostly Unsuccessful

The response reflects an attempt to address the task, but it is mostly ineffective.

Elaboration

Limited or highly irrelevant details to communicate purpose.

Syntax & Lexicon

Some connected sentence level writing, but very limited range of syntax.

Errors

An accumulation of sentence structure and/or linguistic usage errors make interpretations difficult.

SCORE 1-0

Ineffective Attempts

Extremely restricted ability or complete deviation from criteria as defined in scoring instructions.

Score 1 Standard

Telegraphic, disconnected phrases. Seriously limited vocabulary or mostly borrowed language from the stimulus.

Score 0 Standard

Blank paper, rejects topic, off-topic, not in English, or random arbitrary keystrokes.

To: Registrar's Office <registrar@university.edu>
Subject: Incorrect Transcript Received

Dear Registrar’s Office,

I am writing because I wanted my transcript yesterday but I got a different document instead. It was not mine.

Can you send me the correct transcript file? I want it fast because I am applying for an internship and the deadline is tomorrow morning.

Thank you.

Sincerely,
Maria Lopez

Thank you for your assistance with this matter.

Sincerely,
Paul Grant

Scoring Comparison Matrix

Systemic breakdown based on official scoring criteria.

Metric Alignment Score 3 Drafts Score 4 Drafts Score 5 Drafts
Elaboration PARTIAL Only partial support for communicative goals. Omit temporal data, IDs, or logical priorities. Expects reader to guess context. ADEQUATE Explains purpose and details clearly. May lack highly advanced arguments or secondary details, but is functionally complete. EFFECTIVE Injects precise, highly specific details (exact times, consequences of file errors) which systematically validate queries.
Social Conventions ABRUPT COMMANDS Inappropriate register. Relies heavily on harsh imperative directives ("Send me this," "Do it now") instead of polite markers. POLITE / NORMAL Applies common modal syntax patterns ("Could you please let me know," "I would appreciate it"). Clear and structured. HIGH FACILITY Impeccable, consistent style control. Sophisticated mitigators ("If it is not too much trouble," "I hope this email finds you well").
Language Facility MODERATE Relies on basic and repetitive vocabulary combinations ("I want," "Get this"). Moderate syntax range with noticeable errors. FUNCTIONAL Clean, natural flow with basic compound structures. Minor grammatical mistakes, but meanings are clear and easily understood. PRECISE & VARIANT Combines clauses complexly, employing passive forms, precise idiomatic vocabulary, and flawless transitional flow.
