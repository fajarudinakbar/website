Complete the Words

Topic: Context Clues, Word Forms, and B1 Vocabulary

Instructions: In this reading task (often called a "C-Test"), you will read a short academic paragraph. The first sentence is complete. Starting from the second sentence, the second half of every second word is deleted. You have 2 attempts to fill in the missing letters.

Task 1 Cultural Connection: Guessing vs. Analyzing

Indonesian learners often guess vocabulary by trying to translate the whole idea into Bahasa Indonesia first.

Question: Why is relying solely on translation dangerous for the "Complete the Words" task, and what should you look for instead?

Strategy: Translation is too slow and imprecise for this task. Instead of translation, you must analyze grammar (Is it a noun? A verb? Plural?) and morphology (word parts). For example, if you see "informat___", knowing the English suffix "-ion" (information) is faster than translating "informasi".

Task 3 The "Second Half" Rule

In this task, if a word has 6 letters (e.g., "doctor"), you will see "doc___". If a word has 5 letters (e.g., "water"), you might see "wa___".
Challenge: Identify the missing letters for these B1 words based on the partial stem given.

1. tra___ (travel)
2. heal___ (healthy)
Answer:
1. travel (3 letters missing)
2. healthy (3 letters missing)
Tip: Always count the letters in your head. The missing part is roughly half the word.

Understanding the "C-Test" Format

The "Complete the Words" task is designed to test your processing speed and vocabulary recall. Unlike multiple-choice questions, you must actively produce the language.

Key Mechanics:

  • Context First: The first sentence of the paragraph is always intact (complete). Read it carefully! It tells you the topic.
  • Every Second Word: Starting from the second sentence, every second word is damaged.
  • The Missing Half: Usually, the second half of the word is missing. If a word has an odd number of letters (e.g., 7), the missing part is usually the larger half (4 letters).
