Welcome!

This interactive lesson will help you master the updated TOEFL iBT® Listening section. You will explore different task types, practice with real-world examples, and test your understanding with a final quiz.

Learning Objectives:

  • Identify the different listening task types.
  • Practice identifying implied meaning in conversations.
  • Practice capturing main ideas and details in academic talks.
  • Review key facts about the Listening section.

Part 1: Listening Section Tasks

Review the four main task types you will encounter in the Listening section.

Listen to Task Explanations

1. Listen and Choose a Response

Hear a short, everyday dialogue. You'll choose the most appropriate response from four options. The question is only heard, not read.

2. Listen to a Conversation

Listen to a short conversation between two people. You'll answer two questions about the content and implied meaning.

3. Listen to an Announcement

Hear an announcement from an academic context (like a classroom). You'll answer two questions about the details.

4. Listen to an Academic Talk

A short academic talk (100-250 words) from various fields. You'll answer four questions on the main ideas and details.
