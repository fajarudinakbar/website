Listening: Listen & Choose a Response

Skill: Pragmatic Competence Level: CEFR B1/B2 Items: 10

How to Master This Task

In the "Listen and Choose a Response" task, you will hear a short sentence (a question, statement, or greeting) and must select the most natural response.

Key Strategies:
  • Identify the Type: Is it a Yes/No question, a Wh- question, or a statement?
  • Listen for Tone: Is the speaker apologizing, offering help, or complaining?
  • Watch for Distractors: Incorrect options often use words that sound similar to the prompt but don't make sense.
The Two-Attempt Rule:
  • You have two chances to answer each question correctly.
  • 1st Attempt: Feedback is hidden. Try again if wrong.
  • 2nd Attempt: If wrong, the correct answer, detailed explanation, and transcript will appear.

Guided Example

Let's look at one example with "Think-Aloud" logic to understand how to eliminate wrong answers.

Audio
"Would you mind keeping the noise down a bit?"
(A) Yes, I enjoy loud music. x Incorrect (Rude/Literal misinterpretation)
(B) Oh, I'm sorry, I didn't realize I was being loud. ✓ Correct
(C) No, the noise is over there. x Incorrect (Irrelevant)
(D) Yes, please turn it up. x Incorrect (Opposite meaning)
Teacher Fajar's Logic: The phrase "Would you mind..." is a polite request to stop doing something.
Option A answers "Yes" (meaning "Yes, I mind") but then contradicts it.
Option B apologizes and complies, which is the socially appropriate response to a complaint.
