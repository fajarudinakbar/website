Welcome!

This interactive lesson covers the updated TOEFL iBT® Speaking section. You will evaluate your current skills, learn about the specific task types through an interactive exercise, and practice answering real prompts.

Learning Objectives:

  • Evaluate your current confidence and ability in English speaking.
  • Understand the "Listen and Repeat" and "Virtual Interview" task types.
  • Practice formulating responses to common speaking topics.

Example Weekly Plan:
  • Monday: Listen to the "6 Minute English" podcast by BBC while commuting. Write down 3 new words I hear.
  • Wednesday: Record myself answering one TOEFL Speaking prompt on my phone. Listen to it and check if I spoke for 45 seconds clearly.
  • Friday: Meet my language exchange partner for coffee and try to speak only English for 20 minutes.
  • Sunday: Read a short news article aloud to practice my pronunciation and intonation.
