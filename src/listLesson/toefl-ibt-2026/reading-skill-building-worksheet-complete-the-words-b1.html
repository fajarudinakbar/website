How to Beat "Complete the Words"

In this task, the rule is strict: The second half of every second word is deleted. Here is your 3-step strategy to solve them:

1. Anchor on the First Sentence

The first sentence is always fully intact. Read it carefully. It provides the "schema" (the topic and context) that primes your brain for the vocabulary that will follow.

2. Grammar is your Guide

Look at the word before the blank. If you see "the", you need a noun. If you see "to", you likely need a base verb. Use grammar rules to limit the possibilities.

3. The Half-Word Math

If a word has 4 letters, 2 are kept, 2 are missing (e.g., ha-- for have). If a word has 5 letters, 2 are kept, 3 are missing (e.g., ba--- for basic). If a word has 6 letters, 3 are kept, 3 are missing.

Beware of the "Sound Trap"

English spelling doesn't always match its sound. A common mistake is writing the letters you hear instead of the correct spelling. Always double-check your spelling before moving on!
