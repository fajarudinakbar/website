TOEFL iBT Skill-Building Worksheet

Section: Writing | Task Type: Write for an Academic Discussion


Skill Focus: Joining an academic discussion clearly, politely, and with relevant reasons (B1)

1

Step 1: Understand the Task

Read the short discussion post from a professor and two students.

👨‍🏫

Professor Lee

Many universities want to help students stay healthy. Some schools plan to offer short daily exercise sessions between classes. What do you think? Should universities add these sessions to the schedule?

A
Student A

I think short exercise sessions are helpful. Students sit for many hours. A short break helps them focus better in the next class.

B
Student B

I disagree. Students already have very busy schedules. If the university adds sessions, the days could become too long.

Your task: Write a short response giving your opinion and supporting it with reasons. This task asks you to join an academic conversation, not write a full essay.

2

Step 2: Learn Useful Language

At B1 level, students need friendly formulas that sound natural in academic discussions.

Useful Starters:

  • I agree with… because…
  • I disagree with… because…
  • I think… since…
  • Another reason is that…
  • For example…

Useful Closings:

  • So, for this reason…
  • Therefore…
3

Step 3: Guided Practice

Complete the model response by choosing the best option.

I with Student A short exercise sessions help students stay focused. , many students feel sleepy after long classes, and a short activity can wake them up. I think these sessions are a good idea.
4

Step 4: Controlled Practice

Discussion Topic: Some teachers want to allow students to use digital dictionaries during class. Others worry it will slow learning.

Write a short response (B1 style): State your position + give one reason + add one simple example.

Sample Response:

"I agree with the teachers who want to use digital dictionaries because they are very fast. For example, if I see a new word in a reading passage, I can find the meaning in two seconds on my phone. Therefore, I think they help us learn more efficiently."

5

Step 5: Independent Practice (TOEFL-style B1 Task)

👨‍🏫

Professor Grant

Many universities are considering offering more evening classes to give students flexible schedules. Do you think evening classes are a good idea?

Student A:

Evening classes help students who work during the day.

Student B:

Evening classes are not helpful because students feel tired at night.

Your Turn: Join the discussion. Give your opinion and support it with explanations or examples. (Write 3–5 sentences).

Sample Response:

"I agree with Student A that evening classes are a great idea. Many students have jobs or families to take care of during the day. For example, my cousin works at a bank until 5 PM, so she can only study at night. So, for this reason, evening classes give more people a chance to get a degree."
