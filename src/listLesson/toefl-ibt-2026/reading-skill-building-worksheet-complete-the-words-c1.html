How to Process the Missing Halves

Do not try to solve the blank the second you see it. Use this cognitive process to figure out the exact word required:

1. Scan the Perimeter

When you hit a blank, keep reading to the end of the sentence. The logical answer is almost always hidden in the context that follows the blank, not before it.

2. Build a Semantic Map

What is the overall topic? If the intact sentence is about 'global warming', your brain should instantly prepare related vocabulary: climate, temperature, emissions, atmosphere.

3. Apply Half-Word Math

Only after you have deduced the probable meaning, check the math. A word with 8 letters keeps 4 and misses 4 (indu---- for industry). Ensure your logical guess fits the hyphens.

Beware of the "Sound Trap"

English spelling doesn't always match its sound. A common mistake is writing the letters you hear (e.g., spelling "process" as "prosses"). Double-check your spelling before submitting!
