Build a Sentence (B1 Level)

Topic: Academic & Professional Expression | Skill: Conventional Phrases

Welcome! In the Build a Sentence task, you must drag and drop scrambled chunks of text to form a coherent, grammatically correct sentence. At the B1 level, the secret to speed and accuracy is recognizing Conventional Phrases—words that naturally stick together (collocations and idioms). You have a maximum of 2 attempts per interactive exercise before correct answers are revealed. Let's start building!

3. The project succeeded. Everyone worked together.

2. The student asked a question which was difficult.

3. The project succeeded because everyone worked together.
