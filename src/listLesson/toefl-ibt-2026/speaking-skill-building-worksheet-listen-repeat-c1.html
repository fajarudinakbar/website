Welcome to C1 Level! In the scenario-based "Listen and Repeat" task, the goal is not to act like a parrot. If you try to memorize every single word, you will panic. Your strategy is to understand the core message and deliver the exact same idea using fluid, natural English. If you miss a specific word but seamlessly replace it with an accurate synonym, you succeed! Use the built-in Audio Player and Transcriber Tool below to master this high-level fluency skill.

Imagine a trainer at an orientation event tells you: "Get your name badge at the registration desk."

If you turn to a visitor and say: "You can pick up your name tag at the registration table," you have perfectly conveyed the instruction.

Fluency at the highest level requires capturing the Subject, Action, and Key Location/Detail. You are listening to a scenario playing out like a movie in your head, and then simply describing the movie out loud.

How to Process Sentences Instantly

When listening to an instruction or statement, immediately break it down into three conceptual chunks. If you remember the concepts, you can successfully repeat the sentence.

1. The "Who/What"

Identify the main subject immediately. Is the sentence about the visitors, the snacks, or the keynote speaker? Pin down the core entity.

2. The "Action"

What is happening? Are they picking up, testing, disposing, or reviewing something? Focus on the main verb.

3. The Location/Condition

Catch the nuance. Where or when is this happening? Listen for locations or conditions (e.g., "at the information desk," "in advance").

Paraphrase if Panicking!

If you hear: "Please see the information desk if you need an agenda." and you forget "agenda," just say: "Please go to the information desk if you need a schedule." You have successfully delivered the message!
