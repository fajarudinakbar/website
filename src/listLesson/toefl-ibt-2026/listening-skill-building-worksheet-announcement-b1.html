Listen to an Announcement (B1 Level)

Topic: Campus Life & Updates | Skill: Identifying Main Ideas & Details

Welcome! In the TOEFL iBT® Listening section, the Listen to an Announcement task tests your ability to understand short spoken messages (about 40 to 85 words) in a school setting. At the B1 (Intermediate) level, you must listen for straightforward facts: what is happening, where it is happening, and what you need to do.

  • This worksheet uses a Text to Speech (TTS) Simulator. Listen to the audio before reading the transcript!
  • You have a maximum of 2 attempts per exercise before the correct answers are revealed.

When you hear an announcement at a train station or an airport, you don't need to understand every single word. You just need to know the facts that affect you. You listen for the "W" words:

Who is this for? What is changing? When is it happening? Where do I need to go?

Campus announcements on the TOEFL are exactly the same. They are usually about a schedule change, a new rule, or a special event. If you answer the "W" questions in your head while listening, you will easily answer the test questions.

Metacognitive Question: Imagine your teacher says, "Tomorrow's class is moved to the library." What are the two most important words in that sentence to remember?

Types of Announcement Questions

After you listen to an announcement, you will usually answer 2 or 3 questions. Here is how to handle them:

1. The Main Purpose

"What is the announcement about?"
The speaker usually states the main reason in the very first sentence. Listen closely to the beginning!

2. The Specific Detail

"Where will the event take place?"
For details, take quick notes on numbers, days of the week, and locations while you listen.

3. The Next Action

"What should the students do next?"
Listen to the very end of the announcement. Speakers usually finish by giving an instruction (e.g., "Please arrive early").
