How to Beat "Complete the Words" at B2

In this task, the rule is strict: The second half of every second word is deleted. At the B2 level, your strategy must evolve:

1. Anchor on the First Sentence

The intact first sentence is your thematic anchor. If it discusses "Cognition," expect words like memory, retention, or brain to appear later in the text.

2. Leverage Word Families

Use syntactic clues to determine suffixes. If an adjective is needed after "is," fundam_____ becomes fundamental. If a noun follows an article, reten____ becomes retention.

3. The Half Word Math

Pay attention to the blanks! A word with 5 letters usually keeps 2 and misses 3 (hu___ for human). An 8 letter word keeps 4 and misses 4 (indu____ for industry).

Beware of the "Sound Trap"

English spelling doesn't always match its sound. A common mistake is writing the letters you hear (e.g., spelling "process" as "prosses"). Double check your spelling before moving on!
