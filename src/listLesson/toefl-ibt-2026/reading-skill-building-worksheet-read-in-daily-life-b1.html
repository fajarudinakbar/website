TOEFL iBT® Reading Section Read in Daily Life

TOEFL iBT Skill-Builder

Skill Focus: Scanning for Specific Details & Identifying Purpose (B1)

The "Read in Daily Life" task asks you to understand practical texts like emails, menus, or signs. You don't need to read every word perfectly. Instead, you need to scan (look quickly) for specific details.

Activity: Linear vs. Nonlinear Reading

Text A (Linear):
"The library will be closing early today at 3:00 PM because of the national holiday, so please return books early."

How you read: Left to right, word by word.

Text B (Sign):

LIBRARY NOTICE

Date: Oct 12

Closing Time: 3:00 PM

Reason: National Holiday

Action: Return books now!

How you read: Eyes jump to bold words.

Warm-up Question:

In Text B, which word tells you what you should do?

Strategy: Scanning for Conditions. Many daily texts have "conditions." A condition is a rule you must follow to get a result (e.g., "Until 10 AM only").

Bean & Leaf Café - Daily Specials

  • Morning Kick: Large Coffee + Muffin $5.00
    - Until 10 AM only!
  • Lunch Combo: Sandwich + Iced Tea $9.50
  • Student Deal: Show your university ID for 10% off any drink.
  • * Note: Oat milk substitution is an extra $0.50.

Model Question 1: How can a customer get a discount on a drink?

1. Scan for keywords related to money: "$", "off", "deal".

2. Find "Student Deal" and "10% off".

3. Read the condition: "Show your university ID".

Answer: By showing a university ID.

Model Question 2: If you order the Morning Kick at 10:30 AM, can you get the special price?

1. Scan for "Morning Kick".

2. Look for time limits. The text says "Until 10 AM only!".

Answer: No, the offer ends at 10 AM.

Read the email below. Then, answer the three multiple-choice questions.

To: All Residents

From: University Housing Office

Subject: Upcoming Maintenance Schedule

Dear Residents,

We will be conducting routine inspections of the fire alarm systems in Dormitory B next Tuesday, November 5, between 9:00 AM and 4:00 PM.

Maintenance staff will need access to every room during this time. You do not need to be present in your room while the inspection takes place. However, please ensure that all personal items are cleared away from the smoke detectors on the ceiling.

If you have a cat or dog (for registered emotional support animals), please secure them in a crate or take them with you to avoid them getting scared by the noise.

Thank you for your cooperation.

1. What is the main purpose of this email?

2. What are residents asked to do before the inspection?

3. Based on the text, which of the following is true about pets?

  • Scanning Speed: Were you able to find the specific details (like dates or prices) quickly without reading every word?
  • Vocabulary: Did you understand functional words like "inspection," "secure," "remit," or "valid"?
  • Inference: In Question 3 of the simulation, the text didn't explicitly say "The alarms will be loud," but it mentioned pets getting scared by the noise. Did you catch that connection?
