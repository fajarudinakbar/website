Topic: Daily Life & Personal Opinions | Skill: Spontaneous Speaking

Welcome! The Take an Interview task evaluates your ability to speak spontaneously. You will have a short online interview with a prerecorded researcher. You must answer questions immediately — no preparation time is provided. At the B1 (Intermediate) level, you should focus on answering clearly, giving simple reasons, and providing basic examples. Use the built-in Transcriber Tool below each question to practice speaking for exactly 45 seconds!

In real life, people ask us questions all the time without warning. If a classmate asks, "Why do you like studying in the library?" or a friend asks, "Do you prefer watching movies at home or in the theater?", you answer immediately.

The Take an Interview task simulates this natural conversation. You will hear a question, and you must start speaking right away. The goal at the B1 level is not to use perfect grammar or complicated words; the goal is to keep talking smoothly and clearly so the listener understands your opinion.

Structuring a Quick Answer

Because you have zero preparation time, you need a simple map in your mind. Use the P.R.E. method to speak easily for 45 seconds:

1. P = Point

Answer the question immediately in your first sentence.
"I definitely prefer studying at night." or "I agree that sports are important."

2. R = Reason

Give one simple reason why you feel that way.
"The main reason is that it is much quieter." or "This is because sports keep you healthy."

3. E = Example

Tell a short personal story. This helps you fill the 45 seconds easily.
"For example, last night I studied for two hours in my room..."

Buying Time (Lexical Fillers)

Don't stay silent! If you need 2 seconds to think, use these phrases: "That's an interesting question...", "Well, in my opinion...", or "Let me think about that for a second..."

Interviewer Audio

Interviewer: "Hello. Thanks for talking to me today. I want to ask you about your daily habits. Some people prefer to wake up early and study in the morning. Other people prefer to stay up late and study at night. Which do you prefer, and why?"

Example:

Point: I prefer to stay up late and study at night.
Reason: The main reason is that my house is very quiet at night, so I can focus better.
