The B2 Response Strategy

At this level, test makers use distractors that sound perfectly grammatical but are pragmatically incorrect. Apply these rules:

1. Spot the "Hidden" Question

"Do you happen to know..." or "I was wondering if..." are not yes/no questions. They are polite requests for detailed information or action. Don't just answer "Yes."

2. Address the Condition

If a speaker presents a problem with a condition (e.g., "I'd love to help, but I have a deadline"), the correct response must acknowledge the limitation ("No problem, I'll ask Sarah").

3. Track Idioms & Phrasal Verbs

Expect phrases like "look into it," "put it off," or "run it by me." Ensure your chosen response aligns with the idiomatic meaning, not the literal translation.
