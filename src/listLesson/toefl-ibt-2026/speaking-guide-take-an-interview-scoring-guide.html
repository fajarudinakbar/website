Task Guidelines

How This Oral Task is Evaluated

In the Take an Interview speaking task, you respond directly to an interviewer's vocal question. Raters grade your performance from 0 to 5 on three primary scales: Delivery (fluency, pace, rhythm, intonation), Language Use (accuracy, vocabulary range, grammatical variety), and Topic Development (relevance, depth of details, and elaboration).

Select score tiers from the official testing guidelines

SCORE 5

Fully Successful

The response fully addresses the question, and it is highly clear, comfortable, and fluent.

Elaboration

The response is on topic, complex, and well elaborated with illustrative examples.

Conversational Pace

Good conversational speaking pace is maintained with appropriate and natural use of pauses.

Pronunciation Rhythm

Pronunciation is easily intelligible. Rhythm and intonation patterns effectively convey precise meanings.

Grammar Range

A wide range of accurate grammar and vocabulary allows clear expression of precise, complex meanings.

SCORE 4

Generally Successful

The response addresses the question and is reasonably clear throughout.

Topic Elaboration

The response is on topic and elaborated, but it may lack effective sentence-level connectors.

Speaking Flow

Good speaking pace is generally maintained, with some pausing that may minimally affect overall flow.

Intelligibility

Meaning is not impeded by pronunciation, rhythm, or intonation, though occasional words/phrases require minor effort.

Grammar Control

Grammar and vocabulary are adequate to express general meanings most of the time without major confusion.

SCORE 3

Partially Successful

The response addresses the question but with limited elaboration and/or delivery clarity.

Limited Elaboration

The response is generally on topic, but elaboration may be relatively limited and repetitive.

Choppy Pace

Frequent or lengthy pauses result in a choppy, broken pace; filler words (um, uh, like) are frequent.

Intelligibility Issues

Intelligibility is sometimes affected by inaccuracies in word-level pronunciation or stress/rhythm control.

Precision Restrictions

Limited range and accuracy of grammar noticeably restrict the precision and clarity of speaking meanings.

SCORE 2

Mostly Unsuccessful

Reflects an attempt to address the question, but is not supported in a meaningful or intelligible way.

No Real Elaboration

Minimally connected to the question, with little or no relevant elaboration; consists mainly of language copied from the prompt.

Low Discernibility

Intelligibility is heavily limited; the speaker's intended meaning is often difficult to discern or follow.

Severe Language Limits

Shows a highly restricted, repetitive, and narrow range of grammar and vocabulary.

SCORE 1-0

Ineffective Speech

Reflects an ineffective attempt to respond. Low control over phonetic or syntax structures.

Score 1 Standard

Vaguely connected to prompt language. Mostly unintelligible response consisting mainly of isolated, disconnected words or brief phrases.

Score 0 Standard

No response, completely unintelligible response, non-English response, or consists only of phrases such as "I don't know."

"One activity I really enjoy doing in my free time is watching movies, especially dramas and documentaries." I like this because it helps me wind down after school.

"Although it is a simple activity, it allows me to manage my daily stress and makes my free time more enjoyable."

"One activity I genuinely enjoy in my free time is watching films, particularly documentaries and character-driven dramas." I enjoy this not only because it is relaxing, but also because it allows me to learn.

"For instance, I recently watched a documentary about environmental protection, which made me far more aware of how individual actions affect global ecosystems."

"Overall, watching films is highly meaningful to me because it seamlessly combines lighthearted entertainment with intellectual growth and deep personal reflection."
