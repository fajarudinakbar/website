How to Tackle B2 Daily Life Texts

At the B2 level, test-makers use synonyms and complex sentence structures to hide the answers. Use this 3-step strategy:

1. Look Past Exact Words

The correct answer rarely uses the exact words from the text. If the text says "students must submit documentation," the correct answer might say "proof is required."

2. Analyze the Conditions

Identify the cause and effect. "Failure to comply will result in immediate termination." Ask yourself: What happens if they don't follow the rule? What is the penalty?

3. Isolate the Exceptions

Test questions focus heavily on loopholes. Scan for words like subject to, exempt from, waived if, or with the exception of. This is where the trap lies.

Addendum B: Early Termination of Lease

Tenants wishing to terminate their housing contract prior to the end of the academic year must submit a formal written request to the Housing Authority at least 30 days in advance of their intended move-out date.

An early cancellation fee of $300 will be automatically applied to the student's account. This fee is non-negotiable, unless the student provides official, verifiable documentation demonstrating withdrawal from the university due to severe medical issues or mandatory military deployment.

Furthermore, failure to vacate the premises and return all issued keys by the approved termination date will result in additional daily pro-rated charges. Tenants remain liable for the full condition of the room until a final inspection is completed by a Resident Director.

Explanation: A student is exempt from the fee only if they provide official proof that they are withdrawing from the university due to a severe medical emergency or military deployment. Recognizing the word "unless" is key to finding this exception.

Office of Financial Aid: Academic Progress Policy

To maintain eligibility for institutional scholarships and federal grants, students must adhere to the Satisfactory Academic Progress (SAP) guidelines.

Requirements for Continued Funding
  • Maintain a minimum cumulative Grade Point Average (GPA) of 3.0.
  • Successfully complete at least 75% of all attempted credit hours per semester.
  • Ensure that total attempted credits do not exceed 150% of the credits required for the degree program.
Probation and Appeals

Students who fail to meet these criteria will be placed on Financial Aid Warning for one subsequent semester. During the warning period, students will continue to receive funding. If the SAP criteria are not met by the end of the warning period, all financial assistance will be suspended.

Appeal Process: A student whose aid has been suspended may submit a formal appeal if extenuating circumstances (such as severe illness or the death of an immediate family member) hindered their academic performance. Appeals must include supporting documentation and an academic recovery plan approved by a faculty advisor. Lack of awareness of the SAP policy is not a valid basis for an appeal.

Student Recreation Center: Guest Pass Policy

Currently enrolled students may sponsor up to two guests per day to use the Recreation Center facilities. The sponsoring student must remain in the facility with their guests at all times.

Fees and Registration: A daily guest pass costs $12 per person. Passes must be purchased at the main service desk using a debit or credit card; cash is strictly prohibited. Guests must be 18 years of age or older and present a valid, government-issued photo ID upon entry.

Liability Exemption: All guests are required to sign a digital liability waiver before utilizing the weight room or aquatic center. Guests merely observing an intramural sports game from the designated spectator seating area are exempt from the fee and waiver requirements.

Global Education Office: Pre-Departure Guidelines

Congratulations on your acceptance into the University Study Abroad Program! To ensure a seamless transition, please review the following mandatory pre-departure protocols.

Documentation & Visas: Students are solely responsible for securing their own passports and student visas. Passports must remain valid for at least six months beyond the scheduled conclusion of the program. The Global Education Office will provide the necessary enrollment verification letters, but we cannot expedite consular visa processes.

Health & Insurance: The university mandates that all participating students enroll in the comprehensive international travel health insurance plan provided by our partner agency, GeoGuard. Personal domestic health insurance plans will not be accepted as a substitute. Proof of GeoGuard enrollment must be uploaded to the student portal no later than 45 days prior to departure.

Orientation: Attendance at the Pre-Departure Safety Orientation is compulsory. This session covers emergency protocols, cultural integration, and academic expectations abroad. Failure to attend this session will result in the immediate revocation of your study abroad approval, and you will be liable for any non-refundable program deposits.
