Skill Focus: Stating an Opinion and Supporting with Personal Examples (B1)

1

Awareness & Warm-up (The 'Hook')

In this task, you must contribute to an online class discussion. You need to give your opinion clearly and support it with a reason or a personal example.

Activity: Compare the Responses

Topic: "Is it better to study in the morning or at night?"

A
Student A

"I think studying at night is better. I have more time then. Morning is too busy for me."

B
Student B

"In my opinion, studying at night is much better because the house is quiet. For example, last week I finished my history essay at midnight because my younger brothers were asleep and I could concentrate."

1. Which student provided a specific example from their own life?

2. Which student used transition words (like "because" and "for example")?

Guided Practice (The 'Show')

A simple way to get a good score (100+ words) is this structure:

Opinion + Reason + Personal Example

Topic: Should schools ban smartphones?

Opinion: I believe schools should ban smartphones... Reason: ...because they distract students from learning. Example: For instance, in my math class yesterday, two students were playing games on their phones and missed the teacher's explanation.

Controlled Practice (The 'Train')

Part A: Connecting Opinions to Reasons

Fill in the blanks with "because," "so," or "but."

1. I think uniforms are a good idea they save time in the morning.
2. Cars create a lot of pollution, I prefer to take the bus.
3. I want to learn a new language, I don't have enough free time right now.
4. Online shopping is convenient you can do it from home.
5. Some people like living in cities, I prefer the quiet countryside.

Part B: Adding Examples

Finish the sentence by adding a specific example relevant to the topic.

Topic: Healthy Eating

"It is hard to eat healthy food. For instance, ..."

Suggested: ...fast food is often cheaper than vegetables.

Topic: Learning English

"Watching movies helps us learn English. For example, I watched ..."

Suggested: ...Toy Story in English and learned new words for toys.

Topic: Team Sports

"Playing sports teaches us teamwork. Last year, my soccer team ..."

Suggested: ...learned to work together to win the championship.

Topic: Saving Money

"It is important to save money for emergencies. Once, I had to ..."

Suggested: ...pay for a car repair unexpectedly.

Topic: Traveling

"Traveling teaches you about new cultures. When I went to ..."

Suggested: ...Japan, I learned how to be polite in a different way.

4

Real Test Simulation (The 'Test')

👨‍🏫

Professor Dr. Miller

"This week, we are discussing how students are graded. Currently, most final grades are based on exams and tests. However, some people argue that grades should be based on homework and daily participation instead. What do you think? Should grades rely mostly on exams, or should they rely on daily work? Why?"

S
Sarah

"I think grades should be based on daily work. Some students get very nervous during big exams and forget everything. If we are graded on homework, it shows that we work hard every day, not just on one morning. It is a fairer way to measure our effort."

D
David

"I disagree with Sarah. I think exams are the best way to grade. Homework can sometimes be done by parents or copied from the internet. An exam proves what the student actually knows in their own head. It prepares us for stressful situations in the future."

Your Response:

Express your opinion, support it with a reason/example, and try to write at least 100 words.

Model Response:

"In my opinion, I agree with Sarah that grades should be based on daily work and participation. I think this is a much better way to see if a student understands the subject.

A big problem with exams is that they only happen one time. A student might be sick or very tired on that day, and then they get a bad score. This happened to me last year in math class. I knew the answers, but I had a headache during the final test, so I got a low grade.

Also, daily work helps students learn consistently. If we only have exams, students might only study the night before. Therefore, I believe daily participation is a fairer system."
