Part 1: Warm-Up Activity

Think about the following questions. There are no right or wrong answers. This is a chance for you to reflect on your own writing habits.

Part 2: What's in the Updated Writing Section?

There are three types of Writing tasks: the Build a Sentence Task, Write an Email Task, and Write for an Academic Discussion Task.

1. Build a Sentence

Test takers see several sentences with words or phrases in the wrong order and move them to form a grammatical sentence or question.

2. Write an Email

Test takers are presented with a scenario in text regarding either an academic or social setting and asked to share information for a specific purpose.

3. Academic Discussion

Write your own answer to a professor's question in an online discussion, taking into account other students' responses. (10 mins, min 100 words).

Check Your Understanding
