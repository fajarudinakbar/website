1.3 AI-Supported Vocabulary Check

AI Prompt:

“Explain TWO words: WORK SAMPLES and HOLISTIC. Use B1 English. Write ONE short sentence for each.”

Instructions: Listen for these words in the video context. Which is more helpful for job seekers?

Many of us do not end up working in a field related to our college major. Only a few graduates work in the jobs that match their degree. This shows that job experience and school subjects are not always connected. Jason Shen explains that traditional hiring systems focus too much on job history and not enough on a person’s real abilities.

Rather than judging candidates only by their resume, employers can look at what people can actually do. One way to show ability is with work samples or demonstrations that prove skills. Jason gives examples of people who succeeded by showing their real work instead of only talking about past jobs. This helps employers see talent that might be overlooked.

Shen also says that recruiters often miss great candidates because they rely on limited information. To make better decisions, employers should take a holistic view of a person. This means considering abilities, potential, and not only experience. Candidates, too, should learn to tell their story in a convincing way that shows what they can achieve. With these ideas, job seekers can improve their chances even without long experience.

5.1 Career Change

Scenario: Science grad wants marketing job. Difficulty? Ability to show? Improvement?

Difficulty: Degree doesn't match the job field.
Ability: Show a portfolio of social media campaigns or a personal blog.
Improvement: Proves they have the necessary skills despite lacking the specific degree.

5.2 No Experience Candidate

Scenario: Entry-level job. Show ability? Work samples? Explain process?

Show Ability: Create a mock project relevant to the company.
Work Samples: Demonstrates potential and practical skill.
Process: Explaining how they learn shows adaptability and problem-solving skills.

5.3 Employer Perspective

Scenario: HR Manager. Why experience not enough? Qualities to look for? Holistic benefit?

Not Enough: Resume doesn't show how a person works or thinks.
Qualities: Problem-solving ability, adaptability, unique skills.
Holistic: Helps find hidden talent that traditional screening misses.
