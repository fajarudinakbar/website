Task 2: Vocabulary in Context

Read the sentences taken from the text. Choose the correct meaning of each CAPITALIZED word.

Document A

Resident Registration (Juminhyo)

All foreign medical professionals moving to Japan must visit their local City Hall within 14 days of moving into their new PREMISES. This registration is MANDATORY.

You must bring your passport and Residence Card. Failure to COMPLY with this deadline may result in administrative fines.

Once registered, you will receive a Resident Record, which is required to open a bank account or sign a mobile phone contract.

Document B

Utility Contract Summary (Electricity & Water)

Welcome to Tokyo Power Grid. To ensure continuous service, please select a payment method upon moving in. We offer three primary options:

  • Convenience Store Payment: A paper invoice is sent to your mailbox monthly.
  • DIRECT DEBIT: Funds are automatically withdrawn from your Japanese bank account on the 25th of each month.
  • Credit Card: Online registration is required.

Please note: If your account falls into ARREARS for more than 60 days, we reserve the right to TERMINATE your service. For medical residents working night shifts, we highly recommend setting up automatic payments to avoid missed deadlines.

Task 9: Case Study – Decision Making

Scenario:

You are a resident surgeon. You have been on duty for 36 hours. You get home and find a final notice for your water bill in the mailbox. You are exhausted and need to sleep before your next shift in 8 hours.

  • Option A: Go to the convenience store immediately to pay so service isn't terminated.
  • Option B: Sleep now and try to set up an online payment later, even though the system is confusing.

Discuss which option is more responsible for a medical professional.

Analysis: Both options carry risks. Option A prevents utility termination but sacrifices crucial rest needed for patient safety. Option B prioritizes medical readiness but risks losing essential utilities. The professional choice is likely Option B (sleep), paired with delegating the task or resolving it immediately upon waking, highlighting the critical need to set up direct debit *before* such situations arise.
