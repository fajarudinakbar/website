The lecture discusses the development of suburban housing designs in the United States after the 1940s. It explains how Cape Cod houses were designed to be affordable and efficient for young families, with simple layouts and shared plumbing systems. The professor then describes ranch houses, which offered more privacy by placing living spaces at the back of the house. Finally, the lecture shows how cost-saving construction methods helped make these homes affordable.

Lecture Transcript

NARRATOR: Listen to part of a campus talk in the library. The speaker is an architect, and he has been discussing housing designs in residential communities.

PROFESSOR (MALE): In my last talk, we looked at housing designs in America in the 1940s. We looked at some photos and talked about some apartment buildings in Chicago from that time. Now, today we are going to discuss housing designs in suburban residential communities. In the late 1940s, a lot of young families were looking for less expensive houses outside of cities. Now, we have to first talk about a firm called Levitt & Sons, which designed homes and communities to meet this demand. Their residential community Levittown was a good example of this. Does anyone know about Levittown?

FEMALE STUDENT: I heard that Levittown was the first residential community of its kind. There were about 2,000 homes, and everyone lived in Cape Cod houses.

PROFESSOR: Yes, Cape Cod houses were very popular in the northeastern United States because their design was based on the needs of people. Okay, let’s move on to the features of the Cape Cod house. As I mentioned before, these homes were designed to meet the expectations of young suburban families, so the original floor plan was very simple. One thing about the floor plan was that the living room, the bedrooms, the bathroom, and the kitchen were all on the same floor. The bathroom was next to the kitchen so that they could share one plumbing system. This kept building costs down. Another feature of the Cape Cod house was that it could be expanded if the family needed more space. The house had an attic space that could be changed into another bedroom. Furthermore, all the houses were built economically because they were built in the same way and with the same material. Remember, young families who lived outside the city couldn’t afford expensive houses, so it was very important to keep construction costs down. As a result, the whole community looked the same except for the color of each house.

MALE STUDENT: Wouldn’t a community like that look a bit boring? I mean… all the houses looked identical.

PROFESSOR: You’re right. So, after a few years, the company designed a second version of the Cape Cod house. The roof was a bit different from the original version and the walls had a more modern look. The second version was called a ranch house. Now, I guess it’s a good time to talk about the ranch house because it was very efficient and popular with families at that time. When you look at the two types of houses, the Cape Cod, and the ranch, you’ll find that they are similar in most ways. But there’s an important difference too. The living room of the ranch house is in the back, while the Cape Cod house has a living room in the front of the house. The way the ranch house is set up actually gives the residents more private space. People felt more comfortable talking about work problems when others couldn’t overhear them. Children, instead of running in the street, could play in the backyards of their homes safely. And parents could stay at home and watch their children from inside the house. There was a clear separation between private space and public space. Oh, I have to mention that Levitt & Sons offered their ranch houses for sale at a low price. They had several ways to keep their construction costs down. One way was to use simple and costsaving building methods. Another way was to train each worker to do a specific task. You know, just like the assembly line where one worker is responsible for one particular job. This could actually make the construction move faster and keep the costs down at the same time. Thanks to these cost-saving methods, Levitt & Sons was able to offer the ranch house at a lower price. And their designs became a model for the suburban residential community throughout the country.
