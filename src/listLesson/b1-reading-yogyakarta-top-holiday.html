Yogyakarta Becomes Top Holiday Choice Over Bali

Yogyakarta, Indonesia – This holiday season, many Indonesian families and travelers are choosing Yogyakarta instead of Bali. Social media posts and travel reports show that Jogja is now one of the busiest places in the country, while Bali is quieter than usual.

For many years, Bali was the most popular destination for both local and international tourists. Its beaches, resorts, and nightlife made it famous around the world. But in December 2025, things look different. Videos on TikTok and Instagram show crowded streets in Yogyakarta, especially Malioboro, while Bali’s tourist areas are less busy.

Why People Choose Jogja

Travel experts say there are several reasons why people prefer Jogja this year. First, the cost of travel is lower. Hotels, food, and transport in Jogja are cheaper than in Bali. Families find it easier to plan a holiday in Jogja without spending too much money.

Second, Jogja offers strong cultural and educational experiences. Tourists can visit Borobudur and Prambanan temples, explore traditional markets, and enjoy local food. Many parents say Jogja is a good place for children to learn about history and culture while having fun.

Third, the city has a friendly atmosphere. Street vendors, artists, and performers make Malioboro lively. Visitors enjoy walking along the street, shopping for batik, and tasting local snacks. This feeling of tradition and community attracts more domestic tourists.

Social Media Buzz

On social media, people are talking about the difference between Jogja and Bali. Some posts say Bali feels “too quiet” this holiday season. Others show videos of Jogja full of visitors, with captions like “Jogja is the real holiday crowd.”

Travel bloggers also write that Jogja is “lebih ramah di kantong” (more friendly to the wallet). They compare prices in Jogja and Bali and find Jogja much cheaper. This message spreads quickly online and influences more people to visit Jogja.

Impact on Tourism

The change in holiday preference is important for Indonesia’s tourism industry. Local businesses in Jogja are enjoying higher income, while some tourism workers in Bali are surprised by fewer visitors.

Looking Ahead

Experts believe this trend may continue. With affordable prices, strong culture, and government support, Jogja may remain a favorite destination for Indonesian families.
