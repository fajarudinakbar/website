Task 9: Case Study Decision Making

Scenario:

You are at the pharmacy. Your digital wallet app crashes exactly when you are supposed to SCAN the code. There is a long line of people behind you.

  • Option A: Apologize, cancel the transaction, and leave the store to fix your phone.
  • Option B: Quickly look for cash in your bag, even if it takes an extra minute, to finish the purchase.

Discuss which option is the more professional choice in this public setting.

Analysis: Option B is generally more practical and standard if you actually have the cash, as canceling an order creates administrative work for the staff. However, if finding the cash takes too long, Option A shows respect for the time of the other waiting customers. Communicating clearly is the most professional part of either choice.
