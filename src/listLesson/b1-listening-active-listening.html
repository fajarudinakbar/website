Strategy: Catching Keywords (Dictation)

The "Keyword" Rule: In a listening dictation task, focus on stressed content words like nouns and verbs. These words carry the main meaning of the sentence and are often missing in the blanks.

Audio Example
"Active takes ."

Think-Aloud Logic:

  • listening: This is the core noun of the sentence and receives clear stress in the audio.
  • effort: This is the object noun explaining what active listening requires. It is heavily stressed at the end of the sentence.

Part 4 Academic Talk (Psychology)

Lecture

"Active listening is not just hearing words. It requires you to be fully engaged with the speaker. You should show the speaker you are paying attention by nodding your head or making eye contact. You can also ask questions to clarify points you missed. This active approach prevents misunderstandings and builds trust between people."

Question 1: What is a physical sign of active listening?

Question 2: What is one benefit of active listening mentioned in the lecture?

Answer Key & Explanations
Part 1:

Correct: A

Reasoning: Directly and naturally answers the request to repeat the deadline information.

Part 2:

Correct Idea: Write key words instead of full sentences.

Reasoning: The professor advises the student to "just write key words and listen to my main ideas."

Part 3:

Correct: C They must sign up at the front desk today.

Reasoning: The announcement clearly states "please sign up at the front desk today" to join.

Part 4:

Q1 Correct Idea: Nodding or making eye contact.

Q2 Correct Idea: It prevents misunderstandings or it builds trust.
