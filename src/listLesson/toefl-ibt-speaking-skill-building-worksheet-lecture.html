TOEFL iBT Speaking Skill-Building Worksheet

Task Type: Integrated Speaking (Task 4)

Skill Focus: Summarizing an academic lecture

Step 1: Understand Speaking Task 4

In Speaking Task 4, you will:

  • Listen to an academic lecture.
  • Take notes on the main idea and key points.
  • Summarize the lecture in your own words.

Your goal is to explain the main concept and the supporting details or examples used by the lecturer.

Note: You do not give personal opinions.

Step 2: Lecture Content

Topic: Social Loafing

Lecture Audio

Lecture Transcript

The professor explains a concept called social loafing. Social loafing happens when people put in less effort while working in a group than when working alone. The professor explains that this happens because individual contributions are harder to notice in group situations.

The professor then gives two reasons why social loafing occurs. First, when people believe their effort will not be recognized, they feel less motivated to work hard. Second, group members may assume that others will take responsibility, so they reduce their own effort.

To illustrate this concept, the professor describes a study in which students were asked to work on a group project. When students worked individually, most of them completed the task carefully. However, when the same task was assigned as a group project, some students contributed much less. According to the professor, this study clearly demonstrates social loafing in academic settings.

Step 6: Build the Speaking Structure

A strong Task 4 response usually includes:

  1. Introduce the topic.
  2. Explain the main concept.
  3. Describe supporting points.
  4. Explain the example.

Useful Language (B2):

  • "The professor discusses..."
  • "He explains that..."
  • "One reason is that..." / "Another point is..."
  • "The professor illustrates this by..."
