When a customer places an order on our platform, a complex chain of events begins. The system must pass two major security checks before the package leaves the warehouse.

Phase 1: Inventory and Allocation

First, the order enters the system. The database immediately checks the stock levels. If the item is out of stock, the system automatically triggers a "Restock Request" to the supplier and sends an apology email to the customer. The order is then cancelled. However, if the item is available, the system allocates the product to the customer so no one else can buy it. The status changes to "Reserved."

Phase 2: Payment Verification

Next, the customer must pay. The system connects to the bank gateway. If the funds are insufficient or the bank details are wrong, the payment fails. The system does not cancel the order immediately. Instead, it enters a "Payment Pending" state for 24 hours. If the customer does not retry successfully within this timeout period, the order is cancelled, and the item is returned to inventory. If the payment is successful, the system issues a digital invoice and sends the data to the warehouse.

Phase 3: Fulfillment and Dispatch

Once the warehouse receives the data, staff pick the item from the shelf. Before packing, the item goes through Quality Control (QC). If the item is damaged, it is discarded, and the process restarts at Phase 1. If the item passes QC, it is packed, labeled, and finally dispatched to the logistics courier for delivery.
